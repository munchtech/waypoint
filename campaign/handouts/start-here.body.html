What's It About?

Start here: the short pitch, session zero and your character. A player handout, no spoilers.

  • Setting: Corvane, in the Forgotten Realms
  • Rules: D&D 2024
  • Party: four characters, starting at level 3

The short version

Four hundred and twelve years ago, every bell in Corvane fell silent in a single night. The dawn went grey. Winter froze into glass. The dead stopped resting. People learned to live without bells, and they paid for their forgetting in memories.

Thirty years ago, ships from the Sword Coast found a way through the storm that had hidden Corvane for centuries. They land at Pellmoor Landing, a busy port on a drowned lake.

Now something bronze is humming under that lake, and a fourteen-year-old girl can hear it. You are the people who find out why.

What you'll be doing

You'll be looking for the bells' Hearts: the bronze clappers that make each bell ring. Some are hidden. Some are guarded. Some are in the hands of people who want very different things from them.

Ringing a bell restores something that was lost. It also costs the person who rings it something of their own: the Toll. The DM always warns you before you ring: what kind of thing the bell will take, and how heavy it will be. Never exactly what. You can always walk away.

The question at the heart of the game: what do you want to ring, and what will it cost you? There is no single right answer. The story goes where your choices take it.

Session zero

Before the first game we'll agree what the game should include and what it should leave out. Everyone gets a say, and you can answer in private.

WordWhat it meansExample
Off-limitsNever in the game, not even mentioned."Spiders are off-limits."
Off-screenCan happen in the story, but we skip past it without describing it."Torture stays off-screen."

Anyone can say "off-limits" or "off-screen" at any time, even mid-scene, and we change course without asking why. The campaign touches on drowning, harm to children, memory loss and family separation; say so if any of those should be off-limits or off-screen.

Your character

Any class, species and background from the 2024 Player's Handbook works. You start at level 3 with your subclass chosen, as one of four characters. You can be a newcomer who just arrived by ship, a Faerûnian who settled in Pellmoor years ago, or someone born in Corvane (The Ringing Land has more on each).

Some ideas, if you want a starting point:

  • Bell-touched. You can hear one silent bell, and you can't stop hearing it.
  • Already paid. You've sold or given away a memory, and you know something is missing.
  • Ferry-hand or pilot. You know the Mere and the names it has taken.
  • Court clerk or Dawn knight. Orderly, sworn, and starting to have doubts.
  • Bellwright's apprentice. You learned a hunted craft.
  • Sailor or trader from the Sword Coast. Your ship sailed without you, or you came to buy memories.

Bring to session zero

  • A level 3 character, with your subclass chosen.
  • One reason your character is in Pellmoor.
  • Anything you want off-limits or off-screen (you can tell the DM privately).
  • Someone your character would pay to remember, or would forget on purpose.

Questions to think about. You don't have to share your answers.

  • What would your character give up to remember someone they lost?
  • Is there a person your character would forget on purpose?
  • What do you owe a stranger who is suffering?
  • When would your character break an oath?
  • Would your character ring a bell that might end the world, if it might also save one person?
