**Table jobs (optional).** Offer each player one small job. Nobody has to take one, and a job is never a reason to miss their character's moment. Swap them round whenever someone asks.
- **Chronicler:** writes one line per scene: who we met, and what we promised. At the start of next session they read the lines back as "Last time…".
- **Mapper:** sketches Pellmoor as the party learns it: the Old Shore, the Quays, the Mere. The painted map on the player site fills in the rest.
- **Keeper of what was lost:** keeps the party's own list of memories given, sold or stolen, and every Toll paid, so the players remember what their characters gave up. Your Memory ledger in Waypoint stays the record.
- **Names keeper:** writes down every name you say out loud. It saves you inventing the same ferry-woman twice.
- **Conditions keeper:** in a fight, reminds the table who is Grappled, Frightened or concentrating. Foundry still runs the turns.
