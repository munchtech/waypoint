## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. A frozen bell at the top of two hundred feet of ice, a pack asleep at the bottom, and a price. It's the big night of Act III: the climb is the action, the ringing is the choice.*

### Before you start (Session 14)

**Level:** four characters at level 6. If anyone hasn't levelled up yet, do it first.

**Check the world state first.** Five facts change tonight:
- **Who carries the Frost Heart?** Ask now, so nobody argues about it at the top.
- **Is Imber with them?** He watches from the foot of the Spire, in somebody's cloak. If he's in the glass, he'll hear it from the chapel. If he's dead, finish his sermon for him, if they promised to.
- **How many bells have rung?** That sets the weight of the Toll: none or one is **heavy**, two or three is **grievous**, four or five is **ruinous**. Look up what that means for a memory in Part 2.7 before you start.
- **Did the wolves ever see them in Session 11?** If not, they can have surprise.
- **Exhaustion.** Write down each character's level. It's −2 for each level on every climbing check.

**What this session is about.** Hieme is the bell of winter. It stopped in the middle of a note, and everything in the forest stopped with it. If someone carries the Heart up and rings it, the winter ends, and the bell takes a memory from them, and they don't get to choose which. Nobody has to.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Make the climb feel high. Describe what they can see at sixty feet, a hundred and twenty, and the top.
- Give three honest ways up, and let any of them work: fight, sneak, or the old stair.
- **Before the ringing, say the warning in full and wait for a yes.** If it's a no, that's a real ending for the night, and you don't argue.
- Read the ringing slowly. Then stop talking for a few seconds.
- End on the question: **"What did the Frost Spire cost the thaw?"**

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; seven Glass-Wolves; six Stilled; Yara; Imber.
- **Maps:** draw the Spire from the side, as a tall triangle with a line every twenty feet and the stair on the north face. Mark where the six Stilled stand on it. It's all you need.
- **The memory.** Think about what you'd take from each character who might ring: one clear thing they've talked about at the table. Keep it kind, and make it matter. You say it after the thaw begins. Don't take the thing a player has asked you not to touch.
- **Props:** a wine glass and a wet finger. One note, quietly, when they ring it.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **The climb:** three Strength (Athletics) checks, DC 14. A failure is a slip: fall 20 feet, 2d6 bludgeoning, and try that same check again. Someone roped to the climber can Help, for advantage.
- **The old Sexton's stair** is on the north face. If Yara is Friendly she shows them where it starts, and it takes away one check. Anyone who's stuck can use all of it: an extra hour of cold, no checks.
- **Exhaustion:** each level is −2 to every D20 Test and 5 feet off your Speed.
- **Extreme Cold:** each hour, Constitution DC 10 or one level of Exhaustion. A warm cloak succeeds.
- **Falling:** 1d6 for every 10 feet, and you land Prone. Feather Fall stops it.
- **Magic works here.** Fly and Spider Climb skip the checks. Misty Step is 30 feet. Levitate needs a friend with a rope. But every spell hums, and the pack hears it.
- **Silence** hides the sound only from those inside it. It doesn't stop the bell from ringing.
- **Glass-Wolves** burst when they drop: Dexterity DC 12 or 1d6, everyone within 5 feet. Vulnerable to Thunder.

**Places at a glance:**
- **The foot:** broken columns of ice, seven shapes lying among them. Good cover.
- **The lower stair (0 to 60 feet):** three Stilled stand on it. Wolves can reach this far.
- **The high stair (60 to 140 feet):** three more. Wolves can't.
- **The open face (140 to 200 feet):** wind should be here. There isn't any.
- **The crown:** a room with no walls, a frozen bell, and the whole forest below it.

**Rumours:** none. They're here.

### Opening recap (Session 14)

> **SAY:** "Last time, a man who broke the world told you he was sorry, and gave you the bell's Heart. *(Say what they did about him.)* He asked you not to ring the Spire without it. You've carried it since yesterday, and it's so cold that nobody has been able to hold it for long. Look up. That's the Spire."

- **Then hand it over:** "Who's carrying the Heart today, and what have you wrapped it in?"
- **If Isolde's lot is close:** Yara points south-east. Smoke, one thin line of it. "That's a day off. They don't light fires unless they want you to see."

### Scene 1: The foot of the Spire

**Where and why.** The middle of the glass forest. They see the whole climb before they start.

> **SAY:** "A spire of ice rises from the centre of the glass forest like a frozen fountain. Inside it the bell hangs motionless, locked in frost. Every breath you take freezes on the air. The bell is singing, so softly you hear it only in your teeth."

**What's really going on.** Seven Glass-Wolves are lying in the ice at the foot. They look like ice until they don't. Up the stair stand six people who were climbing to ask the bell to stop.

**Yara.** *Lines:* "Two ways up. There's the one that kills you and the one that takes longer." · "I got to that fellow there. Red ribbon. I turned round." · "I'll rope the first pair. I won't go up. Somebody has to know where you were." · If she's Friendly: "North face. There's a stair under the frost. He had it cut." (She nods at Imber.)

**Imber,** if he's here. He sits on a pack and looks up. *Lines:* "I was younger than any of you when I climbed it." · "You needn't ring it for me." · "It will ask you for something. It asked me, once, and I'd already given it."

**If they…**
- **Look at the base properly:** Perception DC 15, or ask Yara. Seven long shapes, and one of them is breathing.
- **[Sneak past:](play:s14-sneak-guards)** group Dexterity (Stealth) DC 14. Yara's map gives advantage; Pass without Trace gives +10. Read:

> **SAY:** "You pass so close to the nearest wolf that you can see the bubble of air frozen in its eye."

- **If one character fails,** one wolf follows them, alone. The rest sleep.
- **[Distract them:](play:s14-sneak-guards)** a thrown glass-flower, a whistle, a Minor Illusion of a running deer. The pack goes after it for two rounds.
- **[Fight them:](play:s14-fight-guards)** see the fights.
- **Use the cold:** nobody has told the wolves that fire is a different thing. A bonfire lit well away brings them all to the wrong place.

**Bring it back.** The bell hums, once, a half-note higher, and every wolf's ear turns towards it.

**How it ends.** They're on the ice, with somebody's foot on the first step.

### Scene 2: The climb

**Where and why.** Two hundred feet of ice. Three checks. Each one is a place they stop.

**What's really going on.** [It's hard, and it isn't a puzzle.](play:s14-climb) The ice stair is broken in two places and stops short of the top; each gap is one of the three checks. They go as a rope team, or one by one. Ask each time: who's roped to whom, and who's Helping? Say the fall before they risk it.

> **SAY (the first third):** "At sixty feet you pass the first of them: a man with his hand on the rail, looking up. Somebody has tied a red ribbon round his wrist. The wood has vanished under you, and from here it looks like a lake."

- **The Stilled on the stair:** they don't move unless glass breaks nearby, or someone passes within 5 feet. If one wakes, it lashes out at whatever's nearest. A hit means a Strength save, DC 12, or the climber falls 20 feet. Say so before they squeeze past one.
- **Talk to it:** an action, Influence DC 15. Read its ribbon aloud for advantage. A calmed one sits down on the step, holds the rail, and doesn't let go of the next climber.

> **SAY (the second third):** "At a hundred and twenty feet the stair stops. Above it there's only the face of the ice, and the bell, humming in your back teeth. You can see Yara's fire. You can see a smudge of smoke to the south. You can see all of it, the whole forest, like something under a glass dome on a shelf."

> **SAY (the top):** "The last ten feet are a lip of ice as wide as a bed. You pull yourself over it, and there's the bell. It's as tall as a person, bronze under a skin of frost, and it's not swinging. There's a hook inside it where a clapper ought to be."

**If they…**
- **[Fly or use magic:](play:s14-magic-climb)** it works. Read the box and let them have it. The wolves run for the base and the Stilled turn their heads.
- **Fall:** 2d6, Prone, 20 feet down. Roped to someone? They're caught at the end of the rope: no damage, but the other climber makes a Strength save, DC 12, or is pulled off with them.
- **Keep slipping:** somebody finds the stair on the north face. An hour of cold. They arrive.
- **Stop to draw it:** at the top, or on the way. One minute and no roll. Yara will want it ([Session 15](play:s15-yara-map)).
- **Get the Heart out to look:** it's singing. The bell answers it.
- **Leave someone below:** they'll be the one to see the smoke move, and they'll hear it first.

**Bring it back.** The frost on the nearest pane has a face-sized clear spot in it. Somebody's breath has been on the other side. Four hundred years of it.

**How it ends.** At least one of them is standing under the bell with the Heart.

### Scene 3: The bell

**Where and why.** The crown, the bell, and the person who'll ring it. There is no hurry in this scene.

> **SAY:** "Nobody's put a hand on it since the night it stopped. The rope's still there, stiff as a rod. The hook is empty. The Heart is humming in your hands, and you can feel it leaning."

**What's really going on.** The one with the Heart can ring. They can't do it without the Heart, and they can't do it without agreeing to pay.

**Say it out loud, to the player who'll do it, and then wait.**

> **SAY:** "Hieme will take a memory from you, and it will be *(heavy / grievous / ruinous)*. I'll choose which memory after the winter breaks. You won't get it back, and no spell will. Do you ring it?"

**If they…**
- **Ask what the weight means:** tell them straight, from Part 2.7. *Heavy:* one defining memory, gone for good. You rewrite what it held up. *Grievous:* one person who matters to you, gone from memory. You know of them. You don't know them. *Ruinous:* a whole year of your life, gone, with everyone you met in it.
- **Ask which memory:** "I don't know yet. I'll tell you after." That's the bell's rule, not yours.
- **Say no:** [the bell doesn't ring.](play:s14-refuse-to-ring) "The bell keeps its one soft note in your teeth, patient as snow." Nothing is taken. Somebody else can ring. Nobody at all is a real choice; record it and don't shame it. Go to the end of the session.
- **Say yes:** [they ring it.](play:s14-ring-hieme) Make them describe the swing. Go to Scene 4.
- **Hesitate:** give them all the time they need. Ask the others one question each, quietly: what would you tell them? Then look back.
- **[Strike it without the Heart:](play:s14-strike-without-tongue)** stop them first. Yara's voice from below, or Imber's: "It has no Heart! It'll freeze!" If they swing anyway, the frost surges. Constitution DC 15 every minute for up to ten minutes (cloaks still succeed), the wolves wake, and climbing down ends it. No Toll, because nothing rang.
- **Try to have the whole party pay it:** one Ringer. It doesn't split.
- **Try to have Imber do it:** he can't climb it. He'd ask.

**Bring it back.** The Heart pulls, very gently, towards the hook.

**How it ends.** It rings, or it doesn't.

### Scene 4: The thaw

**Where and why.** The top, and then the whole forest. Read it slowly. Then be quiet for a moment.

> **SAY:** "The bell rings once. The sound is high and thin and clear, like a single thread of ice pulled across the sky. The frost on the Spire cracks. The glass trees hum. Everywhere in the forest, something begins to move. The winter is going. Not all at once, but slowly, like a tide turning back."

**What's really going on.** The Toll lands after this. Let them have the thaw first.

**Then the Toll.** Ask the Ringer to close their eyes, if they like. Then tell them, plainly and kindly, what's gone. Say it as something missing, not as something stolen.

> **SAY (an example, for heavy):** "You're thinking about *(say who)*, because you often do when something is beautiful. And there's a gap. You know you had a mother. You know she had a kitchen. You can't see it."

- Ask them to write down the belief, bond or fear that went with it, and what they'll say instead.
- **If they want a private moment:** stop the game for a few minutes. It's a hard thing.

**If they…**
- **Look down:** the Stilled on the stair are moving, very slowly. They stop fighting the moment it rings.
- **Look for Imber:** he's weeping. He isn't hiding it. "It's the right note," he says. "It's the right note. I never heard it, you know, not properly. I was standing too close."
- **[Leave the Heart in the bell:](play:s14-leave-tongue)** it hangs where it belongs and it's still ringing, very faintly. Safe. The ringing can't be undone. But Isolde will have to climb for it.
- **Take it down with them:** lighter than it was. Warm, almost.
- **Draw it, now:** the thaw changes the shape of everything. Yara hasn't seen this before either.

**Yara,** at the bottom, when they come down: she's looking at a drop of water on her glove. *Lines:* "That's wet." · "Nobody's going to buy ice from me." · "...Good."

**Bring it back.** Water. One drop, then another, then so many drops it sounds like rain, and it's been so long that nobody can remember the word.

**How it ends.** They sleep by the fire, with the forest dripping around them. Ask: **"What did the Frost Spire cost the thaw?"** Start with the Ringer. Let them answer in their own words, or not at all.

### Running the fights (Session 14)

#### The Spire guards

*Seven Glass-Wolves at the base and six Stilled on the stair. Half the Stilled are asleep until the first wolf shatters.*

- **Setup:** the wolves lie in a ring at the foot. The Stilled stand on the stair at 20, 40, 60, 80, 100 and 120 feet. The characters may be anywhere on the face.
- **What the wolves do:** they drag people off the first rope: bite, knock Prone, and pull. They can't reach past sixty feet. When three have shattered, the rest break off and go, fast.
- **What the Stilled do:** nothing, until glass breaks near them or someone passes within 5 feet. Then they hit whoever is closest (two claws) and try to shove them off: a hit means a Strength save, DC 12, or the climber falls 20 feet, 2d6.
- **The rhythm:** every wolf that bursts cracks the stair. The first one wakes three Stilled. Say the sound, then look up: "One of them has turned its head."
- **Show the way out early.** In round 1, a Stilled on the stair looks at a climber and you describe the fear. Talking works, as an action, at DC 15.
- **If it drags:** in round 4 the bell hums louder and the Stilled stop to listen for one round.
- **When it ends:** when three wolves have shattered, when everyone's above sixty feet, or the instant the bell rings. The Stilled stop at once.
- **Scale on the fly:** level 5, leave out two Stilled and one wolf. Three characters, five wolves and three Stilled. The climb and the falls will do the damage: add nobody.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** someone who drops on the stair is caught by the rope. Someone who drops at the base is dragged a little way off and left, and Yara has a bandage.

### Pacing (Session 14)

| Time | What's happening |

| 0:15 to 1:10 | Scene 1: the foot of the Spire (fight, or the sneaking) |
| 1:10 to 2:15 | Scene 2: the climb |
| 2:15 to 2:50 | Scene 3: the bell |
| 2:50 to 3:30 | Scene 4: the thaw, the Toll, the question |

- **Where to break:** at the top of the stair, before anyone steps under the bell.
- **Running long:** let the stair cover all three checks, and skip the wolves once they're over sixty feet.
- **Running short:** start Session 15's first scene at sunrise.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop with somebody's hand on the rope. Ask "Do you ring it?" and don't take an answer.

### If you get stuck (Session 14)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **A wolf gets up and stretches,** without waking. Then it lies down again.
2. **The bell hums and the Heart hums back.** Whoever's nearest feels it in their teeth.
3. **Yara points at the smoke.** It's closer.

**If nobody will pay:** it's all right. Ask, once: "Has everybody who wants to say something said it?" Then go to camp. Hieme can be rung another day. Isolde will still come for the Heart.

**If someone argues about the weight:** show them Part 2.7. It isn't a punishment, and it isn't a vote.

### End of session 14

**The closing question:** "What did the Frost Spire cost the thaw?"

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the climb, the guards, the ringing (or the refusal), and where the Heart ended up.
- **World-state facts to check:** Hieme is rung; the Heart is with the party or in the bell; Stilled trust, if any of them were calmed or hurt.
- **The Toll:** in the Memory ledger as a Toll, with the weight and exactly what you said. The player's new belief, bond or fear goes next to it.
- **A sketch:** who drew it, or that nobody did. Yara will ask.

**Rewards:**
- **Hieme:** one more of the six for the Concord.
- **Glassreach:** beginning to thaw. The Stilled will wake over the coming weeks.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to the Ringer, and to whoever stayed below and kept the rope.
