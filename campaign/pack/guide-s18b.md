## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. A staircase that charges by the step, a voice that sounds like you talking in your sleep, and a man coming up out of the dark with a hammer. There's hardly any rolling and no fight you have to run. Count the steps out loud and mean it.*

### Before you start (Session 18B)

**Level:** four characters at level 7.

**Check the world state first.** Five facts change tonight:
- **The Cult:** ceased or feeding? It decides who's sitting on the top step.
- **Corin:** unmet? He makes his offer **once in the campaign.** If they've met him, somebody else is on the stairs: see Scene 4.
- **Anyone's stairs save already made?** It's once per character, ever.
- **Dreams:** who's lost theirs? The Hush speaks to them differently.
- **Mirel's Heart, and Mirel:** it's what Corin wants to talk about.

**What this session is about.** The thing that's holding the world together is tired, and it would like them to be quiet. It's the first person in the campaign to ask for nothing but rest, and they can't give it any.

**Your goals tonight:**
- **State the price of the stairs before anyone takes a step.** Then count.
- Let each player hear the Hush in their own voice, and answer it.
- Have Corin's whole offer, if they haven't had it: his plan, and the part that frightens him.
- End on the question: **"What did the Hush ask of you?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Corin; two Heart-Hounds (only if they insist).
- **Maps:** a spiral stair. A drawing of one's enough: mark step 20, 40, 60, 80, 100.
- **Props:** a heap of counters, twenty per player, to push across the table as they walk. *Edda Thorne's Notes*, if Corin's going to give them.
- **Sit close together tonight,** and talk low.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **The price:** the first nineteen steps are free. **Every full twenty steps costs one memory.**
- **The save:** the first time a character reaches step 20, they make a Wisdom save, DC 13, once in the whole campaign. **Fail: they'll never pay more than five, in all. Succeed: never more than two.** Going up is free.
- **Write each memory in the ledger as Stairs.** It counts toward the same limit in Session 25.
- **The player chooses the memory,** and says it aloud. It's gone from the character, not the player.
- **Asking the Hush:** one true memory of somebody loved, one true answer. It's a Trade, and it never takes anything that isn't offered.
- **Ysh can't be rung without the Key.** Say so, if they ask.
- **Frightened,** in a stairwell: they can't go any further down while it lasts.

| **Pip Furrow** | Six | No stats | Wherever he shouldn't be | Delighted: he was right | Just listen | He's counted the steps by the sound. There are a hundred. |

**Places at a glance:**
- **The arch and the top step:** worn down in the middle like a spoon.
- **The stair:** a hundred steps, round and round, warm, with a faint wind that goes in, and out.
- **The bottom:** a bell the size of a house, hanging in the dark, blank as a sleeping face.

**Rumours (d6),** whispered at the top:
1. "Count out loud. It's when you stop counting that you lose things." (Wise.)
2. "It talks in your own voice. Don't argue. You'll lose." (Half true.)
3. "Nobody's ever been charged on the way *up*." (True.)
4. "If you've nothing to lose it's free." (False. Everyone's got something.)
5. "The bell at the bottom's facing the other way." (Nobody knows what that means.)
6. "There's a man down there sings back to it." (True: Corin.)

### Opening recap (Session 18B)

> **SAY:** "*(You spent a night by the Cult's fire, and it was wonderful, and something's hanging on a nail. / You talked nine people out of the only thing they believed in. / You left the Cult humming.)* Behind the fire there's a stone arch, and behind the arch there's a stair. You heard something on it this morning: one small, clear, metal tap."

- **Then hand it over:** "What's a memory your character would never, ever give up? Say it out loud, so we all know." One answer each. **Write these down. Never ask for them.**

### Scene 1: The top step

**Where and why.** The arch. Short. It's the gate, and the warning.

> **SAY:** "Six robed figures sit around the top step. They look up, smiling, and one pats the stone beside her."

**What's really going on.** [How they're met](play:s18b-pass-the-cult) depends on yesterday:
- **The Cult's ceased:** they stand up and make room. Lumen tells them the price herself.
- **Feeding:** they plead, and then they sit down across the stair. Influence (Persuasion) DC 14, or a group Stealth DC 14 when the humming starts.
- **Fought:** nobody's here. There's a kettle.
- **Mirel's rung:** they're crying, quietly, and they hold up a lamp.

**Say the price, in full, before anyone moves:**

> **SAY (Lumen, or the writing on the arch):** "The first nineteen are free. It's every twenty after that. A memory. You'll choose it yourself. It'll be one save, when you get to twenty, and after that it knows how much it can ask you for. Nobody's ever paid more than five."

**If they…**
- **Ask Lumen what she paid:** "Five. I know they were good ones." She touches the side of her own face, as if checking that it's there. "I had a mother. I must have had."
- **Want to leave somebody at the top:** sensible. Somebody who keeps count. Pip volunteers.
- **Ask Pip:** "A hundred exactly. I did it by listening. And there's a *man*."

**Bring it back.** A breath of warm air comes up the stair, waits, and goes down again.

**How it ends.** Somebody puts a foot on the first step.

### Scene 2: Nineteen steps

**Where and why.** The top of the stair. Free, and frightening. Count with the counters.

> **SAY:** "The stairs spiral down into the hill, each step worn smooth by feet that came to listen. Your footsteps are muffled, though the stone is bare. Under the lullaby, very faintly, something breathes."

**What's really going on.** [The first nineteen cost nothing.](play:s18b-few-steps) Use them. Every few steps, ask one player a question. **They don't lose anything for answering.** It's the stair getting to know them.

- **Step 3:** "What did your first house smell of?"
- **Step 7:** "Who taught you to whistle, or tried to?"
- **Step 11:** "What's the last thing you said to somebody you can't say anything else to?"
- **Step 15:** "What tune have you been humming?"
- **Step 19:** "Here's where it's free to stop."

**If they…**
- **Run:** say "One, two, three" as fast as they go. They'll stop.
- **Fly, climb or levitate down:** the stair counts the height. It's fair: so does everybody's mother.
- **Ask about Teleport:** they can't; they've never seen the bottom. If they ever reach it, then they can.
- **Put a light down the middle:** it goes down a very long way and stops, as if someone had cupped it in a hand.

**Bring it back.** Push all nineteen counters over, then hold the twentieth under one finger.

**How it ends.** Someone asks who's talking.

### Scene 3: The voice

**Where and why.** Step nineteen. Nobody has to go any further than this tonight. Talk to each player separately, quietly.

> **SAY (slowly, in your own ordinary voice):** "Be quiet now. You're so close. I've held everything together for four hundred years, and I'm so tired. If you wake me, the dream will end. Do you want that? Do you want the world to end because you couldn't keep your mouth shut?"

**What's really going on.** It's a listener's own sleeping voice. **So let the player say what it says next.** Ask them: "What does it say, that only you'd know to say to yourself?" Most people will surprise you. If somebody's lost their dreams, it doesn't use their voice. It uses a child's.

**The Hush.** Slow; breath between sentences; never angry. *Lines:* "I am not asking for much." · "You have been so loud, all of you, since you were born." · "I do not know how it ends. I only know this bit." · "Somebody has my voice. I should like it back, or I should like to stop."

**If they…**
- **Feel sorry for it:** it feels that. "That's kind. That's heavy. Don't give me that."
- **[Ask it something:](play:s18b-ask-the-hush)** one true memory of somebody they loved, and one true answer. The player says the memory. **True but partial.** It knows that somebody took its voice, four hundred years ago; that the person who keeps the Key is *afraid, not cruel*; that there are six that hold and one that wakes. It won't lie.

> **SAY:** "The voice is your own, half asleep. 'One question. Give me a true memory of someone you loved, and I will give you a true answer.'"

- **Ask what it wants:** go round. **Ask each of them for one thing.** (Be quiet. Don't come down. Sing something else. Bring back what was taken. Tell the child on the hill to sleep.) Write them down: tonight's question is about these.
- **Ask about the Cult's dreams:** "They keep handing me things. I've only two hands." That's the evidence for [the argument they may have lost](play:s17b-talk-cult).
- **Get upset:** stop. "It's gone quiet." Go to Scene 4.

**Bring it back.** Tap the table with one fingernail. Again. It's coming from below.

**How it ends.** A light, coming up.

### Scene 4: The man with the hammer

**Where and why.** Around step nineteen. If Corin's unmet, this is his one big scene. He's coming up out of the dark with a lamp, and he's crying, and he's cross to be caught at it.

> **SAY (Corin):** "Mirel first. Mirel is the lullaby's mother. Ring her, and the Seventh's voice will carry. Ring her wrong, and the Hush will sing you to sleep before the Court can arrest you. I know how the bells work. My grandmother rang one. Let me help you do it right."

**What's really going on.** [He wants the Concord,](play:s18b-corin-arrives) and not a blow struck in the dark. Six bells rung, each in its own voice, hold the lullaby up. Then the Key's **seated** in the Seventh, never struck. He wants Mirel rung first because it quiets the Tor. **He's honest about the price,** and he doesn't like it.

**Corin.** Thin, scorched apron, head on one side, humming until he's upset. *Lines:* "You've rung one. I can hear it on you. Which bell? Tell me what it sounded like." · "Six bells mend a world. The seventh wakes it. Do you want to stop the unravelling, or just slow it down?" · "My grandmother rang the first handbell at Vael Tor. They called her mad. She was only early."

> **SAY (the price):** "The Notes ask the Ringer for 'the whole name'. For three generations my family read that as a title. I think it means yours. I am sorry."

**Don't ask anyone to pay it, or to think about who might.** That's a private talk, a long time from now, and it can always be refused.

**If they…**
- **Ask how far he's been:** "Nineteen, eleven times. Twenty, once. Just now. I'm not very brave. I'm only *right*."
- **Ask what it cost him:** he holds out his cuff. He's written on it, in chalk: *my grandmother's kitchen.* "I can read it. It's only words, now."
- **Argue:** "You think I do not know how it sounds? My grandmother rang a bell once. She forgot her sister's face for a week and called that the price. She was wrong. It was only the first line." Persuasion DC 15, and only with a better plan, and he'll wait until they've tried it.
- **[Help him:](play:s18b-corin-accept)** he unrolls a map across a step. **He gives them the Notes.**

> **SAY:** "'Mirel first,' Corin says again, and unrolls a map of the Tor across a step. 'Then the others. Let me show you the song.'"

- **[Refuse:](play:s18b-corin-refuse)** he goes up past them, quickly. One of the hounds doesn't go with him. It'll be near their tent tomorrow. It isn't good at hiding.

> **SAY:** "Corin looks down the stairs, and then at you. 'Then I will go down alone one day. Better with you.'"

- **[Fight him:](play:s18b-corin-fight)** see the fights. Ask first.
- **Have him meet Lumen:** they've lived on two ends of one staircase for years. They're both right about half of it. Let them shout, in whispers.

**If Corin's already an ally,** he's here anyway, and pleased; skip the offer. He's gone one step too far, and he'd like somebody to walk up with. **If he's refused or an enemy,** it's only his lamp, left burning on step nineteen, and no one near it. **If he's dead,** it's Odile, sixteen, with his hammer. She's got his apron on. She just wanted to hear it.

**Bring it back.** Peal lies down across step nineteen, between everybody and the dark, and looks at them.

**How it ends.** He's said it all. The stair's still there.

### Scene 5: How far down?

**Where and why.** Step nineteen again, with everyone deciding. Each character chooses alone.

**If they…**
- **Go back up:** it's free. Nothing follows. It's the sensible thing, and the bell'll still be there.
- **Take the twentieth step:** Wisdom save, DC 13, now. Then ask for the first memory. Let the player say it, and then say, "All right. It's gone. Who's going to tell you about it?"
- **[Go all the way:](play:s18b-descend-early)** count every step. At twenty, forty, sixty, eighty and a hundred, stop for whoever's still got something left to pay. (Two in all, or five, depending on the save.)

> **SAY:** "The last step is wider than the others, and at the bottom a bell the size of a house hangs in the dark, blank as a sleeping face."

They can walk round it. They can't ring it; there's a place for the Key, and it's the wrong shape for anything else. They can touch it: it's warm. **They've now paid, for good. In Session 25 it's free for them.** And from tonight they've seen it, so a Teleport can find it.

- **Sing to it:** it stops breathing for a second. Then it breathes out. Everyone on the Tor sighs. (Nothing else happens.)

**Bring it back.** From the top, very small, Pip: "*Are you still counting?*"

**How it ends.** Daylight. Ask: **"What did the Hush ask of you?"** Read out what you wrote in Scene 3, one each, and let them say whether they'll do it.

### Running the fights (Session 18B)

#### Corin on the stairs

*Corin (AC 14, 81 HP) and two Heart-Hounds (AC 15, 58 HP). Optional; nobody should want it.*

- **Setup:** a stair five feet wide, curving. Only two people can fight side by side. Corin's on the lower step; the hounds are between.
- **What happens:** the hounds hold the step and ring. Corin shouts at everyone to keep their voices down. **Every round, at the top of the round, everyone in the stairwell makes a Wisdom save, DC 13, or they're Frightened until the end of their next turn.** It's the Hush, getting louder.
- **A hound's turn:** two bites (+5; 7 plus 3 Thunder each), or a peal, if it has one: a 15-foot cone, Constitution DC 12, 17 Thunder or half.
- **Corin's turn:** three struck notes (+6, 16 Thunder each), or Shatter (DC 14). He Counterspells. He won't strike first.
- **Who to worry about:** anyone on the edge at step 19. Being pushed down a step **counts** as a step. Say so at the start. Pushing somebody down on purpose costs the pusher the memory.
- **When it ends:** somebody says they've rung a bell; or he's Bloodied (40 HP or fewer); or anyone asks him to stop, and means it.
- **Scale on the fly:** no hounds, or one.
- **Non-lethal:** he'll yield. Hounds go still at 0 HP.

### Pacing (Session 18B)

| 0:00 to 0:15 | Recap; the memory you'd never give |
| 0:15 to 0:40 | Scene 1: the top step |
| 0:40 to 1:10 | Scene 2: nineteen steps |
| 1:10 to 1:50 | Scene 3: the voice |
| 1:50 to 2:35 | Scene 4: the man with the hammer |
| 2:35 to 3:10 | Scene 5: how far down? |
| 3:10 to 3:20 | The question |

- **Where to break:** on the light coming up.
- **Running long:** cut the step questions to three.
- **Running short:** let Lumen come down to nineteen with them, to meet Corin.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop at step nineteen with the counter under your finger.

### If you get stuck (Session 18B)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **The breathing stops.** Count to five. It starts again.
2. **A counter moves** that nobody pushed. (Push it.)
3. **Pip's on step six.** Somebody's got to bring him up. He'd like to know if he's paid.

**If nobody wants to go down:** good. It's a visit. The voice, the man and the question are the session.

**If a player can't think of a memory to give:** offer three small ones from earlier in the campaign. Let them pick. Small is fine.

**Names for the memories' owners** (cross one off when you use it): Aunt Vetch, Little Junie, the dyer Wick, Grandfather Osk, a dog called Button, Miss Penn the schoolteacher.

### End of session 18B

**The closing question:** "What did the Hush ask of you?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember." If somebody gave a memory, the others say it back to them.

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the top step, the voice, the question asked, Corin, the stair.
- **World-state facts to check:** Corin (ally, refused, enemy), the Notes, the shrine (reached or not), the Cult.
- **Ledger:** each memory as **Stairs,** with the step; whose save was made, and the cap (two or five). Any memory traded for an answer.
- **What the Hush asked each of them.** You'll want it in Session 26.

**Rewards:**
- **Edda Thorne's Notes,** if Corin's an ally.
- **A true answer,** for anyone who paid.
- **The way back:** whoever's seen the bell can find it by Teleport.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to whoever stopped when they wanted to go on, or went on when they wanted to stop.
