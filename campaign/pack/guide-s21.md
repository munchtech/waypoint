## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. It's the last bell on this side of the sea, and the first time the Court comes with everything it has on the road. The ringing's the middle of the night, not the end. After it, somebody brings news: Maudrin Vesk has stopped waiting.*

### Before you start (Session 21)

**Level:** four characters at level 8.

**Check the world state first.** Six facts decide the night:
- **Act IV road:** **A** rings **Mirel** tonight, on Vael Tor. **B** rings **Thrane** tonight, in the Lightning Shafts.
- **The Heart:** have they got it? No Heart, no ringing. Go to the refusal in Scene 2, or run the bargain again.
- **How many bells are already rung?** None or one, **Heavy**; two or three, **Grievous**; four or five, **Ruinous.** Waypoint shows it on the play.
- **Kessa** (road B): living, or dead? And the **oath:** sworn, or not? (Road A: was it kept or broken in 19A? It matters in Session 22.)
- **The Cult** (road A): ceased, or feeding?
- **Corin:** an ally? He's here, and he can put a Toll off an hour.

**What this session is about.** They've come to the end of what they can do without her. Every bell they ring from here, Maudrin Vesk hears.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Give the warning at its new weight. It's worse than last time. Say so.
- Let the bell be wonderful, and then give them half an hour of it.
- Hold or lose the road against a real Court patrol.
- End on the question: **"Maudrin is coming. Where will you meet her?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; four Wardens; two Sergeants; two Knights; eight Hushmen.
- **Maps:** the bell's place (the ledge, or the bed); a road with a ridge above it.
- **Road B:** a bowl of rice to pour for rain. **Road A:** a lullaby.
- **The Toll Deck,** and each player's off-limits list in front of you.
- **The Forecast page,** open. If they refuse tonight, you'll show it to them.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **The Sense Toll (Thrane takes one):** *Heavy*, one sense dulled for a year, with Disadvantage on every check that relies on it. *Grievous*, dulled for good. *Ruinous*, gone for a year (sight means Blinded, hearing Deafened), then dulled for good. Sight and hearing only if that player agreed at session zero.
- **The Fear Toll (Mirel gives one):** in sight of it, a Wisdom save, DC 13, at the start of each turn, or Frightened until that turn ends. *Heavy*, a year. *Grievous*, for good. *Ruinous*, for good, and it's something they can't avoid.
- **Lightning-Marked** (Thrane): once before the next dawn, when the Ringer takes Lightning damage, they can use their Reaction to deal 1d6 Lightning to someone within 10 feet.
- **Blinded:** can't see; Disadvantage on attacks, Advantage against them. **Deafened:** can't hear; fails any check that needs hearing. **Frightened:** Disadvantage while they can see it, and can't choose to move closer.
- **Warden's Stone:** no spells within 10 feet while it's out. AC 12, 10 HP, or Sleight of Hand, DC 15.
- **Nothing undoes a Toll.** Not Greater Restoration, not Wish. If somebody asks, say so before they ring, not after.

### Opening recap (Session 21)

> **SAY:** "You crossed the plain. You bargained for a Heart, and it cost you *(the oath / a dream / a Gift / a night's work you're not proud of)*. And then you came over the last rise and stood in front of *(Thrane / Mirel)*, and the bell leaned toward you. The dust on the road behind you is closer than it was. You've been saying, all the way here, that you'd decide who in the morning. It's morning."

- **Then hand it over:** "What's the last thing your character did before they went in?" One line each.

### Scene 1: The threshold

**Where and why.** The way in. Short. Settle who, and say what it costs now.

> **SAY (Thrane):** "The ladder goes down into blue light. Every nail in it is humming. Halfway down there's a ledge, and on the ledge a bell as tall as a horse, with sparks crawling over it like ants."

> **SAY (Mirel):** "Hundreds of people are asleep. The bed in the middle's made up, with one corner turned down. The bell over it is the colour of old honey, and it's so quiet in here you can hear it being patient."

**What's really going on.** It's the same as last time, and it's worse. Every bell that's rung makes the next one heavier. The bells aren't cruel; there's simply less of the world left for them to lean on.

**If they…**
- **Can't agree who:** ask each player, "If nobody else would, would you?" Then ask whoever said yes fastest to wait, and whoever said it slowest to speak.
- **Want Corin's help:** an hour's delay, never less. He says so. "It's a kindness, that's all. It isn't a discount."
- **Haven't got the Heart:** they stand there. It can still be got. Offer them one more try at the [quick loan](play:s20-accept-kessa-quick) or the [quick trade](play:s20-courier-bargain-quick), with the Court an hour nearer.
- **Want to know what's on the road:** an hour away. Enough to ring. Not enough to rest.
- **Ask what Maudrin knows:** she felt the last one. She'll feel this.

**Bring it back.** The bell's a little closer than it was when they came in. It hasn't moved.

**How it ends.** Someone has their hand on the Heart.

### Scene 2: The second ringing

**Where and why.** The bell. Straight, slow, and with the music off.

**Say the warning out loud, to the whole table:**

> **SAY (Thrane):** "Ringing Thrane will take a sense from you, and it will be *(heavy / grievous / ruinous)*. You won't know exactly what until it's paid, and it can never be undone or bought back. Do you ring?"

> **SAY (Mirel):** "Ringing Mirel will give you a fear, and it will be *(heavy / grievous / ruinous)*. You won't know exactly what until it's paid, and it can never be undone or bought back. Do you ring?"

**Then, out of character,** read them the one line for that weight from the rules above. Yes or no, nothing in between. Anyone can ring for anyone. Anyone can say no and not say why.

**If they…**
- **[Ring Thrane:](play:s21-ring-thrane-second)** ask the Ringer to hit the table with a flat hand. Pour the rice.

> **SAY:** "The bell cracks like a whip. For the first time in four centuries a real storm rolls over the mountains."

  Then be quiet, and give the Toll. Choose a sense that character's enjoyed *at this table*. Start with the small true thing: "You can smell rain. Everyone's always told you about it. It smells of nothing."
- **[Ring Thrane with Kessa dead:](play:s21-ring-thrane-second-kessa-dead)** the same storm. Nobody cheers. Yenna stands in it with her hands open.

> **SAY:** "The bell cracks like a whip, and a real storm rolls over the mountains."

- **[Ring Mirel:](play:s21-ring-mirel-second)** almost whisper it:

> **SAY:** "The bell sings like a lullaby sung by someone who loves you, and the Sleepless dissolve into mist."

  Then go round: "What does your character hope for, right now?" **Then** the Toll. Something small and particular, and a bit odd, that'll turn up again.
- **[Refuse, all of them:](play:s21-refuse-second)** it's a real ending to the scene. Read this and leave a gap after it.

> **SAY:** "The second bell waits, silent, as you turn away from it."

  Open the Forecast and show them what it closes: the Concord needs all six. The Second Dawn stays open. They can come back for this bell in Session 22, and it'll cost them days. Maudrin still moves.
- **Drop before it's struck:** the ringing's cancelled, and nothing's owed. After the strike, it's owed.

**Record it at once:** the kind, the bell, the weight, and the player's own one line.

**Bring it back.** The Ringer's first bell answers, very far off. They're the only one who hears it.

**How it ends.** They come up, or out, into whatever they've made.

### Scene 3: What it gave back

**Where and why.** Outside. The reward. Twenty minutes, and nobody's allowed to interrupt it.

> **SAY (Thrane rang):** "It's raining on people who've only read about it. A whole camp's standing in it with their mouths open. An old man's got his boots off and he's stamping in a puddle, scowling, as if it might get away."

> **SAY (Mirel rang):** "They've all turned over. Somebody's snoring, and somebody's laughing in their sleep, and a little old woman in the third row says, very clearly, 'Oh, *there* you are.'"

> **SAY (neither):** "Nothing happens. It's an ordinary afternoon. You can hear a lark. You'd forgotten there were ordinary afternoons."

**Pick one or two:**
- **Road B, Kessa living:** he picks up a cup, with two fingers, easily. It's a season's ease and not a cure, and he knows it. "I'll wait at the Hall, rider. Don't be long." Then settle the oath, here or on the way:
  - **[Give the Heart back:](play:s21-return-tongue)** the oath's **Kept.** They'll ride at the Strike.

> **SAY:** "Kessa hangs the Heart back on its skull. 'You kept your word.'"

  - **[Keep it:](play:s21-keep-tongue)** the oath's **Broken.** He doesn't chase them.

> **SAY:** "Kessa watches you ride out with his Heart and says nothing."

  - **Never swore:** it's a thing between honest people. Ask what's fair.
- **Road B, Kessa dead:** Yenna asks for it. Nobody's oath binds them. "I'd like one thing of his to be where he left it."
- **Road A:** Lumen's on her knees. [The whole Cult's stopped humming.](play:s19b-cult-after-mirel) If they weren't convinced, they are now. Brother Lull's going along the rows with a list of names, and where they came from.
- **Road A, and they swore to Kessa months ago:** somebody rides in from the Ironhorn with one bronze bead. It's the same news: "He says, is it kept?" They know whether it is.

**Go round:** "What does your character do with twenty minutes of this?" The Ringer last.

**Bring it back.** The Toll, gently. The thing they can't smell; the thing in the corner of the room.

**How it ends.** Birds go up, all at once, along the ridge.

### Scene 4: A long grey line

**Where and why.** The valley road. The big fight of Act IV, if they want it.

> **SAY:** "Grey riders appear on the ridge, a long line, gloves like mirrors."

**What's really going on.** [It's the biggest patrol they've seen.](play:s21-court-patrol-arrives) It was sent to hold the road, and it was sent two days before Maudrin decided to come herself. They want the Ringer and the Heart. They'd rather not kill anybody. Commander Rushe has been told not to come back without one of the two.

**Commander Rushe.** Fifty, spare, exact; a scar through one eyebrow. Reads from a paper and hates it. *Lines:* "By order of the Court of Hush: the bell, the Ringer, or the Heart. I'm to ask for all three. I'd settle for the one." · "I've a list of your names. They've spelt yours wrong." · "I'd take a surrender. I'd take it at any point. Please remember that I said so."

**If they…**
- **Talk:** Influence, Persuasion DC 17, once. A success doesn't send Rushe home. It buys the thing they asked for: ten minutes, or the wounded left alone, or the Hall untouched.
- **Give up the Heart:** they'll go. Write it down. [It can be won back at the Strike:](play:s23-recover-thrane) tell them so. (Mirel's is [the same](play:s23-recover-mirel).)
- **Give up the Ringer:** it's a jail, not a grave, and there's a rescue.
- **Use a friend:** the Ironhorn (oath kept, or Yenna alone), or the Cult's circle, or the Bellwrights with Corin, can break the line. **An ally takes an objective, never a kill:** Yenna's riders drive off the Knights' horses; the Cult links hands across the road; Corin's hounds open every lead case in the rear rank.
- **Run:** there's one track. Horses are faster. They lose the tail of the party unless somebody holds the narrow place. Who?
- **Hide:** group Stealth, DC 14, in a valley in broad daylight. With rain, or with mist, Advantage.
- **Fight:** see below. Surrender's offered before, and once in each round.

**Bring it back.** Rushe, between sentences, wipes rain off the list with a thumb. (If it's not raining, dust.)

**How it ends.** Three Wardens down, or a Heart handed over, or the party gone. Rushe looks at the paper, and folds it up very small.

### Scene 5: Maudrin is coming

**Where and why.** The far end of the valley, at dusk. The whole of Act IV's been leading here.

> **SAY:** "There's a rider on the road, coming the other way, on a horse that's nearly finished. They don't slow down for you. They just fall off it, more or less, at your feet, and start talking."

**Sela Pask.** Young, filthy, hoarse. Talks in bits, with her hands on her knees. *Lines:* "Hallowmere. I've come from… give me a minute. She's packing." · "She's leaving the city. Vesk. *Herself.* Within the day." · "I've watched that gate four years. She's never once come out of it."

**What's really going on.** The ringing frightened her. She hasn't been frightened since she was a child, and she doesn't know what to do with it except work. She's going to Vael Tor, with the Key, to seal the stairs before anyone can reach the Seventh. **She leaves within a day,** unless something they did earlier changed that.

**What Sela knows, if they ask:**
- **How many?** A column. Carts. She counted forty before someone looked up.
- **Who's with her?** The Dawn Order, if Rook lost the duel. Only the grey, if Rook won.
- **Why now?** "Because of *you*. Everyone in the city heard it. The windows hummed."
- **Where?** "The south road. It only goes to one place."
- **What was she carrying?** "One small trunk. And a case, about this long, and she wouldn't let anybody else touch it."
- **If Maudrin's dead already:** it's Tristan packing, with a ledger under one arm. The rest's the same.

**If they…**
- **Want to go for her now:** Session 22 starts at dusk. This is dusk.
- **Want to rest:** they can have a Long Rest. She's four days from the Tor.
- **Ask for help:** who've they been kind to? Start the list. It's next week's session.
- **Give Sela a horse:** she's theirs for life. Where does she ride?
- **Despair:** Sela looks at them, puzzled. "She's never come *out* before. You did that."

**Bring it back.** Somewhere, a long way south, the bell they've just rung is still going. Very faintly. Nobody's touched it.

**How it ends.** Ask: **"Maudrin is coming. Where will you meet her?"** Let them talk. Write down every place they name.

### Running the fights (Session 21)

#### The Court patrol

*Four Wardens (AC 16, 82 HP), two Sergeants (AC 15, 52 HP), two Dawn Order Knights (AC 18, 52 HP), eight Hushmen (AC 12, 32 HP). It's a big fight for level 8: talk first, run it outside, and use the friends they've made.*

- **Setup:** a valley road, 20 feet wide, with a ridge 30 feet up on one side and a stream on the other. The Wardens are on the road in a line. The Knights are mounted, behind them. (If Rook won the duel, they're the last two who followed Haldric. Leave them out if you'd rather.) The Hushmen are along the ridge.
- **What happens:** the Wardens hold the road and open their cases, staggered, so the light goes flat in patches. Round 2, the Knights charge through the middle. Round 3, the Hushmen come down off the ridge in two fours, at whoever's carrying the Heart.
- **A Warden's turn:** two blows (+5, 12 damage, and pushed 10 feet), or two crossbow shots (+4, 13).
- **A Sergeant's turn:** two light blows or shots (+4; about 5 each, and 3 Necrotic with the glove), or one and a signal: an ally attacks with a d4 extra.
- **A Knight's turn:** two swords (+5, 10 and 4 Radiant each). They won't strike anyone who's down. They won't strike a child, or a bell.
- **A Hushman's turn:** a glove (+5, 5 and 2 Necrotic; Advantage beside a friend), or an arrest (Strength DC 12 or Grappled; escape DC 12).
- **Who to worry about:** the Ringer. If they're Grappled and carried 60 feet, they've been taken. Say how far every round.
- **The easy wins:** break a stone (AC 12, 10 HP). Spook the horses. Block the road with a cart. Put the Heart somewhere nobody expects.
- **When it ends:** three Wardens down; or they've got a Heart, and they go; or the party's away.
- **Scale on the fly:** take out a Warden and both Knights for a lighter fight. Keep four Hushmen on the ridge until round four if it's going badly.
- **Non-lethal:** all of it. They arrest. Anyone who drops wakes up tied, and not far away.

### If you get stuck (Session 21)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **The bell rings once more by itself,** and everyone, on both sides, stops and looks up.
2. **One of the grey riders takes their gloves off,** and puts them down on the road.
3. **Sela arrives early,** through the middle of everything, shouting the news to both sides.

**If the Toll lands badly:** stop, change it, out loud. "It wasn't that. It was this."

**If they refuse and then regret it:** they can come back, in [Session 22](play:s22-detour-for-a-missed-bell). It'll cost days, and the Court will use them.

**Names for riders, scouts and grey coats** (cross one off when you use it): Hushman Selwyn, Hushman Gale, Corporal Dunne, old Bryn the drover, Veda, Corm.
