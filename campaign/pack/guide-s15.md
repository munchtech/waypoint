## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. The winter is over and everyone it took is waking up hungry. Then the Court arrives, very politely, for what the party carries. It ends with a choice of road that decides the whole of Act IV. It's mostly people, and one short show of force.*

### Before you start (Session 15)

**Level:** four characters at level 6. They reach level 7 at the end, once Glassreach is settled.

**Check the world state first.** Six facts change tonight:
- **Did Hieme ring?** If nobody rang it, there's no thaw: the Stilled who are awake are the only ones, Scene 1 is a few people by one fire, and Isolde still comes.
- **Isolde:** following them, turned informant, captured (then Tristan Vesk comes instead), or retreated to the Court or never sent (then skip Scene 3 and give its time to Scenes 1 and 2).
- **Imber:** frozen, freed, dead, or taken. If he's free, Isolde's scene changes. If he's frozen in the chapel, she finds him.
- **The Hearts they carry.** Make a list. Isolde asks for Hieme's, and for Calla's if they have it.
- **Do the Stilled trust them?** No bond, wary, or earned. That picks which plays appear.
- **Which Stormborn ally do they have?** None, the Harpers, or Sister Aurelie's Dawn Mission. If none, tonight can give them a crew.

**What this session is about.** They've changed something very big, and now there are consequences: ninety-odd hungry strangers from four hundred years ago, a bosun who wants to go home, and a woman in grey lace with a small army of gloves. Nobody has to be beaten. Then they choose which of the two last bells to go for first.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Give each player one Stilled to help. Ask: "What does your character do for one person who's just woken up?"
- Let Isolde be frightened of them, and make her polite about it.
- Say the two roads fairly. Both will be visited: the one they leave gets shorter later.
- End on the question: **"Brassfall or Vael Tor: which bell next?"** Then stop. They'll plan on their own time.

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Isolde (or Tristan); one Sergeant and four Hushmen; Yara; Maren; Imber; Vell.
- **Maps:** Yara's camp among thawing trees: one fire, a long table, a track to the south.
- **Props:** something wet. A bowl of melting ice in the middle of the table that you don't mention.
- **The sketch,** if one of them drew it. If not, be ready to ask someone to describe the top of the Spire.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Influence** is the action for changing somebody's mind. Roll the skill you're using against the DC. Friendly people are easier to ask and hostile ones won't listen without a reason.
- **Winning back the Stilled:** Influence DC 17, with advantage if Maren or a Stilled they saved speaks for them. A sincere apology or a night on guard counts as a reason. If it fails, try again after a night of real work.
- **Grappled:** Speed 0, and Disadvantage on attacks against anyone but the grappler. A Hushman's Arrest is a Strength save, DC 12. Escape takes an action: Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics), DC 12.
- **Isolde's Cunning Action:** Dash, Disengage or Hide as a Bonus Action. That's how she leaves.
- **A creature at 0 HP** can be knocked out instead of killed, when a melee attack brings it there. Captured, not dead.
- **Heroic Inspiration** for a memory or a Gift given freely to a waking Stilled.
- **Cold:** it's thawing. No more saves by the fire. If they go out at night, Constitution DC 10 without a cloak.

### Opening recap (Session 15)

> **SAY:** "Last time, you climbed the Frost Spire, and one of you heard Hieme ring. *(If it rang: say who, and what it cost.)* You slept to the sound of dripping. *(If it didn't: 'You came down, and it's still winter.')* You wake up and the first thing you hear is somebody crying, and then somebody laughing, and then a lot of people asking each other's names."

- **Then hand it over:** "What's the first thing your character does for a stranger this morning?" One answer each, quickly. That's Scene 1.

### Scene 1: The thaw

**Where and why.** Yara's camp, and the trees around it. Skip it if Hieme wasn't rung; instead, do the fire with whoever's awake.

> **SAY:** "The forest drips. Everywhere, glass is turning back into bark and leaf, and people are stepping out of their own winters with their arms still raised, asking each other's names."

**What's really going on.** There are ninety-odd people who haven't eaten since 1082. Yara has a kitchen and no plan, which is a first. [Helping them is the whole scene.](play:s15-help-stilled)

**Go round the table. Ask each player: "One person. What do they need, and what do you do?"** Take whatever they say, let it work, and give Heroic Inspiration for anything that costs them.

**Who they might meet:** a tall woman who can't stop apologising; a boy with a fishing rod; two brothers who want to know who won; a baker; a nun of the Ninefold (Brother Ewin's friend Sister Corrow); Joss Arrand with his bucket, if he was calmed in Session 11.

**If they…**
- **[Organise it:](play:s15-help-stilled)** Yara's camp as a hall, Wyn and Maren as guides, Kell's Crossing bread if Calla rang. It's the biggest thing a group can do here. It earns their trust.
- **Are in a hurry:** Yara takes charge. "Coin and one promise. Come back."
- **The child asks for her mother.** Let the table decide who tells her, and what. Don't rush. Her mother isn't among the Stilled. Her name was on a ribbon that Maren's family never found.
- **[The Stilled remember them badly:](play:s15-win-back-the-stilled)** if trust is wary, a woman steps out of the winter: "You. You woke my husband with fire." Read it, and let them answer. Influence DC 17, with advantage if Maren or a Stilled they saved vouches.
- **Vell and his crew:** twelve of them are awake and standing in a row, in order, the way they stood for muster.

> **SAY:** "'The *Brightwater* was moored at a river mouth, west of here, under a sky full of weather.' The bosun looks at the clear, still air. 'Where did the weather go?'"

- **[Promise them passage:](play:s15-brightwater-crew)** it'll take Brannagh Wick's pilots and a captain who'll carry them. Influence DC 15 on a Baldurian factor or the Corvane Venture. Venn will ask for nothing and want everything. If they show Vell's badge, it counts as his word; the crew follow whoever holds it. Let the player who's holding it say what they promise.
- **If somebody offers Venn's agents:** they'd pay well to show the crew in Athkatla. Vell gets to hear that. He says no. "We're not a fair."
- **Won't take them on:** they set out south alone, and Isolde's Hushmen arrest them at Kell's Crossing. Say so.

**Bring it back.** Someone who was calmed in Session 11 comes and puts a ribbon in a player's hand. It's theirs, with their own name on it.

**How it ends.** Everyone has a fire, a name and a bowl. And somebody says, "There are horses on the track."

### Scene 2: The request

**Where and why.** The long table, that afternoon. Only if enough of the Stilled are awake (Waypoint offers this card once the thaw's well along). Otherwise fold it into Scene 1.

> **SAY:** "The woman who steps forward is Maren Talla, thin and upright, with the dust of four centuries in her hair. 'We have a request,' she says. 'And a debt we would like to repay.'"

**What's really going on.** [The Stilled have a voice.](play:s15-stilled-emissary) They want food, shelter and somewhere to go. They'll walk to the Tor when the time comes, if trust is earned, and carry the Sexton's story. If trust is wary they want amends first. Influence DC 15 then; none if it's earned.

**Maren.** *Lines:* "I've been told. I should like to hear it from you. What year is it?" · "We don't want to be a wonder. We want to be neighbours." · "We'll walk. Slowly. Tell us which road." · "He asked me to forgive him. I said I'd think about it. I've had practice thinking."

**If they…**
- **Ask the year for her:** she listens to the number, nods, and counts it on her fingers.
- **Promise them somewhere:** record it in the bargain log. Kell's Crossing, Yara's camp, the Hall: anything they can do.
- **Ask them to fight:** they'll think about it. If trust is earned, they'd stand in front of somebody. They won't carry a blade.
- **Say no:** they thank them and ask for a map.

**Bring it back.** Maren gets up, looks at the Spire dripping, and sits down again in the first chair she's chosen for herself.

**How it ends.** The horses.

### Scene 3: What a lovely thaw

**Where and why.** The south track, at the edge of the camp. Yara's at their back, and Maren's. Isolde comes with a Sergeant and four Hushmen.

> **SAY:** "She's on foot, which she never is, with her skirt pinned up out of the mud and a Sergeant two paces behind. She looks at the fires, the table, the people. Then she looks at you, and smiles, the one that reaches her eyes."

Then, unless she's their informant or Tristan came instead, she says the plan's speech:

> **SAY (Isolde):** "What a lovely thaw. I'm told you have something of ours. The Frost Heart, let us say. Give it to us, and the Court will forget your little interference. Refuse, and the Court will remember it very precisely."

**What's really going on.** She's alone in the north, she doesn't want a fight, and she's frightened of what these people did. She'll leave if they refuse, and remember everything.

**Isolde.** Silk over mail, two sugars in a travelling cup, kind when nobody's looking. *Lines:* "You've made such a mess. It's charming." · "I'm not here to be clever, darling. I'm here because it's *cold*." · "Put it in my hands, and it stops being your problem. I'd love it if it stopped being your problem." · If they mention her son: "Leave my son out of this, or I'll put you in it."

**If they…**
- **[Refuse:](play:s15-refuse-isolde)** she smiles and leaves. On the way out she looks once at whatever they love most here, so they see her notice. Insight DC 15: relief.
- **[Give it to her:](play:s15-give-tongue)** she takes it in both hands, away from her body. If they carry Calla's Heart she asks for that too, sweetly, and the Hushmen step closer. Hieme has already rung, so the thaw goes on. Record her promise in the Bargain Log, word for word, and write down what it doesn't cover.

> **SAY:** "She takes the Heart in both hands and holds it away from her body, as if it might drip. 'There. Wasn't that easy?'"

- **If it's hanging in the bell:** "Go and get it, then." She looks at the Spire. She isn't going up it. Her Hushmen spend a day finding that out.
- **[Attack her:](play:s15-attack-isolde)** see the fights. She's already moving.
- **[Capture her:](play:s15-capture-isolde)** a successful grapple, or 0 HP before her second turn. "Oh, you wonderful, wonderful people," she says, with her eyes on the road. Her Hushmen surrender. Treated kindly, she'll talk about her son, the Ironhorn sales and what the Court is planning.
- **She's their informant:** she makes the speech in front of her own people, word for word. [Then, while they water the horses,](play:s15-isolde-informant) without moving her lips: "I need a story. Give me one I can sell." They "lose" the Heart in the thaw, and she reports it. Deception DC 12 to stage it. She tells them what she knows: the Chancellor marches on the Tor the moment two more bells ring.
- **[She recognises Imber:](play:s15-isolde-wants-imber)** she stops in the middle of a word.

> **SAY:** "Isolde stops smiling. She looks at the old man in the borrowed cloak, at the shape of his nose, at the ring on his finger. 'Oh,' she says softly. 'Oh, Maudrin will hate this.'"

- **What she offers:** take him to Hallowmere, for his safety. She means Maudrin's. Influence DC 15 makes her forget she saw him; she wants something for it. If they say nothing, Imber introduces himself by his full title.
- **[She takes him:](play:s15-isolde-takes-imber)** only if they let her, or lose. If he was frozen, her people carry him out in his block with a lamp under it.
- **Tristan comes instead:** [his own offer](play:s15-tristan-envoy), less charming and more honest. "Tristan Vesk. The Chancellor's cousin, and, I'm afraid, the one who writes her letters." He wants Isolde back; her release is a bargaining chip. He mentions, to see their faces, that the Court has a second Chancellor waiting.
- **Haven't got it:** lie (Deception DC 15), or say who has.

**Bring it back.** The fourth Hushman, the one who heard the bell, has stopped listening to anybody. Ask them what they hear.

**How it ends.** Isolde is gone, or taken, or on their side. The Hushmen are wet to the knee.

### Scene 4: The map and the road

**Where and why.** Evening. Yara's table, one lamp, and the road.

> **SAY:** "Yara clears a space with her forearm and unrolls the sketch. She weights its corners with cups. 'Nobody has ever drawn that,' she says, and for once she does not name a price."

**What's really going on.** [The sketch.](play:s15-yara-map) If nobody drew one, she asks them to describe it and writes it herself. It earns her loyalty: a guide, stores and a lower price. Heroic Inspiration for a drawing given freely.

**Then the road.** Tell them both, the same way, and don't lean.

> **SAY (Brassfall):** "East, past the last glass trees, the sky flickers over a line of bronze-veined mountains, though there are no clouds at all."

> **SAY (Vael Tor):** "South and east, the plain rises to a single hill ringed with grey stones. Even from here, if you close your eyes, you can hear something breathing in its sleep."

**What they know, so you can say it straight:**
- **[Brassfall](play:s15-road-brassfall):** six days around the Ironhorn foothills. The bell is Thrane, storms; its Heart is in Kessa's Heart-Hall. Ringing it brings the storms back, and sailors say it'd loosen the Stormwall.
- **[Vael Tor](play:s15-road-vael-tor):** about a week, by Kell's Crossing. The bell is Mirel, dreams. If they have its Heart, they can ring it as soon as they get there; if not, the Librarian's courier is waiting at the Hall.
- **Both bells are needed** for the Concord. The one they don't choose now becomes a shorter visit later (Sessions 20 and 21).
- **Yara:** "Brassfall pays better."
- **Imber,** if he's alive: "The Tor. It is where it ends. I would like to see it before."

**Bring it back.** Yara unrolls another sheet, empty. "That's for whichever one you draw next."

**How it ends.** Ask: **"Brassfall or Vael Tor: which bell next?"** If they can't agree, they don't have to tonight. Let them say what they'd want to know first.

### Running the fights (Session 15)

#### Isolde's escort

*Lady Isolde (Court Agent), one Hushman Sergeant and four Hushmen. A show of force. The party is meant to win, or she's meant to leave.*

- **Setup:** at the edge of camp, in mud. Isolde at the front and the Hushmen in a loose half-circle.
- **What happens:** she talks first. She fights only if attacked: one round of poisoned daggers, then Cunning Action to Disengage and Dash for the trees. The Hushmen grapple whoever's closest to her and fall back by twos.
- **She has silence-stone gloves.** Her attacks make no sound. They don't stop spells.
- **Who to worry about:** nobody. Hushmen want arrests. If one drops a character, it ties their hands and waits.
- **When it ends:** she's out of sight among the trees. Her people surrender if the Sergeant goes down, or if she's taken. Nobody chases anybody south.
- **If they fought out of Hallowmere in Session 9,** add two Hushmen.
- **If they raid her camp by night,** add two more Sergeants.
- **If it's Tristan,** a smaller escort and no Cunning Action. He leaves with a promise.
- **Scale on the fly:** three characters, the Sergeant and two Hushmen.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** it's a fight in front of ninety witnesses, and she knows it. She'll call off her own people the moment it looks ugly.

- **Running long:** fold the request into the thaw. Never cut the road.
- **Running short:** let each player say one thing to a person they've helped, on the way out.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop when Isolde leaves. Ask the road question by message, between sessions.

### If you get stuck (Session 15)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Somebody asks them their name** and gets it wrong on purpose, politely.
2. **The child comes out from under the table** and asks to be held.
3. **A Hushman drops their gloves in the mud.** They've got no idea what they're for now.

**If they can't choose the road:** ask each of them to name one person they want to see again before the end, and where that person is.

**Names for the waking** (cross one off when you use it): Sister Corrow, the baker Hen Tull, the boy Finny with the fishing rod, the tall woman Iva Roke, Mr. Aske the schoolmaster, two brothers, Ren and Hal Dorne.

### End of session 15

**The closing question:** "Brassfall or Vael Tor: which bell next?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember."

**Rewards:**

- **Heroic Inspiration:** to whoever gave the most to a stranger, and to whoever drew the Spire.
