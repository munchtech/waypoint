## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. Tonight they leave Hallowmere, perhaps after ringing its bell. It has more possible scenes than any night so far, and you won't run them all: check the world state, pick the two or three that fit, and keep the Ledger for the end. They reach level 5 tonight.*

### Before you start (Session 9)

**Level:** four characters at level 4. They reach level 5 at the end, once Hallowmere is settled.

**Check the world state first.** Seven facts in Foresight decide which scenes you run:
- **The duel:** did Rook or Haldric win? It decides who guards the Belfry and whether the Dawn Order helps or hunts.
- **Vaer's Heart:** still in the armory, or did they take it in Session 6? Ossa's may hang beside it.
- **Did they leave last night?** If they left by the north gate in Session 8, skip Scenes 1 and 2 and start on the road.
- **Aurelie:** with them, on the terraces, or under arrest?
- **Jonah and Ilam:** together, apart, or not yet met?
- **The Tithe Clock:** at 6 there's a checkpoint on every road, and the gate is the Gate Sweep.
- **Bells rung so far:** count them. It sets the weight of the next Toll.

**What this session is about.** Hallowmere has one bell, and it hangs in chains of silence-stone above a knight's barracks. If they ring it, the city sees one real day of sun, and the Ringer is wanted everywhere the Court rules. Then they have to get out, with a Court lady a mile behind them, and three days up the road a burned granary holds a second bell that wants a gift.

**Your goals tonight:**
- The ringing is the night's high point. Slow down for it: state the Toll, wait for the yes, read the box.
- Getting out is a puzzle with many answers. Reward any plan that uses a friend they made.
- Nobody dies in the street. Capture is a cell, not a Tithe.
- Leave twenty minutes for the Ledger. It's the whole of Act II in seven questions.
- End on: "Which unfinished promise do you carry north?"

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; two Dawn Order knights; a Hushman Sergeant and six Hushmen; Lady Isolde; Rook, Aurelie, Bet.
- **Maps:** the Belfry stair and chain-room; the north gate with a cart queue. Kell's Crossing needs no map.
- **Props:**
  - *The Ledger:* the seven questions on one card (they're in Scene 5).
  - *A Bargain Log line:* a slip of paper for the promise they carry north.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **The armory grille:** Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) with Thieves' Tools, DC 17. The yard: Stealth DC 15. Pass without Trace makes the yard easy; the lock is still a lock.
- **The chains on the bell:** Rook's chain-key opens them; or Thieves' Tools DC 17; or break three chain-stones (AC 12, 10 HP each), which is loud.
- **The Toll warning:** before any ringing, say what kind of thing it takes and how heavy, never exactly what. Weight: 0 to 1 bells already rung is Heavy, 2 to 3 is Grievous, 4 to 5 is Ruinous. Wait for a yes. You choose what's lost after it rings. Children never ring, and an absent player's character never pays.
- **The Silence Warrant:** any Hushman may arrest the Ringer on sight in a Court city. A bluff about the Ringer is 2 harder.
- **The gate:** Group Stealth DC 15 through the drains; Deception DC 17 to bluff a search (+2 if Sergeant Cole remembers them from Session 4).
- **A Heart hums.** Locate Object finds one within 1,000 feet, unless it's in a silence-stone-lined box. The box itself is contraband.
- **The Tithe Clock:** +1 a night in the city. At 6, the sweep and checkpoints. At 12, silence-stones go into every street.

### Opening recap (Session 9)

Come to Glassreach.* A carriage brought you the Chancellor's goodbye. *(Say what they did with Isolde.)* You said your farewells. *(Say who, in a few words.)* Tonight, Hallowmere still has one thing in it that isn't grey, and it hangs in chains above a barracks."

### Scene 1: Vaer's Belfry

**Where and why.** The Dawn Order barracks, at night. Skip this scene if they left last night or don't want the bell.

> **SAY:** "The armory is a long room of racked spears and yellow pennons gone grey. Behind an iron grille, a bronze clapper hangs on a peg, the colour of sunlight in a country that has none."

**What's really going on.** Vaer's Heart (its clapper) is in the armory, behind a grille. The bell is at the top of the Belfry, chained in silence-stone, with two knights on the stair. Rook, if she won, would like it rung and can't be seen to help.

**Rook.** Clipped, says "we" for "I". *Lines:* "One night. If it goes wrong, we never saw you." · "If it goes right, we'll be the one standing under it." · "We've waited our whole life. We can wait one more minute."

**If they…**
- **[Steal Vaer's Heart:](play:s09-steal-vaer)** Stealth DC 15 across the yard, Thieves' Tools DC 17 on the grille. A quiet job never meets the knights, unless they climb straight up to ring. Haldric (or Rook's quartermaster) finds the gap by the second hour.
- **[Take Ossa's too,](play:s09-steal-ossa-too)** if it hangs beside it: no further roll. It hums once, very low.
- **[Ask Rook for a loan:](play:s09-rook-loans-vaer)** Influence DC 15. She "misplaces" the key for the night and brings her knights down from the chain-room.
- **Go up to ring:** the chain-room knights, unless Rook cleared the stair or they carry Rook's writ (see the fights below). Then the chains.
- **[Ring Vaer:](play:s09-ring-vaer)** warn the Ringer: "Vaer will take a memory of a beginning from you, and it will be *(the weight)*." Never say which. Wait for a yes. If they decline, the bell doesn't ring and nothing is taken. After it rings, you choose the memory: a dawn they loved or, for someone who never saw one, a hoped-for beginning. Then read:

> **SAY:** "The bell cracks the grey like a knife through glass. For one heartbeat the cloud tears open, and a line of gold falls across the terraces. Every person in the city looks up. The opening widens. For this one day, sunlight reaches every terrace."

- **After it rings:** one whole day of real sun, and then a real dawn every morning for a year. Dawn-based abilities are at full strength today. The Ringer has a Silence Warrant. If Aurelie is in the city, [she's on the terraces singing](play:s09-aurelie-on-the-terraces) within the hour.

**Bring it back.** Down in the yard, a horse that has never seen the sun stamps and whinnies at it. Somebody in the barracks starts to laugh.

**How it ends.** They have the Heart, or the bell has rung, or both. Either way, the Court knows by morning.

### Scene 2: Getting out

**Where and why.** The next day, in the sun if Vaer rang. The north gate is the road to Glassreach, and the Court is searching carts.

> **SAY:** "At the north gate, four grey-gloved men are unloading every cart onto the road: grain sacks slit, barrels sounded, a coffin opened and closed again with an apology."

**What's really going on.** Cart searches follow a vault theft for two days, and the Warrant follows a ringing. There are four good ways out, and every one is a friend they made. Failure means a fight at the gate, never losing the Heart outright.

**If they…**
- **[Smuggle it out](play:s09-smuggle-out)** by one of these:
  - *Bet's flour cart:* the Hushmen never search it.
  - *Wren's drains* under the Seaward Market: Group Stealth DC 15, one wet hour.
  - *Ilam's roster:* it names the shift whose Sergeant takes gold.
  - *A night crossing* under Pass without Trace.
  - *Bluffing the search:* Deception DC 17, +2 if Sergeant Cole remembers them.
  - If every plan fails and Rook won, she rides out "on patrol" with the party as her escort.
- **[Lie low](play:s09-lie-low)** two nights in Bet's attic, the Dawn Mission's cellar or Rook's barracks. The searches end; the Tithe Clock goes up 2. Use the time for Lysse, or Ilam finding Jonah. If the clock is near 6, Bet says, "You can't stay. They're counting."
- **[Are spotted by the Warrant:](play:s09-warrant-arrest)** run it as a chase. Hushmen grapple for the Ringer and the Heart. The Dawn Order stands aside if Rook won and joins in if she lost. Hide in a crowd (Group Stealth DC 15), leave by the drains, or fight (the Gate Sweep, scaled down). If it turns grim, Rook rides across the terraces with six knights.
- **Aurelie,** if she sang on the terraces: hustle her out in Bet's cart, the Mission cellar or Rook's barracks (Deception or Group Stealth DC 15). If they're slow, [the Hushmen take her at dusk](play:s09-aurelie-arrested-at-dusk), unharmed, and the Court offers to trade her for the Ringer. [Freeing her](play:s09-free-aurelie): Rook's word, a forged release (Deception DC 15), or the drains; Tithe Clock +1.
- **Jonah and Ilam,** if still apart: [Ilam comes to Bet's door](play:s09-jonah-bakery) with his gloves off. Or [they fetch Jonah from the Hall](play:s09-free-jonah): a forged transfer (Deception or Forgery Kit DC 16) or Ilam's key, Stealth DC 15; Tithe Clock +1. Jonah is never shown in danger.
- **[Go back for the jars](play:s09-second-vault-visit)** with Ilam's key, if they skipped [the Tithe-works](play:s08-tithe-works) in Session 8: no roll at the door; Hushmen pass every ten minutes (Stealth DC 15 to wait in the shadows); Tithe Clock +1. Mara Bray's jar can leave in a satchel; only she can open it.

**Bring it back.** The gate bell clangs once for a shift change, and every Hushman on the road turns to look at it. Ask: "Who moves now?"

**How it ends.** They're through the gate and on the North Road.

### Scene 3: The carriage behind

**Where and why.** Two days out, at a waystation on the North Road. Skip it if they captured Isolde in Session 8.

> **SAY:** "Two days out, a carriage with grey curtains stops at the same waystation you do. Nobody gets out. In the morning it is a mile behind you, and it stays a mile behind you."

**What's really going on.** [Isolde follows](play:s09-isolde-follows) with two Hushmen (four if they fought their way out). She knows from the letter where they're going, so losing her only delays her. If they turned her in Session 8, [she follows as their informant](play:s09-isolde-follows-ally) and pins notes to waystation doors.

**If they…**
- **Notice her:** Perception or Survival DC 15. If nobody looks back, she sends a bottle of warm wine to their table, with her compliments.
- **Confront her:** she's charming and admits everything except her orders.
- **Lose her:** Pass without Trace and a Group Survival DC 15 off the road. She's two days behind.
- **[Capture her:](play:s09-capture-isolde)** she fights one round, then tries to Disengage into the trees. Grappled or dropped, she's taken. Her Hushmen surrender when she's down. Kindness gets her son, the Compact sales and the Court's plan; cruelty gets spit. The Court will send Tristan Vesk in her place.
- **Turn back for Pellmoor:** [a detour](play:s09-detour-pellmoor) of three days by ferry and road, then about two weeks to Glassreach. Ila, Venn's counting-house and [Ossa's bell](play:s09-ring-ossa-later) are there (its Toll is a memory of the dead; Ila may not ring it). Run it as its own session if they go.

**Bring it back.** At the waystation, a ewe pushes her head through the fence to watch them, then turns to watch the carriage.

**How it ends.** The Ninefold hills are ahead, and a plain of ash beside the road.

### Scene 4: Kell's Crossing

**Where and why.** Three days north, at a fork in the road. A village on an ash plain; the Old Granary, burned black, has a bell in its frame. Calla's Heart is in their pack, if they have it.

**What's really going on.** Calla is the bell of the harvest. Its Toll is a gift, given to someone in need. The village has had nothing to eat but Court grain for years. Nessa Harrow, Ila's mother, lives here.

**People.** The innkeeper of the Empty Sheaf has six children and one coat. Bet, if she came of her own free choice (she's eighty, and decides for herself whether to travel three days), would be the natural one to receive the gift.

**If they…** [ring Calla:](play:s09-ring-calla) warn the Ringer: "Calla will take a gift from you, given to someone in need, and it will be *(the weight)*." Wait for a yes. After it rings, you name the gift. Then read:

> **SAY:** "The sound is warm and round, and it smells of baking bread. The ash on the fields stirs. Somewhere a child laughs, and a woman begins to cry without knowing why. For one season, the grain comes up green across the plain."

- **After it rings:** one harvest season of bread for everyone. Any character who gives a gift at Kell's Crossing gains Heroic Inspiration, once per session. Decide with them where the Heart goes: left in the frame, or on with the party.
- **Nessa** comes out to look at the green fields. If anyone says Ila's name, she sits down in the ash. Let it be quiet.
- **The Court's collectors** will raid the Crossing for the rest of the season and try to take the Ringer. If the party stays, play it out; don't assume it.
- **If nobody wants to pay,** Bet (if she's with them and remembers) asks to ring it herself. She has paid before.

**Bring it back.** Behind the granary, the green is already ankle-high. A child is eating a stalk of it raw.

**How it ends.** They camp at the Crossing, or walk on. Either way, ask the Ledger questions.

### Scene 5: The Hallowmere Ledger

**Where and why.** [The North Road, at the last waystation before the hills](play:s09-hallowmere-ledger).

> **SAY:** "The North Road runs up out of the city between grey hedges, and the Dawn Order's watchtower falls behind you. Ahead, the land rises toward the Ninefold hills. Somewhere behind, a single bell tolls once, muffled by the cloud, and then falls silent."

**Ask these aloud, and let the players answer.** Set each in Foresight as they do. If they don't remember something, it didn't happen; don't punish a forgotten thread.
1. **The jar:** whose jar did you open, or take, or leave?
2. **Rook or Haldric:** who leads the Dawn Order now?
3. **Jonah and Ilam:** where are they tonight?
4. **Maudrin's bargain:** what did you agree, if anything?
5. **The diary:** who has Edda Thorne's diary, and what's left in it?
6. **The Hearts:** which do you carry, and which are rung?
7. **Tessa:** is she free?

Then the last question: **"Which unfinished promise do you carry north?"** Write their answer on the slip and in the Bargain Log.

**How it ends.** Level 5.

### Running the fights (Session 9)

#### The chain-room knights

*Two Dawn Order knights (Knight).*
- **Setup:** one holds the doorway at the top of the stair; one stands behind with a crossbow. They shout for the watch.
- **Each round:** they try to disarm and shove, not kill. They're honest men doing their duty.
- **When it ends:** they surrender when Bloodied. If the bell rings during the fight, both stop and stare.
- **Rook's writ:** they stand aside. Otherwise, Influence DC 15 to talk them down before steel comes out.
- **Scale on the fly:** this is Moderate for four level-4 characters. Three characters: one knight. If you run it after level 5, add a third knight.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** a downed character is carried down to the barracks and handed to Rook (or Haldric).

#### The Gate Sweep

*A Hushman Sergeant (Sergeant Cole) and six Gate Hushmen.*
- **Setup:** carts in a queue; the gate half-open. The Hushmen are spread along the carts.
- **Each round:** two grapple whoever carries the Heart; two close the gate; one runs for the alarm bell. The Sergeant goes for anyone matching the Warrant.
- **When it ends:** hold them for three rounds and the cart gets through. The Hushmen turn back after the first mile.
- **Scale on the fly:** three characters, the Sergeant and three Hushmen. If the Ringer has a Warrant and Haldric won the duel, add two Dawn Order knights instead.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** nobody dies at the gate. A captured character goes to a cell, and the others can get them out the same way they'd get Aurelie out.

#### Isolde's coach

*Lady Isolde (Spy) and two Hushmen (four if they fought their way out of the city).*
- **Setup:** night, on the road; the coach stops. Her Hushmen ride on the box.
- **Each round:** Isolde fights one round, then Disengages for the trees.
- **When it ends:** she's taken once she's grappled or dropped. Her Hushmen surrender when she's down. If it goes long, they walk away from the coach.

### End of session 9
