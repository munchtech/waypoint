## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. One room, one bell, and a choice. There's a fight in it, or there isn't. Either way, the important hour is the one where four people sit round your table and talk, so get there before anyone's tired.*

### Before you start (Session 26)

**Level:** four characters at level 11.

**Check the world state first.**
- **Who's got the Key?** This is the whole night. **Maudrin:** run Scenes 1, 2, 4 and 5. **The party:** Scene 2 is a page long. **The Court, sealed away:** they're not here; run [the rescue chapter](play:s27-rescue-chapter) instead.
- **Maudrin.** At the bell, beside them as a witness, captured, dead or exiled? And how hurt: if she was below half at the Strike or on the Tor, she starts on **85 HP.**
- **Ser Haldric.** Still hers, turned, or gone? That picks the fight.
- **Corin.** Ally: he's beside them. **Refused or an enemy: he's coming** (Scene 3). Unmet or dead: skip it.
- **The six bells.** Open the Forecast (Foresight) and read which endings are open, **with their prices.** Write them on a card in plain words.
- **The Session 11 note.** Which player saw themselves in the glass, and the words they said. Have them in front of you.
- **Oum** (asleep) and **Ysh** (unstruck). Check nobody's changed them.

**What this session is about.** Whether you can do a thing that can't be undone, and who gets to decide.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Let them look at it before anybody speaks.
- Give Maudrin her case, properly, before a single die.
- Put every open ending on the table with its price. Nobody should be surprised by a cost.
- **Take a real break** before they choose.
- End on the question: **"What will you ring, and what will it cost you?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **If the Concord's open: talk to the likely Ringer's player in private, days before.** Say what [the Great Toll](play:s27-concord) is: their name, forgotten by everyone, and the character retires as the Seventh's voice. They can say no now, or tonight, or with a hand on the bell. Refusing costs nothing.
- **The endings card,** one line each.
- **Tokens or minis:** one Keeper; five Wardens; four Sergeants; eight Hushmen; Haldric; Corin; two Heart-Hounds.
- **A big dark cloth or a bowl** for the bell. Put it in the middle of the table. Nothing goes on it.
- **The Memory ledger.**
- **The lullaby.** All of it, tonight. Stop on the second-to-last note.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **[Her strike](play:s26-stop-the-strike) is her action, on her turn,** standing next to the bell with the Key out of its case. That's all it takes. Tell them so.
- **What stops it:** Grappling her, getting the Key out of her hand, standing between her and the bell, or an offer she'll take.
- **Grapple:** an Unarmed Strike. She makes a Strength or Dexterity save (her choice) against 8 + your Strength modifier + your Proficiency Bonus. While she's Grappled, she can't strike the bell.
- **Knock the Key from her hand:** a Utilize action, **Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Sleight of Hand), DC 17.**
- **Counterspell does nothing** to the strike. It isn't a spell.
- **[Control spells](play:any-geas-dominate-hold):** Geas and Dominate fail while she holds the Key. Hold Monster and Banishment allow a save, and she's got Legendary Resistance.
- **[Teleport](play:any-teleport-and-gates):** works in and out, now they've seen the room. Not within ten feet of a silence-stone.
- **The six Tolls at Ruinous,** for whoever strikes: a **Memory** (a whole year, and everyone met in it), a **Year** (twenty years older, 5 feet less Speed), a **Sense** (gone a year, then dulled for good; sight or hearing only if that player agreed at session zero), a **Name** (everyone in one town forgets them), a **Fear** (something they can't avoid, for good), a **Gift** (a skill or tool proficiency, given away).

### Opening recap (Session 26)

> **SAY:** "A hundred steps. Some of you walked them, and some of you didn't, and you've each got less than you came with or you know someone who has. *(Who paid? Who turned back?)* You've been sitting on the last one. In front of you there's a bell the size of a house, and it's humming, very quietly, the way you'd hum with a child asleep on your shoulder. One of you stands up."

- **Then hand it over:** "Who stands up?" That player leads the first scene.

### Scene 1: The bell in the dark

**Where and why.** They walk across the floor. Nobody's told them not to.

> **SAY:** "It doesn't get any bigger as you walk. It was already as big as it was. The floor's worn smooth in a ring around it, about as wide as a person pacing. Inside the ring there's writing cut in the rock, in a small plain hand. It only says one thing, over and over. *For whoever can't sleep.*"

**Ask each of them:** "Do you touch it?" Anyone who does hears their own mother, or whoever was nearest to one, humming. Same tune. It always was.

**What's really going on.** Somebody cast this, in the pit overhead, for something frightening they had to look after. Nobody knows who. Nobody was hiding it, either. They only wanted it left alone.

**[The one in the glass](play:s26-winter-figure-empty-hands).** On the far side of the bell somebody's standing. It's the player with the Session 11 note.

*If Maudrin's got the Key:*

> **SAY:** "At the foot of the bell stands one of you, older, with open hands and no Key, waiting."

*If the party's got the Key,* keep the figure for Scene 4.

Say to that player, "What do you feel?" Then read them, word for word, what they said in Session 11. **Don't explain any of it.** Nobody else has to see it.

**If they…**
- **Talk to the figure:** it knows everything they know. It says what they'd say. Let the player answer their own questions.
- **Touch it:** warm, this time.
- **Attack it:** there's nobody there.
- **[Cast Dream or speak to the Hush](play:any-dream-and-sending):** one question for one true memory, told out loud. Nothing's lost. *"Yes. I'm tired. Does it matter?"*
- **Look at the ring on the floor:** hundreds of people's shoes, a very long time ago. Round and round, the way you walk with a baby.

**Bring it back.** The tune. Whoever first heard it in Session 1, off the bronze in their hands: tell them it's the same three notes, and then the rest.

**How it ends.** *Maudrin's here:* a small sound, like a case being unlatched. *She isn't:* nothing. It's only them. Go to Scene 4.

### Scene 2: Her hand is shaking

**Where and why.** *If she's got the Key.* She's been here an hour. She hasn't done it.

> **SAY:** "Maudrin stands beside the bell with the Key raised. 'Let me seal it, and the world continues. Take another step, and I will choose what follows.' Her hand is shaking."

**[Ask each of them what they do](play:s26-stop-the-strike) before she does anything.** Go round the table. Nobody rolls Initiative until somebody makes you.

**What's really going on.** If the Key's sealed in the rock, its hum stops, and so does every silence-stone the Court's got. So she won't seal *it.* She'll seal **the stairs,** and carry the Key close to the tune for the rest of her life, and then give it to someone. She's had four hundred years of other people doing it to prove it works. And she's seen the party ring bells, and the sky didn't fall.

**Maudrin Vesk.** Dove-grey, one glove off. *Uses your name. Doesn't raise her voice, even now.* **Wants** somebody else to be right.

*Lines:*
- "I've stood at the top every midwinter since I was twenty. I'd never come down. I used to think it was talking to me."
- "I'll lock the stairs. Not this. If this goes quiet, everyone who's ever trusted me is standing out there with nothing between them and the dark."
- "Tell me what happens. Not what you hope. *What happens.*"
- "I don't want to. I want you to hear me say that. I don't *want* to."
- *(To whoever's been kindest to her:)* "You'd make a good one of me, you know. I'm sorry."

**If they…**
- **[Talk](play:s26-parley-at-the-bell):** Persuasion, **DC 20.** Read: "'I will seal it,' she says, 'and no one will wake it. Don't make me choose what comes after.'" Make her case for her, honestly. Give **Advantage** for a promise that covers the Court's people, for Cassia's name, for her grandmother Ilse's, or for the one question she asked on the Tor, answered. She'll take **a sealed stair and a witness:** one of theirs, with her, for good. She'll also take somebody she trusts holding the Key.
- **Fail the roll:** she's sorry. She lifts it higher. They've still got a round.
- **[Take the Key](play:s26-grapple-the-key):** grab her, or knock it out of her hand (DC 17). Read: "The Key comes away from her glove with a sound like a drawn breath. She does not reach for it." **She stops.** So does everybody. The fight's over.
- **Stand between her and the bell:** she has to move them. She can't bring herself to for a turn.
- **Say nothing, and nobody moves:** she hesitates one heartbeat, and says a name. **Cassia.** Or **Ilse.** Then she looks at them as if they'd said it.
- **[Walk away](play:s26-walk-away):** "Maudrin watches you go up the stairs. 'Thank you,' she says. She means it." Ask outright: **are you finished fighting this?** Only if everyone says yes is it over. If it's a plan, it's a plan.
- **[Help her seal it](play:s26-help-seal),** having all agreed to stop: "You stand with her at the bell while the Hushmen close the stairs behind you." Same question. Ask it twice.
- **[Let her take the Key away](play:s26-maudrin-leaves-with-key):** "The Key goes back in its case. 'Not today,' she says to the bell." They can follow her, or go up and plan.
- **[Kill her](play:s26-kill-maudrin-at-bell):** "The Key falls out of her hand and rings once on the stone." Only on stone. Her Hushmen put their maces down. If she'd already struck, it changes nothing.
- **[She strikes](play:s26-maudrin-strikes):** read it slowly, below. She pays all six, at Ruinous. And **the players,** not her and not you, will answer what's woken. Go to the end of the session.

> **SAY (only if it's struck):** "The Hush cracks. The lullaby falls silent, and the world goes quiet in a way you have never heard before: not the silence of the bells, but the silence of a held breath about to be released."

**If the party's got the Key and she's here:** she's a step behind them. She's got nothing left, and she's weeping. She won't stop them. If somebody goes back and stands beside her, that's the scene.

**If she isn't here:** skip it. Say her name once, if it's right: "There's a place to stand here, worn smooth, and nobody in it."

**Bring it back.** What they told her on the Tor when she asked, "What would you have done?" She remembers it exactly. She repeats it back to whoever said it.

**How it ends.** They hold the Key, or they don't. Everyone's breathing hard. If Corin's coming, it's now.

### Scene 3: Footsteps

**Where and why.** *Only if Corin was refused, or he's an enemy.* He's had the Notes for months and he's never had the Key. **If he's an ally,** skip this; he's sitting on the floor with his palm on the bronze and his eyes shut.

> **SAY:** "Footsteps behind you, running, and a hammer ringing against the wall."

**What's really going on.** He believes there's a chord that puts everything right. He's correct. It needs six bells. **If all six aren't rung and he seats the Key, it cracks.** And it was Corin who worked out what "the whole name" means. *Yours,* he said. He's never once said it about himself.

**Corin Aldeth.** Scorched apron, a hammer, no coat. *Humming, half a note out from the bell.* **Wants** to finish what Wenna started.

*Lines:*
- "It's all right. I know how it goes. Let me just… I know how it goes."
- "Three generations. Three. And you want to *talk* about it?"
- "Wenna only got as far as the foot of the hill. Look. Look how far I've got."

**If they…**
- **[Get in his way](play:s26-corin-races-to-the-bell):** he tries to take the Key off whoever's holding it. He'll hit a person to do it and be appalled.
- **Tell him what it costs:** the whole name. **His.** Nobody would ever know an Aldeth had been here. He stops dead. "Nobody'd… not even that it was *us?*" He sits down.
- **Show him it isn't whole:** strike or hum the rung bells' notes; there's a gap. Performance, or Intelligence (Arcana), **DC 15,** or no roll if somebody in the party's a Ringer who's rung one. He hears it. He's a craftsman.
- **Offer him a part:** if all six are rung, he might be the one. Not tonight; next session. Mark it.
- **[Let him get there](play:s26-corin-cracks-the-lullaby),** with the Key, before six are rung: "He does not strike it. He seats the Key as the Notes describe, and the chord is not whole, and the bell cracks." It's struck. Oum wakes. Go to the end.

**Bring it back.** In Session 18 each of them said their whole name out loud, and he listened to it like a bell. He says one of them now, all of it, and gets it right.

**How it ends.** He's stopped, or it's too late.

### Scene 4: What's on the table

**Where and why.** [They're standing at the bell with the Key.](play:s26-the-choice) Nothing's chasing them. **This is the scene.**

> **SAY:** "The bell is vast and blank, its surface like the face of a sleeping god. You stand before it with the Key in your hands. The lullaby is still there, very faint, asking you to be quiet. Your choice is the only one left in the world."

**The one in the glass,** if they've got the Key: older, the Key in both hands, smiling. "What do you feel?" Read them their Session 11 words. Say nothing else.

**Then put your notes down and say it plainly.** Out of character is fine. One line each, only the ones that are open:

- **The Concord** *(all six bells rung):* "You seat the Key in the bell, and you don't strike it. Everything you've rung sounds at once, and the song's whole. **Somebody becomes part of it.** They give up their name. Nobody who's ever known them will remember them. It has to be somebody who wants to, and they can say no at any moment and lose nothing."
- **The Hush Unbound** *(always open, until it's sealed):* "You strike it. **Whoever strikes pays all six kinds of Toll, at the heaviest there is.** The one who's sleeping wakes up and asks you something, and you'll have to answer."
- **The Second Dawn:** "You lock the Key in the rock, unstruck. It takes one of you a minute, undisturbed, and **it costs nothing.** What you rang stays rung. What you didn't doesn't come back." Read them the ring ledger, bell by bell. *With none rung, say so: it's a stalemate, and nothing's mended.*
- **Wait:** "You can go back up with the Key and think about it. [It'll still be here.](play:s27-postpone) It's a bit louder every day, that's all."

**Giving up isn't on the list.** Don't offer it. If somebody says, "What if we just stopped?", answer truthfully: the Court keeps the Key, and the collectors go back to their rounds.

**What's really going on.** There isn't a right one. If they ask you what you'd do, say: "I don't have to live there."

**If they…**
- **Ask for the exact price:** tell them the kinds and the weight, never the particular thing. (Nobody knows which year.)
- **Want to know what it'd do for somebody they love:** say. This is the moment to be generous with facts.
- **Ask Maudrin,** if she's here: she's honest. "I'd seal the stairs. You know that. But I've never once seen what happens if you don't."
- **Ask Corin:** he's shaking. He tells them the Concord's true.
- **Ask the Hush:** "Whatever you do, do it gently."
- **Argue:** good. Let it run. Keep anyone from being shouted down. "I'd like to hear from" is a useful sentence.
- **Can't agree:** they wait. Say it kindly, and mean it.
- **Someone starts to do it on their own:** stop. Say, "That's everyone's. Is everyone all right with it?" It only happens if they are.

**Bring it back.** Ask each of them what they wrote at session zero about what they'd lost. One line. Then: "Would you get it back?" Tell them the truth, from the ledger.

**How it ends.** **Take a break. Fifteen minutes, away from the table.** Put the kettle on. When they sit down again, go to Scene 5.

### Scene 5: What will you ring

**Where and why.** Back at the bell. Lights down, if you can.

> **SAY:** "Nobody's moved. It's still humming. It's got to the end of the verse, and it's stopped on the note before the last one, the way it does. It's waiting to see."

**Ask once round the table:** "What does your character want to happen? Not the group. You." No answering back. Then: **"And who's holding the Key?"**

**What's really going on.** You're not resolving it tonight. You're making sure they know what they've chosen. The rite, the strike, the minute: that's all next time. *(If there's an hour left and everybody's wide awake, go straight on to Session 27. It's allowed.)*

**If they…**
- **Have chosen:** say it back in one sentence. "So: you'll seat it, and it's *(name)*'s voice." Or "So: you'll strike it, and it's *(name)*'s hand." Get a nod from each of them.
- **Have a Ringer who wants to think:** of course. Ask that player again, privately, before next time.
- **Have [lost someone tonight](play:s26-help-ringer-falls):** "Your friend is on the stone, and Maudrin's hand has stopped." Everybody's does. Let them reach. [Revivify](play:any-pc-dies) has a minute. The dead pay nothing.
- **Were too late:** it's been struck. It isn't a failure. Somebody enormous is opening an eye, and they're the first thing it'll see.

**Bring it back.** Whoever struck the first bell of the campaign: remind them what they said when they picked up the hammer.

**How it ends.** The last note. Don't hum it. Ask: **"What will you ring, and what will it cost you?"** Let each of them answer. Then stop.

### Running the fights (Session 26)

**Nobody has to fight tonight.** If they do, it's four characters at level 11 against something built to stop them, for about four rounds, and **it ends the second the Key leaves her hand.** Say that at the top, so nobody feels they have to grind through a room of people.

**A fair clock for the strike.** She's by the bell already, so be open about it.
- **Her first turn:** she steps back against the bell, and the Key's out. Say: "She could do it now. She doesn't."
- **Her second turn:** she says the name. Her hand comes up.
- **From her third turn,** if nobody's holding her, standing between, or talking her down: she strikes.
- **She prefers not to.** Any real offer buys a turn.

#### The bell, with Ser Haldric

*One Keeper of the Seventh (AC 17, 170 HP; 85 if she came here hurt), five Wardens (AC 16, 82 HP), three Sergeants (AC 15, 52 HP), six Hushmen (AC 12, 32 HP), Ser Haldric (AC 18, 55 HP). Lighter: two fewer Wardens.*

- **Setup:** she's against the bell. Wardens in a half-ring ten feet out from her. Haldric in front. Sergeants at the last step and the pit. Hushmen anywhere; they'd rather be nowhere.
- **What happens:** Wardens block and shove. Haldric calls somebody out by name and fights only them. Sergeants keep the doors. Nobody chases anyone who backs off.
- **The Keeper's turn:** four Hush Bolts (+9, 23 psychic, 150 feet), or a spell: Silence or Hold Person (twice a day each, DC 17), Wall of Force, Dimension Door or Modify Memory (once each). She uses Wall of Force early, between her and whoever's fastest. She keeps Dimension Door to get back to the bell.
- **Off her turn:** Counterspell and Shield (three times a day each). Three Legendary Actions a round: a Hush Bolt, thirty feet of silent movement, or the Toll of Quiet (Wisdom DC 17, 18 psychic and Deafened).
- **Her gloves:** spell attacks made within ten feet of her have Disadvantage. She's got Magic Resistance and three Legendary Resistances.
- **A Warden's turn:** two mauls (+5, 12, pushed ten feet), or two bolts (+4, 13). The stone: no spells within ten feet; AC 12, HP 10, or Sleight of Hand DC 15 to pull it.
- **Who to worry about:** a wizard inside two stones. Tell them where the stones are, every round.
- **Allies who came down:** each named one takes a Sergeant or two Hushmen off the board. Don't roll it.
- **When it ends:** the Key leaves her hand; she takes an offer; she's at 0; or she strikes. Any of those, and everyone stops. She never leaves this room in a fight.
- **Scale on the fly:** easier, the Hushmen are sitting down by round two. Harder, she's on full HP and starts with Wall of Force up.
- **Non-lethal:** 0 HP with a melee hit is Unconscious and Stable, if they say so. They'll say so for her.

#### The bell, without Haldric

*One Keeper, five Wardens, four Sergeants (AC 15, 52 HP), eight Hushmen. Lighter: two fewer Wardens.*

- **Setup and turns:** as above. One Sergeant stands where Haldric would have, and looks embarrassed about it.
- **If Haldric's come with the party:** she stops talking in the middle of a word when she sees him. She loses her first turn. He says whatever he's come to say. **Wait for it.**
- **When it ends:** as above.

#### The bell, and Corin

*One Keeper, four Wardens, two Sergeants, Corin Aldeth (AC 14, 81 HP), two Heart-Hounds (AC 15, 58 HP). Lighter: no hounds.*

- **Setup:** Corin comes off the last step on round one, sixty feet from the bell, with a hound either side.
- **What happens:** it's a race. He's after the Key; she's after time. The Wardens can't tell who to stop.
- **Corin's turn:** three Ringing Strikes (+6, 16 thunder). Counterspell (Constitution save, DC 14). He goes for hands.
- **A Heart-Hound's turn:** two bites (+5, 7). They sit if he says sit.
- **When it ends:** for him, when someone tells him the cost is his own name. For her, when the Key's gone. **If he gets it to the notch with six unrung, it cracks.**
- **Non-lethal:** nobody in the room wants him dead. Not even her.

### If you get stuck (Session 26)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **The song stops for one breath.** Everyone in the room turns round. It starts again.
2. **Tolliver reads the writing out loud,** because nobody's told him not to.
3. **Maudrin asks one of them to hold her glove.**

**If they attack her the moment they see her:** fine. She talks through the first round anyway.

**If a fight's going badly:** she doesn't want anyone dead on this floor. She stops the Wardens herself. "That's *enough.*"

**If they've got a cleverer plan than any of this:** check it against two things. Is the Key struck or unstruck? Does anybody pay who didn't choose to? Then let it work.

**If someone's really upset:** stop, kettle, come back. It'll wait. It's good at it.

**Names for the Court's people down here** (cross one off when you use it): Sergeant Godric, Tolliver, Bettany, Rusk, Jocelyn.

### End of session 26

**The closing question:** "What will you ring, and what will it cost you?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember."

- **What each player felt** when the figure looked at them. Put it next to the Session 11 note.

- **The Ringer,** if there's one, and that they've been asked in private.

**Rewards:**
- **The Key,** if they've got it.
- **The choice.** Nobody's had it for four hundred years.
- **The bargain,** if they made one: a seal and a witness, in her own words.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to whoever changed their mind out loud.
