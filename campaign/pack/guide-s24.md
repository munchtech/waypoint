## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. It's the last conversation with Maudrin on open ground, and probably a fight. Let her be right about something. Then, at the end, somebody has to go first down a staircase, and that's the quiet part.*

### Before you start (Session 24)

**Level:** four characters at level 10. They go up to **level 11** when the summit's settled.

**Check the world state first.** It decides who's standing in the ring.
- **The Key.** With Maudrin: she's **the Keeper of the Seventh,** and she'll fight. With the party: she'll talk. Run Scene 2, and you may never need Scene 4.
- **Maudrin.** Alive, captured or dead? If she's dead or captured, Tristan's here with what's left of the Court.
- **Her Hit Points.** If she was Bloodied at the Strike, she starts on **85** of 170. If they kept the favour, she's on all 170 and has everyone.
- **Ser Haldric.** Still a knight: use the Haldric version. Disgraced: he's here alone, on foot. Bought: he's watching from the second bend. Dead: skip Scene 3.
- **Corin.** Enemy: he turns up. Ally: he's on one of the stones. Refused: he's halfway up the hill, arguing with himself.
- **The resistance.** If they're riding with her, use "For the Court" in the fights.
- **The Forecast.** Which bells are rung, which endings are open. Read it yourself before anyone asks.

**What this session is about.** The truth about a person. They've fought what she did. Tonight they meet why.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Let Maudrin make her whole case, uninterrupted, once.
- Put a real offer in front of them. Let them want it.
- If it's a fight, make it about **the Key and the stairs,** not her Hit Points.
- Tell them what the stairs cost before anyone steps on one.
- End on the question: **"Who goes down the stairs?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **Her Final Offer,** on good paper: for each player, one thing their character's lost (a Toll, a sale, a Tithe). It's all in her vault. She'll give it back.
- **A map:** a ring of nine stones, sixty feet across, with a round hole in the middle and stairs going down.
- **Tokens or minis** for your version.
- **The ally cards,** face up.
- **The grey glove.**
- **A lullaby,** hummed. Pick the tune now. You'll want it next session as well.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Legendary Resistance:** three times, when Maudrin fails a save, she can choose to pass instead. **Tell them when she does it.** It's something they've made her spend. It doesn't work on ability checks.
- **Legendary Actions:** three of them, used one at a time at the end of other people's turns.
- **Magic Resistance:** Advantage on saves against spells.
- **Counterspell:** Constitution save, DC 17. Fail, and the spell's wasted, but they keep the slot.
- **Knocking the case from her hand:** a Utilize action, next to her. Athletics or Sleight of Hand, DC 17. Or Grapple her first, and then Utilize.
- **Taking the case:** Sleight of Hand, DC 17, after she's been hit once this fight.
- **Influence:** she's Hesitant. DC 20 as the Keeper; 18 if she isn't holding the Key. **No roll at all** if they've got the Key and they're offering a seal.
- **The stairs take memories.** Not a save to avoid it. Scene 5 has the exact words.

### Opening recap (Session 24)

> **SAY:** "Maudrin Vesk said good evening to you, and she meant it. *(What you did: fought, stood aside, took the Key.)* You opened nine wagons in the dark, and somebody asked you what you wanted them to do. And now it's morning, or it ought to be. There's a light at the top of the Tor, and you've known what colour it is since you were about four years old. *(Who said they'd go first?)* You're at the bottom of the road."

- **Then hand it over:** "What's the dream? Just the bit you've got left of it." One line each. If somebody's lost their dreams to a bell, they get to say what they see when the others describe it.

### Scene 1: The three bends

**Where and why.** The climb. Fifteen minutes. It's the last calm they'll get for a while.

> **SAY:** "It's an easy road. By the first bend you can see the whole valley. By the second, you can't hear it. By the third the grass is warm, and you're all walking in step."

**What's really going on.** The lullaby's very near the top. It isn't hostile, and it isn't a trap. It just wants everybody to lie down.

**At each bend, give them one thing:**
1. **First bend.** The battlefield, from above. Somebody's putting up a tent for the wounded, and there are people from both sides carrying the poles.
2. **Second bend.** A folded grey glove on the wall. A Hushman's taken it off and left it. Then another. There are eleven by the top.
3. **Third bend.** Linnet. "She's up there. She's just standing there. I think she's waiting for you to get your breath."

**[Their friends come up with them,](play:s24-allies-hold-the-stones)** and each takes a stone:

> **SAY:** "Behind you the stones of the Tor fill with friends."

At **three** on the allies clock, the party picks which side nobody comes from. At **six,** Maudrin's got no reserve: take the Sergeant out of the fight.

**If they…**
- **Haven't got allies:** Linnet goes and stands by a stone. "I'm one," she says.
- **Look for another way up:** the sheep track on the north side comes out under the casting-pit. Perception, DC 13. They can have a head start on next session.
- **Lie down:** it's lovely. They could stay here. A friend shakes them after a minute. No roll.
- **Sneak up:** group Stealth, DC 15, gets two of them behind a stone. She says, without turning round, "Do mind the edge."

**Bring it back.** Each of them says which bend they stopped being afraid at. It's the same one.

**How it ends.** The ring.

### Scene 2: Shall we begin?

**Where and why.** [The last conversation.](play:s24-negotiate) Twenty-five minutes, if they want it. Put the glove on.

> **SAY:** "The stones of the Tor stand in a ring. In the centre, Maudrin Vesk waits with her Hushmen in a silent line. Behind her, the ground opens onto a spiral of stairs. 'I'm glad you came,' she says. 'Shall we begin?'"

(If she's lost the Key, there isn't a line. She's by herself, sitting on a stone. If she's captured or dead, go to [Tristan,](play:s24-remnant) in Scene 4.)

**What's really going on.** If she's got the Key, she means to go down, seal the stairs behind her, and sit with the lullaby until she dies. If they've got it, she's got nothing left to bargain with except being right. She'd take a seal and a witness. **She doesn't want to strike. She'll strike before she lets it be taken.**

**Say that last part out loud,** before anyone reaches for it:

> **SAY (Maudrin):** "Please don't snatch at it. If I think you're going to take it, I'll strike. I don't want to. I'd be frightened, that's all, and I'd do it."

**Then let her make her case.** Read it slowly. Don't let anyone interrupt until she's done.

> **SAY (Maudrin):** "I'm not your enemy. I'm the last person in Corvane who understands what the silence costs. Four centuries of Tithes. Blanks. Children who forget their mothers. And you want to wake the thing that holds it all together? You're mad, or you're brave. I've never been able to tell the difference."

*More lines:* "I was twenty when I took it out of my family's cupboard. My grandmother Ilse was in the doorway. She said, 'Don't drop it.' That was all the instruction there was." · "You think I like the gloves." · "Tell me what you'd have done. I'm asking. I've wanted to ask someone for thirty-eight years."

**If they…**
- **Answer her:** this is the scene. Let it run. Whatever they say, she takes it seriously.
- **Ask for terms: [the Final Offer.](play:s24-deal-with-maudrin)** Hand out the paper.

> **SAY:** "'Everything on the list,' Maudrin says. 'Every jar, every name. I will sign it. And you will walk down the hill and let me seal the stairs.'"

  She means it. If the **whole group** says yes, that's the end of their resistance, and the Empty Choir's next. **Ask the table, not the dice.** Give it a break if it needs one.
- **[Persuade her to stand down:](play:s24-negotiate)** DC 20, or 18 without the Key. She'll come down as a witness. She won't fight. She may cry.
- **Offer a seal, holding the Key:** no roll. "Would you? Would you really?"
- **[Give her the Key:](play:s24-give-maudrin-the-key)** ask twice, whether they're sure. Then:

> **SAY:** "The case is heavy in your hands. Maudrin watches it as one watches a child on a ledge."

  She doesn't strike. She goes down, and they're welcome to follow.
- **Ask who'd sing it, if somebody had to:** she's thought about that.

> **SAY (Maudrin):** "If it must be a name, then I have more than most to give."

  **[She's offering to pay the Great Toll herself.](play:s24-maudrin-as-voice)** It only counts for the Concord, and only with all six bells rung and the Key in the party's hands. They can say no.
- **Ask about the Blanks:** "I know all of their names. I've got them written down. It's the only prayer I know."
- **Insight, DC 15:** she believes every word.
- **Want to just get on with it:** then get on with it.

**Bring it back.** She's noticed each of them's missing something, and what. She doesn't say it to wound. "You've lost the smell of rain, haven't you? I'm sorry. I'd get that one back first."

**How it ends.** Somebody reaches for something, or somebody sits down.

### Scene 3: Her son

**Where and why.** Only if Haldric's here. It's a beat in the middle of the talk, or the middle of the fight. It ends his part in it.

**How would they know?** [The letters,](play:s24-letters-in-the-case) if they've got the Key; or Rook ("Ask him who paid his fees"); or the diary; or Ansel.

> **SAY (the letters):** "Under the Key, folded into the grey lining, lie forty years of letters, none sealed, all addressed to Haldric Mose."

Read out half a line of one, if they open it. *"You came fourth in the tilting today, and you laughed. I was behind the pillar."* They also say why the Key's lived in a lead box: the Court's stones are all tuned to it, and she never dared open the recess.

**If they…**
- **[Tell him:](play:s24-haldric-truth)** with the letters, no roll. Without, Persuasion DC 18, at Disadvantage.

> **SAY:** "Haldric stops polishing his sword. He looks at Maudrin, and she does not deny it, and that is worse than anything she could have said."

  He lowers the sword. He and any Knights of his walk out of the fight. *Lines:* "You sent me a *pony.*" · "I thought it was the Order." · "I'll be down there. I'll be… I'll be just down there."
- **Tell her that they've told him:** she loses her next turn, just for the length of a breath.
- **Give him the letters without reading them:** Heroic Inspiration. He puts them inside his breastplate.
- **[Kill him:](play:s24-kill-haldric)** it's a hard thing to watch. Ask first whether they mean it.

> **SAY:** "Haldric falls on one knee. 'I did not know,' he says, which is not an answer to anything."

  His Knights surrender. If Maudrin sees it, she stops talking for the rest of the night.

**Bring it back.** He's got her way of putting his gloves together. Somebody spots it.

**How it ends.** He's off the board, one way or another.

### Scene 4: The ring of stones

**Where and why.** [The fight,](play:s24-fight-maudrin) if there is one. Pick your version below.

> **SAY:** "'I'm glad you came,' Maudrin says. 'Shall we begin?' Behind her the Hush rises out of the stairway like a tide, so low you feel it in your ribs."

(If you've used that line already, start at "Behind her".)

**What's really going on.** She isn't trying to win. **She's trying to get down the stairs with the Key and shut them.** The Wardens' stones are there to stop anyone casting near her. She goes down when she's Bloodied. If she started Bloodied, she holds for **three rounds** to slow them and goes at the start of the fourth. Say it: "She's moving towards the stairs."

**Three ways to win it:**
1. **Take the Key.** She stops.
2. **Stop her reaching the stairs.** Stand in the way. Grapple her. A wall of Stilled.
3. **Take her alive.**

**If they…**
- **[Get the case:](play:s24-take-the-key)**

> **SAY:** "The case comes away in your hand, heavier than it looks, humming through the silence-stone like a heart through a wall."

  She stops fighting. Her people stop because she has.
- **[Take her alive:](play:s24-capture-maudrin)** say it before the last blow lands.

> **SAY:** "She sits down on the cold stone very carefully, like an old woman at the end of a long day, and holds out her wrists."

  She'll talk now. About anything.
- **[Kill her:](play:s24-kill-maudrin)**

> **SAY:** "The silence-stone gloves fall open. For the first time since you met her, Maudrin Vesk makes a sound: one breath out, very long, like someone setting down something heavy."

- **Let her go: [she walks.](play:s24-maudrin-flees-down)**

> **SAY:** "She turns and goes down, one hand on the wall, and the Hush rises to meet her."

  Write down her Hit Points. She'll be at the bottom with them. There'll be people on the landings.
- **Have an enemy in [Corin:](play:s24-corin-arrives-hostile)** end of round two.

> **SAY:** "Corin comes over the stones with two Heart-Hounds at heel, hammer ringing. 'Give me the Key. You will not do it right.'"

  He's on nobody's side. **Tell him the Concord costs a name, and it'd be his,** and he stops in the middle of a swing. He'd thought it was the bell's. If it comes to it, [he can be killed,](play:s24-kill-corin) and the hammer rings on the stone, once, and goes still.
- **Are for the Court:** [run that version.](play:s24-for-the-court)

> **SAY:** "Maudrin stands behind you, small and grey. 'Let them come.'"

- **Are losing:** one ally does something enormous. The Stilled stand up. Yenna's riders come over the stones. The hymn starts. It opens a way to the stairs, and that's all they need.

**Bring it back.** At the top of each round, hum two bars of the lullaby. Stop before the end of the line. Every time.

**How it ends.** She's down, or gone down, or sitting. It goes very quiet. You can hear it properly now.

### Scene 5: The first step

**Where and why.** The top of the stairs. No hurry. Everyone chooses.

> **SAY:** "It's a hole with steps in it. They're worn in the middle. The sound comes up them, and it's the thing your mother sang, whoever your mother was. Linnet's still on the top one. She shuffles over, so there's room."

**Tell them the price, now, before anyone steps.** Linnet knows, or the Hush does, or Maudrin does. Read it:

> **SAY:** "The first nineteen steps are free. After that it takes one memory for every twenty. You'll be tested once, at the twentieth. If it goes badly, it's five, and if it goes well, it's two. It's only ever once in your life. They're yours to name. It won't take what you can do, only something you had. You can turn round. Nobody will think less of you."

**What's really going on.** It's a staircase that listens. There's another way down: the **casting-pit.** If nobody finds it tonight, let Corin or the Notes mention it next time. Don't hide it.

**If they…**
- **Ask what's a memory worth:** let them set it. Small and sharp's better than big. "The way the stairs creaked."
- **Want to send somebody else:** nobody can be sent. They can be asked.
- **Want to send a child:** no. And nobody asks one.
- **Ask which friends are coming:** each one says. Ilam isn't. Rook would, if they told her to, so they mustn't. Corin is. Save the rest for next time.
- **Are missing a player tonight:** their character waits at the top, and nothing's decided for them.
- **Take a rest first:** yes. A long one, if they've stopped Maudrin. If she's gone down, a short one; she's shutting doors.
- **Have the Key and no idea what they'll do:** good. That's the next two sessions.

**Level up.** Everyone's **level 11.**

**Bring it back.** The gloves on the wall. There's a twelfth one now. It's small, and it's dove-grey, if she's gone down.

**How it ends.** Ask it: **"Who goes down the stairs?"** Let each of them answer, by name. Then shut the book.

### Running the fights (Session 24)

**Maudrin, Keeper of the Seventh** *(AC 17, HP 170; 85 if she left the Strike Bloodied)*
- **Her turn:** four Hush Bolts (+9, 23 Psychic each, up to 150 feet). Swap one for a spell: Silence or Hold Person (twice each, DC 17), Wall of Force or Dimension Door (once each). No words needed.
- **Off-turn:** three Legendary Actions: a Hush Bolt; **Silence Step** (30 feet); **Toll of Quiet** (20 feet round her, Wisdom DC 17, 18 Psychic and Deafened for a round; half on a success). Counterspell or Shield, three times. Within 10 feet of her, spell attacks have Disadvantage.
- **Go gently.** Four bolts at one person will drop them. Spread them, and use her turn to shut a caster down or put up the Wall.
- **The hymn:** Advantage on saves against Frightened. If nobody's been Frightened, let it count once against her Toll of Quiet.

**The rest:** a Warden (AC 16, HP 82; two mauls, +5, 12, pushed 10 feet, or bolts +4, 13; stone AC 12, HP 10, or Sleight of Hand DC 15). Haldric (AC 18, HP 55). A Knight (AC 18, HP 52; two swords, +5, 10 and 4 Radiant). A Sergeant (AC 15, HP 52). A Hushman (AC 12, HP 32; glove, or arrest: Strength DC 12).

#### Maudrin's last stand

*The Keeper, three Wardens, a Sergeant, four Hushmen.*

- **Setup:** Maudrin by the stairs. The Wardens in a triangle round her, ten feet out. The Hushmen in a line across the top of the road.
- **What happens:** Round one: Silence on the biggest caster. Round two: the Wall of Force, across whoever's nearest the stairs. Round three: she walks backwards.
- **Who to worry about:** whoever's alone on the far side of the Wall.
- **When it ends:** she's Bloodied and goes; or the Key's taken; or she's taken.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out a Warden. For a harder one, add one.

#### Maudrin's last stand (with Haldric)

*The Keeper, two Wardens, Haldric, two Knights, four Hushmen.*

- **What happens:** Haldric calls out the best fighter and holds them. The Knights stand on the stairs.
- **When it ends:** as above; and Scene 3 takes Haldric and the Knights out altogether.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out the Knights.

#### Three sides (Corin's an enemy)

*The Keeper, one Warden, four Hushmen; Corin (AC 14, HP 81: three Ringing Strikes, +6, 16 Thunder; Counterspell, Constitution DC 14); two Heart-Hounds (AC 15, HP 58).*

- **What happens:** Corin goes for the Key wherever it is, and the Hounds go for the case. He and Maudrin fight each other as often as not. Let them.
- **When it ends:** for Corin, when somebody tells him the price. For her, as above.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out the Hounds.

#### What's left of the Court

*Only if Maudrin's dead or captured. Tristan (AC 14, HP 38), Haldric, two Knights, seven Wardens, four Sergeants, eight Hushmen. (Leave out Haldric and the Knights if Rook won.)*

> **SAY:** "Tristan Vesk stands at the stones with the Wardens in a silent line. 'She was wrong, and she is not here, and I am.'"

- **What happens:** Wardens on the stairs, shoulder to shoulder. Knights charge. Tristan stays behind a stone.
- **When it ends:** the party **shows the Key,** or Tristan yields. Either's enough. Tell them so in round two.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out three Wardens.

#### For the Court

*Only if they've ended their resistance. Corin, five Heart-Hounds, an Ironhorn Champion (AC 16, HP 112), four Ironhorn Riders (AC 18, HP 65). The party's beside Maudrin.*

- **What happens:** these are people who used to like them. Give each a line as they come over the stones.
- **When it ends:** Corin's Bloodied.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out the Ironhorn.
- **Non-lethal:** encourage it.

### If you get stuck (Session 24)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Maudrin asks for the time.** She hasn't heard a bell in so long that she's forgotten how to guess.
2. **A Hushman takes a glove off,** in the line, where everyone can see.
3. **The lullaby misses a note.** Everybody on the hill feels their stomach drop.

**If the talk goes round in circles:** "I'll ask you once more," says Maudrin, "and then I'm going to go downstairs."

**If they take the Final Offer and one player doesn't want to:** it isn't taken. It needs all of them. Say so kindly.

**Names for Hushmen and knights** (cross one off when you use it): Alder, Dorrit, Ser Roland, Lyle, Zeb, Gilly, Sergeant Vint.
