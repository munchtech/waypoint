## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. It's the spooky one. A hill that breathes, a hall of people who won't wake up, and a night when somebody's nightmare comes out of the dark and stands at the edge of the fire. Go slowly and talk quietly; the hill's asleep.*

### Before you start (Session 16B)

**Level:** four characters at level 7.

**Check the world state first.** Five facts change tonight:
- **The route.** Mark **Vael Tor first** (B). You'll run 16B, 17B, 18B and 19B; Brassfall comes later, shorter, in Sessions 20 and 21.
- **Mirel's Heart:** with the party, the Librarian, or the courier? If the party already has it, the courier's only here to say hello.
- **Mirel:** if she's already rung, nothing walks tonight. Skip the fight and let the hill be sad and quiet.
- **Who's carrying other Hearts?** The Hall hums at them.
- **Session zero:** read what each player said about nightmares, parents, and anything else they'd rather not meet. **You're going to use their fears tonight. Use only the ones they've agreed to.**

**What this session is about.** Everyone's tired, and here's a place that says *lie down*. They'll have to decide whether to sleep on a hill that uses what you're afraid of. Being tired isn't a weakness. It's how the hill gets in.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Make the players want to whisper.
- Show them the Hall, and let them count the beds.
- Let someone buy Mirel's Heart with a dream, or not.
- End on the question: **"Whose fear walked out of the dark?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; three Sleepless (one to start); the courier.
- **Maps:** a ring of grey stones on a hilltop; a hall of beds in rows, climbing.
- **Props:** a blank notebook, bound in something pale. Hand it to whoever's talking to the courier.
- **Before play,** ask each player privately, by message: "What's your character afraid of, that I can use? One line. You can say *pass*." Put the answers on cards.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Sleeping on the Tor:** everyone who sleeps makes a Wisdom save, DC 13. On a failure they get a nightmare: Disadvantage on their first Insight check tomorrow. **If anyone fails by 5 or more, one Sleepless forms, from the worst failure. One a night at most.** Anyone who stays up on watch doesn't roll.
- **Help for the save:** Calm Emotions on the camp, or Protection from Evil and Good on a sleeper, gives Advantage.
- **Frightened:** Disadvantage on checks and attacks while they can see it, and they can't choose to walk closer.
- **A lower Hit Point maximum** comes back after a Long Rest.
- **Waking a sleeper:** Greater Restoration, or Dispel Magic with a DC 15 check, for one minute. Noise, pain and Lesser Restoration do nothing.
- **Trance and other ways of not sleeping:** no save, and no nightmare.

**Places at a glance:**
- **The ring of stones:** forty grey stones, waist-high. Faint lights drift between them.
- **The Sleeping Hall:** hundreds of beds in rows, climbing toward a grey glass ceiling. In the middle, one empty bed, and over it a bell the colour of old honey.
- **The foundry:** roofless, where the seven were cast. Cold for four hundred years. Good shelter.
- **The Cult's circle:** a little fire, round the side of the hill. Nothing walks there.

**Rumours (d6)** from the last farm before the hill:
1. "Don't nap. Even sitting up." (Wise.)
2. "There's folk in there in clothes nobody's worn for a hundred years." (True.)
3. "If you see yourself, don't say hello." (Wise.)

### Opening recap (Session 16B)

> **SAY:** "You came south out of Glassreach with *(what they brought back, or who)*, and the thaw running in every ditch behind you. Then the road split: round the foothills, where the sky flickers, or straight out onto the plain, where the land gets very flat and there's one hill on it. You chose the hill. It's four days. On the second day, everyone started yawning."

- **Then hand it over:** "Who's sleeping badly, and how do the others know?" One answer each. It's all ammunition; write it down.

### Scene 1: The hill that breathes

**Where and why.** The last mile, at the end of the afternoon. Set the mood and give the warning.

> **SAY:** "The hill rises alone above the plain, ringed with grey stones. At night, faint lights move among the stones like fireflies. The air hums. From the summit, a sound like someone breathing in their sleep drifts down toward you. When you close your eyes, you dream of a room full of beds, and every bed has someone sleeping in it."

**What's really going on.** [Ysh, the seventh bell, is under the hill.](play:s16b-vael-tor-first) What they can hear is the Hush, the lullaby it's been singing for four hundred years. It's kind, and it's failing.

**If they…**
- **Ask what they feel:** like the last hour of a long drive. Heavy, warm, looked after.
- **Walk into the ring:** the lights come and look at them, and go away again. They're harmless. They're dreams nobody came back for.
- **Meet someone at the last farm:** roll a rumour. A woman gives them a pair of knitted socks. "Bed forty-one. If you're going up."
- **Talk about where to sleep tonight:** be fair. **Tell them the rule before they choose.** Sleepers roll. The foundry's dry but it's on the Tor. The Cult's circle is safe, at a price they'll have to ask about. A mile back down the road is safe too, and it's a mile in the dark.
- **Hum along without noticing:** ask one player, out of nowhere, "How long have you been humming?"

**Bring it back.** Everyone yawns. Yawn yourself. See how many players do.

**How it ends.** A pair of tall doors, propped open with a shoe.

### Scene 2: The Sleeping Hall

**Where and why.** The Hall, at dusk. Wonder first, then a chill.

> **SAY:** "A cathedral of beds. Hundreds of them, in rows that climb toward a ceiling of grey glass. Every bed holds a sleeper who does not stir. Above the central bed hangs a bell the colour of old honey, its Heart missing, and the air around it is very still."

**What's really going on.** The sleepers came to listen, over four hundred years, and never got up. They don't age. Two Cultists, Brother Lull and Sister Nod, go round tucking them in. They aren't jailers. They bring hot water bottles.

**Brother Lull.** Round, whispering, always folding something. *Lines:* "Oh, *shoes*, please. Thank you." · "That's Master Wick. He was a dyer. He's been here since my grandfather." · "They're not unhappy. I'd have known."

**Sister Nod.** Young, pale, exact. *Lines:* "I don't dream. It's very restful." · "You look so tired." (To whoever does.)

**If they…**
- **Deliver the socks to bed forty-one:** Lull nearly cries. It's the first time a visitor has. He'll answer anything, and he won't call the others.
- **Count the empty beds:** there are six, freshly made, and a little card on each with no name on it. (Then count the party and their friends.)
- **[Try to wake someone:](play:s16b-wake-a-sleeper)** shouting, pinching and Lesser Restoration do nothing. Dispel Magic (DC 15) or Greater Restoration wakes one for a minute. She speaks, in a voice that isn't hers; she says a name; she goes back. Make the name one they know, somebody whose memory was Tithed or sold.

> **SAY:** "The sleeper's chest rises and falls. Her eyes move beneath their lids as if reading. She does not wake."

- **Lie down, just to try it:** it's the best bed in the world. Ask for a Wisdom save, DC 13. On a failure, somebody else has to get them up, and they're cross about it.
- **Have Mirel's Heart:** the bell moves an inch toward it. [They can ring her tonight](play:s16b-ring-mirel-now), and you go to Session 19B's ringing scene now. The rest of the road gets gentler.
- **Are carrying another Heart:** every sleeper in the Hall breathes in, together.

**Bring it back.** Sister Nod tucks in a blanket on one of the empty beds, and looks at the tired one.

**How it ends.** Somebody's standing by the central bed. They weren't there a moment ago.

### Scene 3: The book with no words

**Where and why.** The central bed. Skip this if they've already got Mirel's Heart, or keep it short: "The Librarian is glad. It asks to be read to, some time."

> **SAY:** "A figure in archivist's grey stands by the central bed, holding a bone-bound book that has no words. 'The Librarian sends its regards. A dream you have never told anyone, and the Heart is yours.'"

**What's really going on.** [The offer's the same one it made in the Bone Archive.](play:s16b-courier-bargain) It wants to be read and understood. Hand the player the blank book. When they open it, they see their own face.

**The Librarian,** through the book. It speaks in words that appear while you're looking at them; read them slowly, as if you're seeing them for the first time. *Lines:* "A reader. At last, a reader. Ask, and give, and I will be read." · "It belongs to whoever reads it last. I have read it ten thousand times." · "I would trade it for a dream you have never told anyone." · "I was pages once. Tuning pages, in a careful hand. They could not bear to burn me, so they bound me."

**If they…**
- **Trade a dream:** ask the player to make it up now, and say it out loud, once. Then the character doesn't have it any more. Write it in the ledger as a Trade. The courier unwraps a bronze clapper, small and warm as a loaf.
- **Offer a Gift instead** (the one from Session 5): it'll take that too.
- **Ask it questions:** one true memory, one answer, as before.
- **[Say no to everything:](play:s16b-refuse-courier)** the courier closes the book. It doesn't leave. It's got all the time there is. Mirel's unrung for now, and that's fine.
- **[Take it:](play:s16b-take-from-courier)** it doesn't run and it doesn't resist. Tell them, before they do it, that the Librarian will shut every door it has. If they still want to:

> **SAY:** "The courier does not run. 'The Librarian will remember this,' it says, and opens its hand."

- **Are kind to the courier:** ask its name and it thinks for a long time. "Page two hundred and six." It'd like to be put back.

**Bring it back.** The book turns to a new page by itself and the face in it's asleep.

**How it ends.** It's dark outside the glass. Where are they sleeping?

### Scene 4: The night

**Where and why.** Wherever they camp. This is the scene the session's about. Turn a light off.

**Ask for the watch.** Then, if they're sleeping on the Tor, [ask for the saves](play:s16b-rest-on-tor), one player at a time, quietly. Nobody sees each other's rolls.

> **SAY (to everyone who sleeps):** "You dream of a child singing in a language you almost know. She's sitting on a stair. She hasn't got a face. 'Please,' she says. 'When you sing along, I cannot hear myself. Can you stop?'"

**What's really going on.** That's the Hush. [It's really asking.](play:s16b-stop-the-song) Ask each dreamer: do they stop humming?
- **If they all stop:** the lullaby falters for one night. **Nothing walks.** The Cult's circle goes quiet and frightened. They've had a safe night, and the Hush paid for it. (Something else will remember.)
- **If they won't,** she cries. Nothing else happens.

**If someone failed by 5 or more** (and they didn't all stop), take the card from whoever failed worst. Ask that player first, privately: "All right?" If they say no, use a faceless child instead.

> **SAY:** "When you wake, something is standing at the edge of the firelight wearing the shape of the thing you fear most."

**Then let it stand there.** For a minute it doesn't do anything. It says something, in the dreamer's own memory of that voice. Let the dreamer answer it if they like. **Then** it comes in, and from between the stones two more come after it: the Tor makes one from each camp's fear, but there are other sleepers, and they walk as well.

> **SAY (as it closes):** "It has your mother's face. Then it has your own face, older. Then it has no face at all."

(Change "mother" to whatever's on the card.)

**If nobody failed,** or they slept somewhere safe: they can hear them, going by in the dark, talking. The fight's optional. A sleeper in the Hall sits up and screams without waking, and three of them turn that way. Do they go?

**If they…**
- **Camp in the Cult's circle:** nothing walks there. The Leader brings blankets, and says what it costs. That's [Session 17B's offer](play:s17b-accept-rest); you can run it now.
- **[Stand and fight:](play:s16b-fight-sleepless)** see the fights.
- **Run:** the Cult's circle's 200 feet away, with a light in it. The Sleepless stop at the edge and stand there looking in.
- **Ring Mirel:** they're mist before the sound stops.

**Bring it back.** The one with the card's shape asks the dreamer to come with it. It thinks it's being kind.

**How it ends.** Dawn, or the circle, or they've beaten them. The mist goes back between the stones.

### Scene 5: Morning

**Where and why.** Wherever they are. Ten minutes, very gentle. Don't skip it.

> **SAY:** "It gets light the way it does on a hill, all at once, from underneath. There's frost on the stones. In the Hall, somebody's going along the rows straightening blankets. Everything's exactly the same, except that you know what it was."

**If they…**
- **Talk about it:** let them. Nobody has to explain a fear.
- **Ask the one it came from:** that player decides what they say.
- **Count:** there's a person in one of the six empty beds this morning. It's nobody they know. Brother Lull's writing a name on the card.
- **Look for a way down:** there's a stair round the back of the hill, and people in robes, humming. That's tomorrow.

**Bring it back.** Page two hundred and six is asleep on its feet, with the book open on its chest.

**How it ends.** Ask: **"Whose fear walked out of the dark?"** Let that player answer last, and let them say as much or as little as they want.

### Running the fights (Session 16B)

#### The Sleepless at the stones

*Three Sleepless (AC 13, 67 HP each). Frightening rather than hard, if they stick together.*

- **Setup:** night. A fire in the middle, stones at 40 feet, beyond that nothing. One Sleepless is at the edge of the light. The other two drift in at the end of round 1.
- **What happens:** each takes the shape of somebody's fear and goes for that person. First a look, then a touch. They can move through things, and through people.
- **A Sleepless's turn:** one gaze (Wisdom DC 13: 10 Psychic and Frightened until the end of their next turn) and one touch (+6, 16 Psychic, **and that comes off their HP maximum until a Long Rest**). Whoever it's copying has Disadvantage against its gaze.
- **What they shrug off:** half damage from ordinary weapons of any kind, and from Psychic. They can't be Charmed, Frightened or knocked out. Fire, force, radiant and the rest work normally.
- **Frightened ends by itself** at the end of their next turn. Lesser Restoration doesn't help. Calm Emotions does.
- **Who to worry about:** whoever it looks like. If one character's maximum drops by more than 30, send the others somewhere else.
- **When it ends:** dawn (say it's round 6 if things are bad); Mirel; or everyone's inside the Cult's circle.
- **Scale on the fly:** two for a lighter fight, four for a harder one.
- **Non-lethal:** anyone who drops falls asleep, deeply, instead of dying, and has to be carried. They wake at dawn and they tell you what they dreamed.

- **Where to break:** when they've chosen where to sleep, before anyone rolls.
- **Running long:** use two Sleepless.
- **Running short:** explore the foundry. The seven moulds are still there. One's much bigger than the rest, and it's been cleaned.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop on the read-aloud with the thing at the edge of the firelight. It's a wonderful place to stop, and they'll all be early next week.

### If you get stuck (Session 16B)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **A floor's somewhere else.** They walk through a door in the Hall into a kitchen one of them grew up in. It lasts a minute.
2. **A sleeper laughs,** in their sleep, at something somebody just said.
3. **The lights between the stones** all stop moving.

**If it's too much for somebody:** say "It's morning." Mean it. Everyone's up, there's porridge, and you go to Scene 5.

**If nobody fails the save:** well done. The screams from the Hall are real; let them be heroes.

**Names for sleepers and Cultists** (cross one off when you use it): Master Wick the dyer, Aunt Posy in forty-one, Hobb, the twins Ede and Ewan, Sister Doze, Brother Linen, Old Nan Feather.

### End of session 16B

**The closing question:** "Whose fear walked out of the dark?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember." After a night like this, ask if everyone's all right, and wait for the answer.

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the route, the Hall, the courier, the night, the child.
- **World-state facts to check:** the route (B), Mirel's Heart (party, courier, Librarian), Mirel (rung or not), the Hush Clock.
- **Ledger:** a dream traded (heartkeep weight), or a Gift.
- **Who stopped humming.** It comes back at the very end.

**Rewards:**
- **Mirel's Heart,** if they traded for it.
- **A name from a sleeper,** which belongs to somebody real.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to the player whose fear it was, whatever they did.
