## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. It's a hundred steps and not much else, and it's the one people talk about afterwards. Go slowly. The memories they give up are the best writing anyone will do all campaign, and it won't be you that does it.*

### Before you start (Session 25)

**Level:** four characters at level 11.

**Check the world state first.**
- **Where's Maudrin?** Gone down with the Key: her sealers are on the landing. Coming as a witness: she's beside them. Captured, dead or exiled: nobody's sealed anything.
- **The Key.** If the party's got it, they can show it on the landing and the sealers stand aside.
- **Mirel.** Unrung: Sleepless on the stairs. Rung: it's quiet, and the Hush sings.
- **The Memory ledger.** Has anyone walked part of this stair before (Session 18B)? **They pay only what's left.** If they made their save then, it stands.
- **The Hush Cult.** If it's ceased, somebody will hum for them.
- **Who said they'd go down,** last time. And **who isn't at the table.** An absent player's character never pays. They wait above.

**What this session is about.** What you'd give up, and what you'd keep. There's no right number.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Say the price again, all of it, at the first step.
- Ask for each memory out loud or on paper. The player picks. Nobody hurries them.
- Let friends say no. It's a good thing when they do.
- Get whoever's going to the bottom, whole or lighter.
- End on the question: **"What did the stairs take from you?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **A strip of paper numbered 1 to 100** down the middle of the table. Move a token along it.
- **Index cards and one pen each.** A card for every memory. They keep it, or they give it to you face down. Ask which.
- **The Memory ledger,** open.
- **The casting-pit.** Know where it is. Somebody will want it.
- **Tokens or minis,** only if you need them: six Wardens, four Sergeants, three Knights and twelve Hushmen; or seven Sleepless.
- **The lullaby.** Hum it at steps 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100. Each time, get one note further.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **[The price of the stairs:](play:any-stairs-price)** the first nineteen steps are free. After that, **one memory for every full twenty.** The first time somebody reaches step 20, they make **one Wisdom save, DC 13.** On a failure their total for the whole campaign stops at **five;** on a success, at **two.** It happens once in a lifetime. Coming back up is free.
- **What a memory is:** something that happened to the character. Never a class feature, a spell, a skill, or anything the player knows. Nobody loses control of their character.
- **It isn't a Toll.** No ending gives it back.
- **The dead pay nothing,** and nor do children, because children don't go.
- **Climbing:** Strength (Athletics). Falling's 1d6 Bludgeoning for every 10 feet, and you land Prone.
- **Teleport and Dimension Door** work only if they've seen the chamber (Scrying, a dream, or standing in it). Otherwise they can't picture it, and nothing happens.

### Scene 1: The top step

**Where and why.** Daylight, just. Who's coming, who isn't, and what it costs.

> **SAY:** "The stairs are warm, as if someone had just stood on them."

**Read the price again, all of it:**

> **SAY:** "Nineteen steps, free. Then one memory for every twenty. At the twentieth there's one save, and only one, ever. If it goes against you, you'll lose five, all told. If it goes your way, two. You choose them. They don't come back, not with any ending. You can turn round on any step. Coming up costs nothing."

**What's really going on.** It isn't a punishment. The Seventh's what keeps the Dreamer asleep, and it's made of what people give it. The stairs are where it's fed.

**Then the friends.** [Let each one answer for themselves.](play:s25-allies-refuse) Go round, quickly.

> **SAY (Ilam):** "Ilam Bray looks down the stairs for a long moment, then at Jonah. 'I've only just got him back,' he says. 'I can't lose him again.'"

- **Ilam:** no.
- **Rook:** "We'll hold the top. That's an order I can give."
- **Corin:** yes. He won't say why he's shaking.
- **Kessa:** "I've a wife's laugh and a horse's name, and I'm keeping both. Shout, and I'll hear."
- **Yenna:** yes, if her father's lamp can stay with Linnet.
- **The Stilled:** they can't bend their knees.
- **Linnet:** "I could. If you want." Let a player answer her.

**If they…**
- **[Turn back:](play:s25-turn-back)** they can. No penalty.

> **SAY:** "The first step is warm, as if someone had just stood on it."

- **[Find the other way:](play:s25-foundry-chute)** Corin or the Notes know. So does anyone who's looked under the north stone.

> **SAY:** "At the top of the Tor, under the ring of stones, the old casting-pit drops straight down into the dark. Warm air rises out of it, smelling of bronze."

  Three climbs, Athletics DC 15. A slip's a fall, usually 20 feet: 2d6. **It never stops them.** It costs no memories and you mustn't add another price. Flying counts too. They can split up: some by the stairs, some by the pit. They all get there.
- **[Try to teleport:](play:s25-teleport-to-the-shrine)** if they've seen it.

> **SAY:** "One blink and the cold of the shrine is on your face."

- **Want to send a child, or be told it's fine:** no, and no.
- **Want to make somebody else pay for them:** it doesn't work like that. It knows whose foot it is.
- **Have all chosen the pit:** lovely. Run Scenes 2 and 3 as voices through the wall, skip the landing, and spend the time on Scene 5.

**Bring it back.** Someone asks what the card said. (The memory they'd never give up.) They don't have to answer.

**How it ends.** The first foot goes down.

### Scene 2: Twenty

**Where and why.** [The stair.](play:s25-descend) Move the token. Let the first nineteen go by in a minute.

> **SAY:** "The stairs go on for a long time. Each step has a memory carved into it, worn smooth by the feet of those who came to listen. Some are your memories. Some are strangers'. You hear a child laughing, a bell tolling, a man telling a joke to someone who isn't there."

**Ask, going round:** "You hear one voice you know. Who is it?"

**At step twenty, stop.** Everyone who's walking, and hasn't before, makes the save: **Wisdom, DC 13.** Tell each of them their number: two, or five.

**Then the first memory.** Here's how. Do it the same way every time.
1. Hum the lullaby. One more note than last time.
2. "It's asking. What do you give it?"
3. They say it, or write it. **Small's fine.** "The smell of my father's coat." "Which foot I put first on the ice."
4. Say it back, and then: "It's gone. You know you had something. You don't know what it was."
5. Write **Stairs** in the ledger, with the step.

**What's really going on.** It wants things with love on them. It doesn't mind how big. Somebody who gives it something they're ashamed of will find it takes that just as gladly. Let them. It's allowed to be a relief.

**If they…**
- **Can't think of one:** give them three words to pick from. *A door, a song, a winter.*
- **Want to keep it private:** face down. They can choose to share one at the end.
- **Give it something cruel that was done to them:** it's theirs. Ask if they're sure, once.
- **Try to give it nothing:** then they can't go any further down. The step's just a step. Nobody's cross.
- **Lie:** it takes the thing the lie was covering.
- **Fly, ten feet above the steps:** that's a real way round it. It costs nothing.

**Bring it back.** Whatever they gave their first bell, whoever's a Ringer. Somebody's voice on the stairs has it. Only for a second.

**How it ends.** They go on. Step forty's the same again, for everyone. **Anyone on two is finished now,** and can say so.

### Scene 3: Forty-one

**Where and why.** One step past forty. It's where they look at what's under their feet.

> **SAY:** "On the forty-first step is the face of a woman you have never met, laughing at a joke."

**What's really going on.** [Some of these weren't given here.](play:s25-read-the-steps) They were Tithed or sold, and they've come to rest. Study, Intelligence (Investigation), DC 13: whose. **This is Jonah's mother.** It's the face he can't remember. (If they met Mara Bray on the steps in Hallowmere with an empty frame in her lap, it's her, laughing. If she's home already, it's still her.)

**If they…**
- **Work out who she is:** they can't take her away. They can look as hard as they like, and tell him. One of them can draw. It matters in the epilogue.
- **Look for their own:** whatever they've paid this stair is carved on the step that took it. They can look at it, like a stranger's picture on a wall. Nothing comes back.
- **Look for the Blanks' names:** there are four hundred on the wall, very small. Someone's been copying them out, by hand, in pencil. It's Maudrin's writing.
- **Have Maudrin with them:** she stops on forty-one. "That one was me," she says. "I signed for it." She waits to be shouted at.
- **Want to smash it:** it's only stone. Smashing it doesn't send it home. Say so first.

**Bring it back.** The joke. The man on step nineteen who was telling one. *She's who he's telling it to.* Give them the punchline now, if you can think of one. Keep it bad.

**How it ends.** A light below, and somebody's breath.

### Scene 4: The landing

**Where and why.** Step fifty: a round room, a ledge, a hole in the middle. Which of these is it?

**The Court's sealers: if Maudrin went down.** [They're on the landing.](play:s25-sealers-on-the-stairs)

> **SAY:** "The stairs end in a wall of grey gloves and silence-stone."

**Sergeant Minna Saul.** Late forties. She keeps touching her ring. *Lines:* "We're to ask you to go back. That's all it is. We're to *ask.*" · "I've lost three on this stair. One of them was his name. I've got the ring, look. It's got an M in it." · "Please don't make me do this part."

- **[Show the Key](play:s25-sealers-on-the-stairs)** (if the party's got it), or Maudrin in custody: they stand aside, and hold a lamp for them.
- **Talk:** Persuasion DC 15 gets one of them past to argue with Maudrin, or gets the twelve Hushmen to sit down. Give her her husband's name (it's in the Ledger; or Ansel knows: it's **Emmot**) and no roll. She weeps, and opens the line.
- **Push through:** see the fights. They won't follow below the landing.

**The Sleepless: if Mirel never rang.** [On every step.](play:s25-sleepless-on-the-stairs)

> **SAY:** "On every step stands someone's fear, patient as a statue."

- **Walk between them:** group Stealth, DC 14, or a slow walk with your eyes shut and one hand on the wall. Let them do it. One person hears their own fear say something sensible.
- **The Cult hums:** if it's ceased, the Leader and two others come down as far as here and hum. They fold up like washing.
- **Fight:** see below.

**Neither: if it's quiet.** The Hush is here, and nothing else is.

> **SAY (the Hush, in each person's own voice):** "Are you coming to hear it, or are you coming to stop it?"

Let each of them answer. It isn't a test. "That's all right," it says, to anything.

**What's really going on.** The people on this landing are all scared of the same thing, and it isn't the party.

**If they…**
- **Came by the pit:** they come out on the far side of the landing, behind everyone, and may walk past.
- **Knock someone into the hole:** it's forty feet to the next turn of the stairs. 4d6. They're all right. They've got a step to pay for, and they know it.

**Bring it back.** One of the Hushmen's the one who took his glove off on the second bend. He's put it back on, and it's inside out.

**How it ends.** Down. Sixty, eighty, a hundred. **Hum it each time.** Anyone who failed the save goes on paying: one at each.

### Scene 5: The last step

**Where and why.** [The bottom.](play:s25-reach-the-bottom) They've arrived. Don't start the next bit.

> **SAY:** "The last step is wider than the others, and at the bottom a bell the size of a house hangs in the dark."

**Let it be big.** Ask: "How far away do you stop?"

**What's really going on.** It's Ysh, the Seventh. It isn't ringing; it's *humming.* It's been humming to one thing for four hundred years, and every few minutes it drops a note. When it does, everyone's stomach goes, and far underneath, something turns over.

**Then take stock.** Let them sit on the last step. Everybody looks at their cards. If they want to, each one reads out **one.** If they came by the pit, they're sitting next to people who didn't. Let that conversation happen.

**If they…**
- **Ask what they've forgotten:** they can't know. Their friends can. "You used to whistle." It's a good few minutes.
- **Want to look at the bell:** there's a notch in it the shape of the Key. The metal's blank. There's writing cut in the rock underneath it, in a small plain hand. *For whoever can't sleep.*
- **Want to ring it now:** next time. It takes what it takes, and everyone should've slept on it.
- **Hear somebody else breathing:** if Maudrin's down here, she's on the far side of the bell. She hasn't lit a lamp. She says, "Take your time."
- **Want a rest:** a short one. Nobody can sleep here. Everyone's being sung to.

**Bring it back.** The card from the opening. The one they'd never give up. Tell each of them: it's still there. Have them hold it for a second.

**How it ends.** Ask: **"What did the stairs take from you?"** Round the table. Nobody has to. Then stop.

### Running the fights (Session 25)

**Ten feet wide, no rail, a drop in the middle.** That's the fight. Only two people can stand side by side. Pushing matters more than damage.

#### The Court's sealers

*Six Wardens (AC 16, 82 HP), four Sergeants (AC 15, 52 HP), three Knights (AC 18, 52 HP), twelve Hushmen (AC 12, 32 HP). They only need to say no.*

- **Setup:** Knights in front, shoulder to shoulder. Wardens behind, with their stones out: nobody's casting within ten feet. Hushmen round the ledge.
- **What happens:** they hold. They don't come forward. They don't chase anyone below the landing. They'll arrest anybody who's fallen over.
- **A Warden's turn:** two mauls (+5, 12, pushed 10 feet), or two bolts (+4, 13). Stone: AC 12, HP 10, or Sleight of Hand DC 15.
- **A Knight's turn:** two swords (+5, 10 and 4 Radiant each).
- **A Sergeant's turn:** two blows (+4; about 5, and 3 Necrotic), or one and a signal (a d4 for an ally).
- **A Hushman's turn:** a glove (+5, 5 and 2 Necrotic), or an arrest (Strength DC 12 or Grappled; DC 12 to get out).
- **Use a few at a time.** Two Knights and two Wardens can reach. The rest are waiting. Don't roll for people who can't get there.
- **When it ends:** three of the front rank down; the Key's shown; Maudrin's a captive; or someone gives Minna her husband's name.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out two Wardens. Or count every three Hushmen as a wall you can climb over.
- **Non-lethal:** suggest it. They're very scared.

#### The Sleepless on the stairs

*Seven Sleepless (AC 13, HP 67). Only if Mirel never rang. Resist weapon damage. Can't be Charmed, Frightened, knocked over or knocked out.*

- **Setup:** one on each step from fifty to fifty-six. They don't move until somebody's next to them.
- **What happens:** each takes the shape of one person's fear. Ask the player. It doesn't have to be large.
- **A Sleepless's turn:** a gaze (30 feet, Wisdom DC 13; 10 Psychic and Frightened for a round), and a touch (+6, 16 Psychic, and that much off their maximum until a long rest).
- **Who to worry about:** anyone who's Frightened and can't go *down.* They're stuck. Somebody has to come back for them.
- **When it ends:** the Cult hums; it's dawn outside; or they're past the last one.
- **Scale on the fly:** five of them. Four, if two people went by the pit.
- **Non-lethal:** they aren't alive. Someone might thank theirs, and it goes.

- **Running long:** no fight. Show the Key, say the name, or walk past with eyes shut.
- **Running short:** more voices. Each player makes one up for somebody else's character to hear.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop on the landing. Nobody likes it, but it's a place.

### If you get stuck (Session 25)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **The song drops a note.** Everyone grabs the wall.
2. **A step has the party on it.** Last month. Round a fire, laughing.
3. **Somebody behind them on the stairs** is counting under their breath. There's no one there.

**If it's too much for somebody:** the pit, always. It's not a lesser way down. Or a break, and a biscuit.

**If it's turning into a competition:** it's private cards from here, face down. No reading out.

**If nobody pays anything:** they all flew, or they all climbed. That's a clever party. Let them have it.

**Names for Hushmen and voices on the steps** (cross one off when you use it): Pru, Kester, Isaac, Mabel, Wilf, Sergeant Minna Saul, Swithin.
