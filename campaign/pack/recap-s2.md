### Opening recap (Session 2)

Start with a recap before anything else, even before the cart if Ila was taken. It gets everyone back to the Old Shore. If someone took the Chronicler job, ask them to read their lines first, then fill the gaps in this shape. Keep it under two minutes.

> **SAY:** "Last time, you came to Pellmoor Landing, and on the oldest dock a girl named Ila showed you a bronze Heart that hums. Mother Sallow gave you bitter tea, a hymn and a bargain: keep the Heart from the Court for three days. Hushman Quill came for his quota, and *(he left with what he wanted / he left suspicious / you paid him / you fought him)*. *(If anyone gave or sold a memory: say what it was, in the player's own words from the Memory ledger.)* That night the Mere sang, and every word was a name. *(If the raid took Ila: and when the song stopped, Ila was gone.)*"

- **Name what was lost, plainly.** A memory given, a friend taken: one sentence each, then move on. Don't soften it, and don't dwell on it.
- **Then hand it over.** End on one player: "Where is your character when the fog comes in?" (If Ila was taken, ask instead: "Who's awake first?" and go to the cart.)
- **In Waypoint:** Foresight's **Last we left off** drafts this from the last session's record and any heavy moments you marked. Read it, fix it, say it in your own words.
