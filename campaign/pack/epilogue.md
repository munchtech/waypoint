### Epilogue round (Session 30)

After the epilogue for the chosen path, and before the closing question, go round the table once. Long-running tables end this way: each player gets their character's last scene, and laughter and tears come quickly one after the other. Let both happen.

1. **Ask each player in turn:** "Where is your character a year from now? What are they doing the first time they hear a bell?" Let them say as much or as little as they like, and don't correct anything.
2. **Give each one image back.** Look at their roster notes from session zero (what they lost) and their lines in the Memory ledger. Narrate one small thing the world returns or remembers for them: a face, a song, a name read aloud.
3. **Read the Tolls aloud.** One line for each Toll paid in the campaign, in the order they were paid, in the players' own words from the Memory ledger. Then pause.
4. **Then the closing question:** "What will you ring next?"

**In Waypoint:** the [Memory ledger](go:memory-ledger) (Campaign → Party & preferences) lists every Toll in order, and the roster keeps the session-zero answers.
