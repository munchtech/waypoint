### Places at a glance (Session 3)

- **The chapel** (Scene 2). *Light:* cold and lamp-lit; it smells of lamp oil and ink. *Footing:* nine plain stools in a row; a grey cloth over a bell-frame that has never held a bell. *Sound:* a man praying the same line over and over. *Exits:* the front door, and a side door to Eld's two rooms (a desk, a narrow bed, Court paper squared off neatly). *Cover:* the stools and the bell-frame.
- **The ferry-stair** (Scene 6). *Light:* grey water, darker as it goes. *Footing:* an old stone stair from the first ferry landing, 10 feet wide underwater (two can stand side by side), running down a drowned street to Ossa's belfry, 40 feet down. *Sound:* the Choir. *Exits:* back up; the Singers never follow anyone out of the water. *Cover:* the drowned doorways along the street. Ila stays at the top.
- **The *Gilded Heron*** (only if the Heart was sold). *Light:* night, with deck lamps that catch a shimmer on the water. *Footing:* the gangplank, the deck and the cabin, where the Heart is locked in a chest. *Sound:* the mate sleeps near the cabin (Passive Perception 14). *Exits:* the gangplank, or overboard; the guards would rather throw someone in than kill them. *Cover:* cargo and the cabin hatch.

### Rumours on the Old Shore (Session 3, d6)

Five are true. The one marked *false* is wishful thinking.

1. Brother Eld writes a lot of letters on grey Court paper, for a man whose chapel has no bell.
2. The Court has been asking about children again: whose they are, and how old.
3. Venn's *Gilded Heron* sails in three days, and he wants something aboard before she does.
4. A Court rider in grey is on the Mere road with a sealed letter, asking for "the ones who carried it."
5. Old Abel Tarn rowed the eastern-marsh ferry longer than anyone remembers. Some say he's rowing it still.
6. Ring a drowned bell and the Mere gives back all its dead, walking. (*False:* a bell gives rest, and the Ringer pays a Toll.)
