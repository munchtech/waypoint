## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. Tonight they wake someone who has been frozen for four hundred years, and something in the glass shows one of them a picture of the end of the campaign. There may be a big, frightening fight. There may be none. It depends on how gentle they are.*

### Before you start (Session 11)

**Level:** four characters at level 5.

**Check the world state first.** Four facts change tonight:
- **Yara's map:** do they have it? It gives advantage on Stealth here and Survival everywhere in the glass.
- **Did they fight the wolves last session?** If two Stilled woke then, the ambush has two fewer sleepers in it, and those two remember who cracked them.
- **Corran Vell:** woken gently, he's a friend. Left in the glass, he's one of the Stilled in tonight's ambush: give him his sea-coat and his badge.
- **Do the Stilled trust them?** Wary, if anyone used fire or thunder. Wary Stilled are calmed at DC 15 all the same, but none of them comes closer than ten feet afterwards.

**What this session is about.** The glass holds more than people. It holds moments, and one of them has a character in it who hasn't happened yet. Past that is Maren Talla, and Wyn, who has waited her whole life. Past *that* is a wood full of sleepers, and the wolves are in it.

**Your goals tonight:**
- The winter-moment is a seed for Act V. Ask one question, write down the answer word for word, and don't explain.
- Waking Maren is the main scene. Make it slow. Give it a full half-hour if they let you.
- The ambush is a tragedy. The Stilled are terrified people. Show the players they can talk.
- Never push the fight harder than written.
- End on: "What did you see in the glass?"

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Wyn, Maren, Yara; five Stilled; five Glass-Wolves.
- **Maps:** a clearing about 80 feet across with five Stilled standing in it, and trees all round.
- **Props:**
  - *A blank card and a pen* for the winter-moment answer. Keep it with your notes until Session 26, when you read it back.
  - *The cold tracker* from last session.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Extreme Cold** is still running: Constitution DC 10 each hour, or 1 Exhaustion. A cloak succeeds automatically. Anyone who gives up their cloak to warm the glass makes the save.
- **Waking someone gently:** an hour of warmth (two cloaks and a fire, or a held flame), or glass-cutting tools with Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 14. No harm done. They wake confused and Indifferent.
- **Breaking the glass:** they wake in pain and attack whoever is closest, and the noise cracks the glass of others.
- **Calming a Stilled:** one action, Influence DC 15, in Hesk or old Common. Calming removes it from the fight. Calm Emotions works on a group. The Tongues spell lets a caster answer in their own dialect.
- **Sneaking past:** group Dexterity (Stealth) DC 13. Everyone rolls; if at least half succeed, all do. Yara's map gives advantage; Pass without Trace adds 10.
- **What magic can't do:** Dispel Magic doesn't end a winter-moment. Speak with Dead only works on the dead. Detect Thoughts finds the last thing they were thinking.
- **A shattered Stilled is dead.** Say so plainly the first time it's about to happen.

**Places at a glance:**
- **The mirror:** a sheet of glass as tall as a door, standing alone between two trees.
- **Maren's tree:** three trees past the clearing. A bench Wyn's great-grandfather built. A hundred years of candle stubs.
- **The sleepers' wood:** between the first frozen stream and the second. Five Stilled you can see; more you can't.
- **The second stream:** frozen mid-splash. On the other side is the chapel.

**Rumours (d6), from Wyn, Yara or Maren:**
1. "The glass shows you things." (True.)
2. "The wolves were a huntsman's dogs." (Near enough. They were a pack the Sexton fed.)
3. "Say a sleeper's name and it hears you." (True. Advantage on calming anyone whose name they know.)
4. "The bell will wake all of them." (True.)
5. "The bell will kill all of them." (False. The bell ends the winter. What they do next is theirs.)
6. "The chapel glass is cracking on its own." (True. Nobody knows why.)

### Opening recap (Session 11)

> **SAY:** "Last time, you walked into a forest of glass. Yara sold you *(say what)*. A child landed, and asked you if it was still winter. *(Say what they told her.)* An old woman asked you to be gentle. And at dusk you heard a man preaching to nobody. This morning, Wyn Talla is standing at the edge of the camp with a basket of candles. 'It's three trees past the clearing,' she says. 'I'll show you.'"

- **Then hand it over:** "What was the last thing you thought of before you fell asleep?" One sentence each.

### Scene 1: The winter-moment

**Where and why.** On the way to Maren, between two trees. The glass shows one character something.

Choose who sees it before you start: the character whose story is most tied to the bells, or whoever looks first. If nobody looks, the glass shows it to whoever keeps watch that night.

> **SAY:** "In a sheet of glass as tall as a door you see yourself. Older. Grey at the temples. Holding a small dark key in both hands, and smiling."

**What's really going on.** [It's a moment from the end of the campaign.](play:s11-winter-moment) You'll read their answer back to them at the foot of the Seventh Bell. Don't explain it, and don't hint. It isn't a promise and it isn't a threat.

**Then ask that player:** **"What does your character feel?"** Write their answer down, word for word, on the card. That's all.

**If they…**
- **Ask what it is:** "You don't know. Nobody here does." Yara has never seen it do that. Wyn draws the Ninefold's circle on her chest.
- **Touch it:** cold. The older face's breath fogs the other side.
- **Break it:** every shard shows the same thing. Read that slowly.
- **Cast something:** Detect Magic shows nothing. It isn't a spell.
- **The others look:** they see themselves as they are now.

**Bring it back.** Later tonight, when it's quiet, ask that player: "Do you tell anyone what you felt?"

**How it ends.** They walk on. It's still there behind them, smiling.

### Scene 2: Maren

**Where and why.** Three trees past the clearing. Wyn's bench, the candle stubs, and a woman in the glass with one hand held out.

> **SAY:** "She's about thirty. She's wearing a shawl, and she's half turned, with one hand held out behind her as if somebody small was meant to take it. There's a bench in front of her, worn smooth, and around its feet a hundred years of candle stubs. Wyn lights one more and puts it down with the others. 'This is her,' she says. 'This is the sister.'"

**What's really going on.** Maren was running from the cold with her little brother. He got to the treeline and she didn't. Wyn is his descendant. The family has come every day since.

**Wyn.** *Lines:* "I talk to her about the weather. It's a joke. The weather's the same." · "What if she's cross?" · "I'm not going to cry till she's looking."

**If they…**
- **[Wake her slowly:](play:s11-wake-gently)** an hour of warmth, or tools at DC 14. Anyone who gives up a cloak makes their cold save. Use the hour: ask each player one quiet question. Who would wait four hundred years for you? Then read:

> **SAY:** "You wrap the glass in two cloaks and wait. It takes an hour. Water runs down the inside like tears, and then Maren Talla takes her first breath in four hundred and ten years."

- **Her first words:** "Did Pell get home?" Pell was her brother. Let Wyn answer. Wyn kneels in the ash: "He got home. He had six children. I'm what's left."
- **The song:** Wyn sings the family's thaw song, four notes falling and one rising. Maren knows it. She taught it to Pell the week before. She sings the second verse, which the family lost three hundred years ago.
- **Ask her about the Sexton:** "His chapel's past the second frozen stream. He sang to us. He has a beautiful voice. But don't argue. He can't bear to be argued with. That's when the glass cracks."
- **Ask what year it was for her:** "Two. The second winter since the bells stopped. They told us it would pass."
- **How long does she stay awake?** Wyn's candles and a fire keep her warm. She stays, as long as someone is with her. She tires easily and keeps touching things.
- **[Crack her awake](play:s11-crack-adult)** by accident or in a hurry: she comes out swinging, screaming Pell's name. Wyn steps in front of her and sings. It stops at once. But the sound has cracked something further off; go to Scene 3 with one Stilled already awake.
- **[Use magic:](play:s11-magic-on-stilled)** Detect Thoughts finds *run, Pell, run.* The Tongues spell lets a caster speak to her in her own words, and she'll trust them first.
- **Won't spend the time:** Wyn sits down by the glass with her own two blankets. "Go on. I've got all day." The party decides whether to leave her there.

**Bring it back.** Maren holds out her hand behind her, the way it was in the glass. She doesn't know she's doing it. Wyn takes it.

**How it ends.** One of the Stilled is free. They know where the chapel is, and one thing not to do there.

### Scene 3: The sleepers' wood

**Where and why.** Between the two frozen streams. It's the only way to the chapel, and there are people all through it.

> **SAY:** "Past the first stream the trees stand closer, and between them stand the people. A man with a bucket. Two girls holding hands. A priest, mid-stride, with his mouth open. Somebody has hung a ribbon on every one of them, a long time ago. The ribbons have frozen too."

**What's really going on.** The wolves are in the trees on the far side. They hunt by sound. Anything loud cracks the glass, and the Stilled closest to it wakes in pain.

**If they…**
- **[Slip past:](play:s11-sneak-past)** group Stealth DC 13. Yara's map gives advantage. On a success they cross without a sound, and the wolves never know (which gives them surprise at the Spire). On a failure, a wolf howls and one single Stilled wakes. Describe it as confused first and angry second, and let them choose.

> **SAY (success):** "You move between the frozen people like guests at a sleeping house. A wolf lifts its glass head somewhere in the trees, listens, and lowers it again."

- **Make a noise, or crack any glass:** run the [Stilled Ambush](play:s11-crack-adult). Read the box, then roll Initiative.

> **SAY:** "A crack runs up the glass like frost on a window. Inside, an eye moves. Then a hand. The man inside is screaming, and you cannot hear it until the glass breaks."

- **[Talk to the ones who wake:](play:s11-ambush-calm)** an action, Influence DC 15. If they ask a name first, or know it from a ribbon, roll with advantage. Read:

> **SAY:** "The man in the glass is shouting at you with his mouth wide open and no sound. When it breaks, he is already speaking: 'The river! The river is cold!' You say his name."

- **[Go for the wolves instead:](play:s11-hunt-wolves)** every wolf that bursts near a Stilled cracks it. Yara: "Not that one. Not near them." Hold out three rounds and her whistle drives the pack off.
- **[Shatter one:](play:s11-shatter-stilled)** stop, and say it out loud: "If you do that, this person dies. Do you want to?" If they do, read the box. The others remember.

> **SAY (if they do):** "The glass comes apart in a sound like a chandelier falling. What was inside does not get up."

- **Use Speak with Dead** on one that's been shattered: five questions. It remembers the first night, and a man in a tower singing so nobody would be afraid.
- **Read the ribbons:** there are names on them, in Wyn's family's hand. Whoever reads a name aloud has advantage on calming that person.

**The ones who wake,** if you need a name and a last moment:
- **Joss Arrand,** with the bucket: "The river! The river is cold!"
- **Ana and Bree Coll,** holding hands: if one is calmed, so is the other.
- **Brother Ewin,** the priest: he was telling people to go inside. He still is.
- **Corran Vell,** if they left him: "All hands! All hands!"

**Bring it back.** Someone who was calmed looks at their own hands for a while, and then asks the nearest character, very politely, what they're supposed to do now.

**How it ends.** They're across. Any calmed Stilled stand behind the party and won't fight, and follow Wyn (or Maren) back to camp.

### Scene 4: The second stream

**Where and why.** The far side of the wood, at dusk. The chapel is on the other bank.

> **SAY:** "The second stream is frozen in the middle of a splash, a crown of drops standing in the air. On the far bank the trees lean together into an arch, and through the arch there is a light, very pale, like a candle behind a curtain of ice. The voice is close now. You can hear the words."

**What's really going on.** They'll cross tomorrow. Tonight, let it be close.

**If they…**
- **Listen:** "*...and there is no shame in it, children, there was never any shame in it...*" He's been saying that sentence for an hour.
- **Cross now:** they can. Then start Session 12 here, and end tonight on the bank.
- **Look behind them:** Yara is counting ribbons. "Ninety-four," she says. "*(Subtract whoever they woke.)* Ninety-*(three)*."

**How it ends.** Ask the question: **"What did you see in the glass?"** Start with the player who saw the winter-moment, and let them say as much or as little as they like. Then ask the others.

### Running the fights (Session 11)

#### The Stilled Ambush

*Five Stilled and five Glass-Wolves. They don't all arrive at once.*
- **Setup:** mark where every Stilled stands before the first one wakes. Two wake in round 1 and attack the nearest creature. One more wakes each round after, up to five. The wolves come out of the trees in round 2 and go for anyone Prone or alone.
- **What they do:** the Stilled claw whoever is closest. They're slow (Speed 20) and hard to hurt: AC 15, 67 HP. Fire drops their AC to 10 until the end of their next turn, and hurts them. The wolves bite and knock people over.
- **Show the way out early.** In round 1, describe a Stilled's face: not angry, terrified. If nobody tries talking by round 2, Maren or Wyn calls out a name and one of them stops.
- **When it ends:** every Stilled who is calmed is out of the fight. The wolves flee when half of them have shattered. If the party pulls back sixty feet, it stops: the Stilled stand shaking where they woke.
- **Cold:** call for a save at the end of it if an hour has passed.
- **Scale on the fly:** three characters, three Stilled and three wolves. If two Stilled woke last session, use three here. Don't add more to make it harder.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** a downed character is left where they fell. The Stilled stop attacking anyone who isn't moving. Maren (if she's awake) stands in front of the next one and says its name.

### Pacing (Session 11)

| Time | What's happening |
|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:15 | Recap; the last thing you thought of |
| 0:15 to 0:40 | Scene 1: the winter-moment |
| 0:40 to 1:40 | Scene 2: Maren |
| 1:40 to 3:00 | Scene 3: the sleepers' wood (and the fight, or the sneaking) |
| 3:00 to 3:30 | Scene 4: the second stream, and the question |

- **Where to break:** after Wyn takes Maren's hand.
- **Running long:** if Maren takes more than an hour, skip the wolves. Let them sneak through at advantage and end on the bank.
- **Running short:** let them wake a second person, by a ribbon they choose. Or cross the stream.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop after Maren's song.

### If you get stuck (Session 11)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Wyn asks them something simple.** "Hold this candle?" "Are your hands warm?"
2. **A wolf howls, far off.** Then, a little nearer, another.
3. **Read a ribbon.** Give it a name and one word about who they were.

**Names on the ribbons** (cross one off when you use it): Joss Arrand, Ana Coll, Bree Coll, Brother Ewin, Tilda Marsh, Old Hask, a tinker known only as Uncle.

### End of session 11

**The closing question:** "What did you see in the glass?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the winter-moment (paste in the player's exact words), Maren, and how the wood went.
- **World-state facts to check:** is Maren awake, how many Stilled they calmed, how many were shattered, how many wolves are left, do the Stilled trust them.
- **Keep the card.** You'll need it in Session 26.

**Rewards:**
- **Maren's warning:** one free chance, in the chapel, to stop arguing before the glass cracks.
- **The calmed Stilled:** the first to trust the party in Session 15.
- **Fewer wolves at the Spire,** if they shattered several here; or surprise at the Spire, if the pack never saw them.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** for the player who answered the winter-moment question, and for anyone who spent an action talking when they could have hit.
