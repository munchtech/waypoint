## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. Tonight is a crowd, a line of Hushmen, and a family that doesn't know it's a family. The fight is optional; the reunion is what matters. A child is in danger tonight, so check your session-zero list before you start: Jonah is never harmed on-screen.*

### Before you start (Session 7)

**Level:** four characters at level 4.

**Check the world state first.** Four facts in Foresight:
- **The oath-duel:** if Rook won, she can send knights to stand between the line and the crowd (once). If Haldric won, the Dawn Order stands with the Hushmen.
- **The Tithe Clock:** at 6 or more, the sweep has started and every road has a checkpoint. Getting anyone out tonight is harder.
- **The export column:** if they found it in Session 5, someone may want to tell the crowd. That's [a play with a cost](play:s07-tell-export).
- **What the Court knows of the party:** named enemies are recognised in the crowd by the Sergeant.

**What this session is about.** Hundreds of Blanks, people the Tithe has emptied, surround the Hall of Tithes asking who they were. The Hushmen form a line at dusk to clear them. In the crowd is a nine-year-old boy, Jonah, holding a wooden bird he can't explain. On the line is the Hushman who carved it, Ilam Bray, who can't remember his son's name. The party can calm the crowd, fight the line, or reunite a family, and then they have to hide it.

**Your goals tonight:**
- The crowd feels like people, not a mob. Give a few of them names and objects.
- Ilam's moment of recognition gets room. It's the quiet scene; protect it.
- The players decide where the family can be safe.
- End on: "Where can this family be safe?"

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Jonah; Ilam (in Hushman grey); Sergeant Orla Gaunt and her line; a crowd (coins or beads); Bet; Captain Rook (if she won).
- **Maps:** the Hall of Tithes steps: a wide stair, the great doors at the top, the square below with the crowd, and the Hushmen forming up across one side with two prisoner carts behind.
- **Props:**
  - *A wooden bird:* anything small and carved, or a folded paper bird. Hand it to whichever player first kneels to talk to Jonah.
  - *The Hushman roster* (Ilam gives it, if they reunite): a list of names and patrol routes.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Calming the crowd.** Influence DC 16 for the whole crowd; Help gives Advantage. Calm Emotions or Thaumaturgy can also give Advantage. If it fails, a second try is DC 18, with Advantage if the speaker says sorry.
- **Restoring a Blank.** Lesser Restoration and Remove Curse do nothing: a Tithed memory isn't a condition or a curse. Only the owner opening their jar restores it.
- **The Silence spell is not silence-stone.** Spells work outside its sphere.
- **Frightened, Grappled:** as before. Hushmen grapple and pass prisoners back to the carts.
- **Child peril:** Jonah is threatened, never hurt. If anything would harm him, it happens off-screen or doesn't happen.
- **The Tithe Clock:** hiding someone in the city overnight is +1.

### Opening recap (Session 7)

- **If Wren's confession hit a character hard, say so.** One line, plainly.
- **Then hand it over:** "You can hear the crowd from three streets away. What do you do?"

### Scene 1: The Blanks at the Hall of Tithes

**Where and why.** The Hall of Tithes, late afternoon, an hour before dusk. Read the crowd box from the plan above.

**What's really going on.** The Hushmen sweep the steps at dusk. If nobody takes a side, the Blanks are arrested and the children among them are taken into custody. The Court is watching whose faces stand with the crowd.

**The crowd.** Give three faces: an old man with a drawing of a woman he can't name; a girl holding a shoe too small for her; a woman who keeps asking, "Was I a mother?" (That's Mara.)

**If they…**
- **[Calm the crowd:](play:s07-calm-crowd)** Influence DC 16, with Help. The Blanks trust them, and expect them to keep their promise. If it [fails](play:s07-calm-crowd-fails), the second try is DC 18.
- **[Use spells:](play:s07-spells-crowd)** Calm Emotions (Charisma save, unwilling only), Suggestion on Gaunt (her Wisdom save is +2). It wins a night, and the caster is marked.
- **[Tell them about the export trade:](play:s07-tell-export)** the crowd boils. Calming it after that is DC 18.
- **[Fight the line:](play:s07-fight-the-line)** see the Hushmen line below.
- **[Read the roster at them](play:s07-read-the-roster)** (after Scene 2): naming the Hushmen out loud, Intimidation or Persuasion DC 16 with Advantage. The line wavers.
- **[Smuggle a handful of Blanks out:](play:s07-smuggle-blanks-out)** Group Stealth DC 15 at the gate; each night +1 on the Tithe Clock.

**Bring it back.** The Hall's great doors open and a clerk reads a list of names in a flat voice. Nobody in the crowd answers to any of them.

**How it ends.** The line steps forward, or holds. Either way, at the end of it a Hushman has stopped dead, staring at a boy.

### Scene 2: Jonah and Ilam

**Where and why.** The edge of the line, nearest the carts. Jonah has wandered to the front with his bird. Ilam has seen it.

Read Ilam's box from the plan above, if the party is close enough to hear.

**What's really going on.** Ilam carved the bird for his son. He was Tithed of the boy's name; Mara was Tithed of the boy himself. Jonah doesn't know either of them. No magic fixes this tonight; all the party can do is get them in the same place.

**Jonah.** Sweet, very literal, repeats what others say. *Lines:* "It's a bird. I don't know where I got it." · "Are you a Hushman? You're crying." · "He said 'my son.' Who's his son?"

**Ilam.** Was warm once; now hollow. *Lines:* "That's my carving." · "I don't know his name. I've tried." · "Please. I need to see him."

**This is a heavy moment.** Let the players carry it. Your lines get short. If one of them kneels by Jonah, give them the wooden bird prop.

**If they…**
- **[Reunite them:](play:s07-reunite)** no check if Jonah trusts the party. Stealth DC 13 to get Ilam out of the line before Gaunt notices. Ilam deserts, and gives them the service-passage key and the roster.
- **[Find Mara too:](play:s07-find-mara)** she's in the crowd. They're a family on paper; one jar from a family in fact.
- **[Attack Ilam:](play:s07-attack-ilam)** the crowd turns on the party. [Killing him](play:s07-kill-ilam) puts Jonah in custody (off-screen).
- **[Leave Jonah:](play:s07-leave-jonah)** he's taken into Court custody. He'll be back, as a hostage, in Session 23.

**Bring it back.** Gaunt's whistle, two short blasts: "Bray! Back in line!"

**How it ends.** Ilam steps out of the line, or doesn't.

### Scene 3: A roof for the night

**Where and why.** After dusk, wherever they ran to. A father who's deserted the Court, a boy who doesn't know him, perhaps a mother who doesn't know either of them.

**If they…**
- **[Hide Jonah](play:s07-hide-jonah)** (and the family): Bet's cellar, Rook's hall, Aurelie's mission, or the party's own room. One night in the city: +1 on the Tithe Clock.
- **Plan to get the jar:** Ilam's key opens the vault's service passage, through the old works under the palace. That's [the Tithe-works](play:s08-tithe-works), Session 8's big dungeon, or [a quick second vault visit](play:s09-second-vault-visit) in Session 9.

**Let them talk.** This scene is the family in a cellar, and the party deciding what they owe them. Give Jonah one line to each character: a question only a child would ask.

**Bring it back.** Upstairs, Bet starts the ovens for the morning. The smell of bread comes down the stairs, and Ilam closes his eyes.

**How it ends.** Ask the closing question.

### Running the fights (Session 7)

#### The Hushmen line at the Hall of Tithes

*Sergeant Orla Gaunt, a second Sergeant and a line of Hushmen (see the encounter). Only if the party escalates.*
- **Setup:** the Hushmen shoulder to shoulder across the square, carts behind. Gaunt stays behind the line with a hand crossbow.
- **Each round:** Pack Tactics, grappling anyone who interferes, passing prisoners back to the carts. Silence-stone gloves silence only their own strikes.
- **When it ends:** Gaunt withdraws when half her line is down, when the party holds the Hall's doors, or when the crowd sits down.
- **Scale on the fly:** lighter: Gaunt and six Hushmen (the plan's lighter line is four and a Sergeant). Three characters: Gaunt and six.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** surrender means a night in the cells. Blanks in the way are Commoners (4 HP): if a fight would hurt them, the crowd moves first.

| 1:15 to 2:15 | Scene 2: Jonah and Ilam |
| 2:15 to 3:15 | Scene 3: a roof for the night |
| 3:15 to 3:30 | The closing question |

- **Where to break:** after Ilam steps out of the line (or doesn't), before they run. Never mid-fight.
- **Running long:** end on the reunion and ask the closing question there. Scene 3 opens Session 8.
- **Running short:** let Mara find them in the cellar, and let a player decide whether to tell her.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop when Ilam sees the bird.

### If you get stuck (Session 7)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Jonah asks a question.** "Are you my mum?" "Why can't I remember?" Somebody answers.
2. **The line steps forward.** One pace. Then ask, "What do you do?"
3. **A Blank asks for a promise.** "Will you help us?" If they say yes, they've promised.

**Names in the crowd** (cross one off when you use it): Hob Fennick, old Mother Ware, Cass Tolley, Lem Ashby, a girl called Pip who doesn't know her last name, Della Hooper.

### End of session 7

**The closing question:** "Where can this family be safe?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember from tonight."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the crowd play, the Jonah and Ilam play, and where they hid the family.
- **World-state facts to check:** Jonah Bray, Ilam Bray, the Blanks, what the Court knows of the party, the Tithe Clock.
- **Bargain log:** any promise made to the Blanks.

**Rewards:**
- **Level:** none. They stay level 4.
- **If they reunited them:** the vault's service-passage key and the Hushman roster, and Ilam as an ally.
- **If they calmed the crowd:** the Blanks will march for them later (the Strike). They remember faces.
- **Coin:** none tonight, unless they search the line's prisoner carts (a strongbox of 80 gp in Court silver, the night's "fees").
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to whoever knelt down to Jonah first, and to anyone who stood between the line and the crowd.
