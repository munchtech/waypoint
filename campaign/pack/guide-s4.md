## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. At the table, read the boxed text aloud and glance at the bullets. The rules from the Act I guides still apply. Tonight's new rules are the courtesy memory (a Tithe, which can be bought back, not a Toll) and the Tithe Clock, which starts ticking the first night they sleep in Hallowmere.*

### Before you start (Session 4)

**Level:** four characters at level 3. No level-up tonight. Level 4 comes after the oath-duel (Session 6).

**Check the world state first.** Open Foresight and look at five facts. They decide which scenes run:
- **Ossa's Heart:** with the party (Maudrin bargains for it in Scene 3), with Ila (she asks where it is), at the Ossa Belfry (rung or returned: no bargain for it), with the Court (she offers an exemption for Ila instead), or sold to Venn (she knows, and asks what they were paid).
- **Pellmoor:** "Under Court pressure" means they stayed to defend it. Run Scene 4 first; the city waits for next session.
- **Writ of passage:** the Court invitation or a true writ gets them through the gate with a nod; a forged writ or none means Sergeant Cole looks closely.
- **Brother Eld:** forgiven (an informant who meets their eyes), exposed and fled to Hallowmere (a frightened Court chaplain at the masque), or neither (use an unnamed chaplain).
- **Sister Aurelie:** if she's a devoted ally, she wants to come in with them (Scene 1).

**What this session is about.** The party walks into the Quiet Court's own city. The gate takes a small memory from each of them, politely. At a masque in the white palace they meet the woman who runs the Silence, and she is kind, patient and reasonable. Then she makes them an offer. Whatever they answer, they leave knowing they're watched.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Hallowmere feels quiet, courteous and wrong. Nobody is rude to them. That's the threat.
- Each player decides about the courtesy memory for their own character. You never choose it.
- Maudrin speaks to every character in turn. Give each one a moment alone with her.
- The players choose how to answer her bargain. Every answer is written up; don't steer.
- End on: "Who in this city can you trust?"

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Clerk Oswin Tell; Sergeant Mabry Cole and two Hushmen; Lady Isolde Drave; Ser Haldric Mose and four palace guards; Maudrin; Brother Eld (if he's here); Sister Aurelie (if she came). For Scene 4: Tobias Quill, a Hushman Sergeant and two Hushmen.
- **Maps:** the Gate Office is a long counter, a ledger, a bench and a door into lamp-lit lanes. The masque is one great white hall with a gallery, a side door Maudrin uses and a terrace outside. You rarely need a grid tonight.
- **Props:**
  - *Grey paper masks* for the players at the masque, if your table likes that sort of thing.
  - *The Memory Ledger* in Waypoint, open on the Tithe page for the courtesy memories.
  - *A silence-stone:* any smooth grey stone. Put it on the table when a Court officer stands close: no spells within 10 feet of it.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **The courtesy memory is a Tithe, not a Toll.** It's small, the player picks it and writes one line, and it goes into a labelled jar in the Tithe-Ledger Room. The owner can get it back by opening that jar (Session 5). Tolls, from bells, never come back.
- **Silence-stone.** A Court stone (on gloves, shields and walls) has a 10-foot field. No spell can be cast inside it or reach into it. Outside a field every spell hums audibly for 30 feet, and the Court notices hums.
- **Influence:** Court officers start Indifferent. The DC is 15, or the person's Intelligence if it's higher (Maudrin's is 17). Friendly gives Advantage; Hostile gives Disadvantage.
- **The Tithe Clock.** Each night they sleep inside a Court city adds one tick. At 6 the Court sweeps the city and puts a checkpoint on every road. Keep it in Foresight.
- **Counterspell:** Maudrin can cast it three times a day. She uses it only to stop harm, never to show off.

### Opening recap (Session 4)

Start with a recap: the Chronicler's lines first if you have one, then this shape. Keep it under two minutes.

> **SAY:** "Last time, you found the page torn from Mother Sallow's ledger in Brother Eld's desk, with Ila's name on it, last. *(Say what they did about Eld, in one line.)* You learned how Tam Harrow really died. *(Say what they chose for Ossa's Heart, and if anyone paid a Toll, say what, in the player's own words.)* And a letter came from Hallowmere, sealed in grey wax, inviting you to the Quiet Assembly."

- **Name what was lost, plainly.** Then stop.
- **Then hand it over:** "Ten days later, the road climbs out of the marshes. Who's walking in front?"
- **In Waypoint:** Foresight's **Last we left off** drafts this from Session 3's record.

### Scene 1: The Gate Office

**Where and why.** Hallowmere's south gate, at midday. Read the arrival box from the plan above, then this.

> **SAY:** "Inside the gate there's a long counter, and behind it a clerk with ink on his fingers and nothing behind his eyes. He opens a book. 'Name? Purpose? Oh, you're expected at the Assembly.' He frowns at the page. 'I have no record of that. But the Court's record is never wrong, is it? Just a small memory, then, for the register. Your first sweetheart's name, perhaps. You won't miss it.'"

**What's really going on.** Every visitor gives a courtesy memory. It's a registration: from now on any Hushman can look up who they are and what they gave. Sergeant Cole watches from the door. If Quill sent a report from Pellmoor, Cole already has their names.

**Clerk Oswin Tell.** Kind, blank, a little frightened. *Wants:* the line to keep moving. *Lines:* "It doesn't hurt. Nobody says it hurts." · "Gold? What would I do with that?" (He stares at a coin like a pebble.) · "Someone must." 

**Sergeant Cole.** Clipped, bored, sharp-eyed. *Wants:* no trouble at his gate. *Lines:* "Hands where I can see them." · "Writ." · "Refusing is your right. So is a night in the Belfry."

**If they…**
- **[Give the memory:](play:s04-give-courtesy-memory)** each player chooses a small memory and writes one line in the Memory Ledger, as a Tithe. No check. The clerk touches a tuning fork to a grey vial by their temple. Tell them where it goes: a labelled jar, which they can open again one day.
- **[Refuse:](play:s04-refuse-courtesy-memory)** Cole steps in. Influence DC 15 to be let through as a refuser; or run (Athletics or Acrobatics DC 13), or lie (Deception DC 15). Failure: one day in Vaer's Belfry, and the Dawn Order hears of it. Refusers are remembered.
- **[Bring Stormborn friends in](play:s04-stormborn-at-gate)** or **[charm their way past](play:s04-charm-the-gate):** see the plays. Every spell hums, and Cole writes down whoever cast it.
- **[Bring Aurelie in without a writ:](play:s04-bring-aurelie)** Influence DC 15 on Cole, or Stealth DC 15. If they fail, [she's arrested](play:s04-aurelie-caught) (gently; the Court likes a bargaining chip).
- **Start a fight:** see the gate Hushmen below. Nobody here wants a death.

**Bring it back.** Cole raps his silence-stone glove on the counter and the sound dies in the air. "Next."

**How it ends.** They're in the lanes, with an invitation for tonight, or they're in the Belfry until morning (then go to Scene 2 the next evening).

### Scene 2: The masque

**Where and why.** The white palace, that evening. Guests wear grey masks. Nobody laughs aloud; they smile instead.

> **SAY:** "The hall is white stone and white candles, and a hundred guests in grey masks move through it without a sound louder than a glass set down. A woman in a grey lace mask detaches herself from the crowd and comes straight to you, a warm cup in her hand. 'You're the ones from the Mere. Oh, sit with me. Tell me everything. Is it true the water sings?'"

**What's really going on.** Lady Isolde is collecting them for the Chancellor, and for herself. Ser Haldric stands near the side door with four palace guards. Brother Eld is here if he fled. Maudrin arrives late and speaks to each character alone.

**Lady Isolde.** Flirtatious, cutting, half-truths beautifully told. *Wants:* to know what they carry and who they love. *Lines:* "Everyone here has lost something. The trick is choosing what." · "You're cleverer than they said. I hate that." · "Haldric? A darling. He'll do anything he's told."

**Maudrin, arriving.** Read the "first meeting" box from the plan above. Then go round the table: she speaks to each character by name, about one thing she has read in their record (their courtesy memory, a refusal, their homeland). Let each player answer. She answers any question truthfully and leaves out the most important part.

**What they can learn here:**
- **Haldric takes her orders.** Free, by watching. Insight DC 15 to see that he is afraid of her.
- **[Eld, if he's here:](play:s04-eld-at-assembly)** forgiven, he whispers that Haldric keeps the city's Hearts in the Dawn Order armory. Exposed, he runs to Haldric.
- **[Reading Maudrin:](play:s04-read-maudrin)** her gloves are silence-stone, so Detect Thoughts fails within 10 feet of her. Insight DC 15 shows she fears something she carries.
- **[If Ossa's Heart is home again:](play:s04-assembly-after-ossa)** she makes no bargain. Insight DC 15: she's relieved it isn't in her city.

**Bring it back.** A servant taps a glass with a silence-stone spoon. It makes no sound, and the whole room turns anyway. The Chancellor has arrived.

**How it ends.** Maudrin asks them to walk with her on the terrace.

### Scene 3: Maudrin's bargain

**Where and why.** The palace terrace, under a grey coin of a moon. Haldric stays at the door, out of earshot.

> **SAY:** "'You carried something out of Pellmoor,' she says, and says each of your names, gently. 'I'm offering you safe passage, a Court pension, and an exemption: one memory of your choosing that no Tithe will ever take. In exchange, the Heart. That's all. I'd like us to be friends.'"

**What's really going on.** She wants every Heart out of reach. If they accept, she still means to keep the Key forever. The favor she asks will come due in Act IV (Session 23). If they refuse, she's polite, and they're followed from now on.

**Maudrin.** Measured, warm, uses their names constantly. *Lines:* "I've never lied to you. I hope you'll extend me the same courtesy." · "If this world cannot be kept, someone must decide what replaces it." · "Then the Assembly will go on without you. Enjoy the city."

**If they…**
- **[Accept:](play:s04-bargain-accept)** she's delighted. Haldric takes the Heart to the Dawn Order armory, beside Vaer's. Influence DC 17 to haggle a better term. They owe her one favor (Bargain Log).
- **[Refuse:](play:s04-bargain-refuse)** her smile doesn't move. Hushmen follow them for the rest of the Act (Group Stealth DC 15 to shake them for a day). Isolde is curious.
- **[Tell her where it is](play:s04-tell-where)** (if it's elsewhere): Deception against her Insight (+4); a lie the Court's records contradict fails. If it's with Ila, [Ila is arrested](play:s04-tell-where-ila) unless they [warn her tonight](play:s04-warn-ila).
- **If she already holds it:** she offers [an exemption for Ila](play:s04-exemption-for-ila), or [Ila's release](play:s04-exemption-releases-ila) if Ila was arrested. They can also [ask for Ossa's Heart back](play:s04-ask-ossa-back) (Influence DC 17).
- **[Threaten her:](play:s04-threaten-maudrin)** she laughs. "Haldric, please see to our guests." See Haldric's escort below.
- **[Mention a key:](play:s04-mention-key)** her face goes still for half a second (Insight DC 15: fear, not anger). "I have no idea what you mean." The Assembly ends early, and they're the Court's enemies.
- **[Skip the Assembly](play:s04-skip-assembly)** or **[go to Rook instead](play:s04-go-to-rook):** no bargain, no favor; Rook or Wren brings the investigation to them.

**Bring it back.** Maudrin says the quietest player's character's name, warmly, and waits for an answer.

**How it ends.** They leave the palace. A Hushman is under the nearest lamp, not hiding. Ask the closing question.

### Scene 4: Pellmoor under siege (only if they stayed)

**Where and why.** If they kept the Heart to defend Pellmoor, run this instead of the city tonight. Tobias Quill has a deadline and torches.

> **SAY:** "Quill's voice carries across the Old Shore. 'By order of the Court: the Heart by dawn, or we search every house.' Behind him, a line of torches. Behind you, a village packing what it can carry."

**If they…** [run the siege](play:s04-pellmoor-siege): negotiate (Influence DC 15 on Quill), [evacuate the Old Shore](play:s04-evacuate-old-shore) with Brannagh's pilots (Group Stealth DC 16 past the cordon), or [hold it](play:s04-siege-holds) against the patrol below. The Sergeant carries grey-wax orders naming the Hallowmere vault: that's the road to Session 5.

**Bring it back.** A torch gutters, and a dog starts barking two houses down. The search has started.

**How it ends.** Pellmoor stands, empty or changed. Start Session 5 at the Hallowmere gate.

### Running the fights (Session 4)

Most tables won't fight tonight. All three are written for four level-3 characters, and every one has a way out.

#### The gate Hushmen

*Sergeant Mabry Cole and two Hushmen. Only if a refusal turns into a run or a brawl.*
- **Setup:** Cole at the door, a Hushman at each end of the counter. The lanes are 60 feet away.
- **Each round:** Cole shouts for the gate to close. His Hushmen grapple runners (Pack Tactics) and drag them toward the Belfry. Cole fires his pistol at legs, not heads (treat it as a hand crossbow).
- **When it ends:** anyone 60 feet into the lanes is lost in the fog. The Hushmen won't chase past the Hall of Tithes.
- **Scale on the fly:** five characters or level 4: add two Hushmen. Three: Cole and one Hushman.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** a downed character wakes in a Belfry cell. Rook hears by morning.

#### Haldric's escort at the masque

*Ser Haldric and four palace guards. Only if they threaten Maudrin.*
- **Setup:** Haldric between the party and the side door Maudrin left by. Guests scatter to the walls.
- **Each round:** Haldric asks twice, then fights to disarm with the flat of his blade while the guards grapple. His shield stops the first spell aimed only at him.
- **When it ends:** leaving ends it. When Haldric is Bloodied he calls for terms, and the screaming brings Isolde to broker them.
- **Scale on the fly:** at level 4, add a Dawn Order knight. Three characters: two guards.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** the downed are carried to Vaer's Belfry. The party is now a named enemy, and has seen Haldric take her orders.

#### The siege patrol on the Old Shore

*Tobias Quill, a Hushman Sergeant and Hushmen (see the encounter).*
- **Setup:** house by house with torches, two at a door and two in the lane.
- **When it ends:** Quill withdraws when two Hushmen drop or the ferry-folk bar the lane. The Sergeant surrenders his orders rather than die.
- **Scale on the fly:** at level 4, add two Hushmen. Three characters: remove the Sergeant.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** retreat across the Mere with Brannagh's pilots.

- **Where to break:** after Maudrin has spoken to every character and before she asks them out to the terrace. Never during Haldric's escort.
- **Running long:** cut the first night. End on the terrace, with her offer answered, and ask the closing question there.
- **Running short:** let Isolde invite one character to her carriage afterwards and trade one secret for one secret. Or let Rook's messenger find them at their inn.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop when Maudrin arrives at the masque. Next session opens with her going round the room.

### If you get stuck (Session 4)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Isolde asks a personal question.** "Who did you leave behind?" "What did you give at the gate?" Somebody always answers.
2. **The Court is courteous at them.** A servant brings a note with their names spelled right. A Hushman holds a door. Then ask, "What do you do?"
3. **A Blank asks for help.** A grey-faced woman at the palace steps asks them if they know her name. Nobody does.

### End of session 4

**The closing question:** "Who in this city can you trust?" Then: "Before we wrap up, let's each say one thing we'll remember from tonight."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened** (press **The party did this** on each card): the gate play (give, refuse, charm), any Aurelie play, the bargain play, and the Eld play if he was here.
- **World-state facts to check:** Ossa's Heart, Maudrin's bargain, what the Court knows of the party, Lady Isolde, Brother Eld, Sister Aurelie, Ila Harrow, Pellmoor, and the Tithe Clock (+1 if they slept in the city).
- **Memory ledger:** each courtesy memory as a Tithe, in the player's own words.
- **Bargain log:** Maudrin's bargain, accepted (one favor owed) or refused.

**Rewards:**
- **Level:** none yet. They stay level 3 until after Session 6.
- **If they accepted:** the Tithe-exemption (one named memory no Tithe can take), safe passage, and a Court pension: 100 gp now and 10 gp a tenday, paid at the Hall of Tithes, where the Hushmen can count them.
- **If they refused:** nothing from the Court, and Isolde's card, with a time and a place.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to anyone who refused the courtesy memory and took the consequence, and to the best answer anyone gave Maudrin.
