## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. A wedding-loud camp, a daughter who's been listening for her father, and a huge cheerful man who's dying and hasn't told anyone. It's mostly talk and games, and it ends with a promise. If they end up fighting the Thane, it's because they chose to.*

### Before you start (Session 17A)

**Level:** four characters at level 7.

**Check the world state first.** Five facts change tonight:
- **The lamp.** Carried: Kessa sees it first, and wants it. Refused: Yenna has no message and is cooler. Freed by Dispel Magic: they've got a message and no lamp; run the cooler Yenna with Advantage.
- **How did they arrive?** As guests of Sorrel's patrol, or watched.
- **Who's carrying storm-stones?** Kessa notices.
- **Was Isolde captured, or their informant?** Then they may already know she sold Tithed memories to the Ironhorn.
- **Thrane's Heart** is in Kessa's Heart-Hall. By the end it's theirs, or it isn't.

**What this session is about.** Everyone in this camp wants to be asked honestly. Yenna wants to hear what her father said. Kessa wants somebody to know he's dying who isn't waiting to be Thane. The Heart is what they came for; how they get it is what the steppe will remember.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Let the party be good at something silly in front of a crowd.
- Make Kessa the most likeable man in the campaign, then show them his hands.
- Say the oath aloud, slowly, so that everybody knows what they're promising.
- End on the question: **"What did you promise the Thane?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Kessa; Yenna; an Ironhorn Champion; five riders and five horses.
- **Maps:** rings of white tents; a fire in the middle; a long white hall; horse lines along one side.
- **The metal list** from last session: the things they'd be sorry to lose.
- **Props:** four mugs, and something to drum on. Keep the drum going, slowly, under Scenes 1 to 3. Stop it for Scene 4.
- **The Bargain log,** open.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **The steppe contest:** three successes before three failures, however many players there are. DC 14: Strength (Athletics), Wisdom (Animal Handling) or Charisma (Performance). Everyone tries once before anyone goes again, and a repeated skill needs a new way of doing it.
- **Help:** a character who's good at it can give a friend Advantage by describing how.
- **Heroic Inspiration** can reroll any of these. Say so before they need it.
- **Influence:** Kessa starts Friendly and DC 15 for anything that costs him. Lying to him makes it harder, not easier.
- **First blood** means the duel ends when one side is **Bloodied**: at half its Hit Points or fewer.
- **Theft:** Dexterity (Stealth) DC 15 for each thief. **A distraction** for Yenna: a group check, DC 14.
- **Knocking out:** when a melee attack drops someone to 0, the attacker can choose to leave them unconscious.

### Opening recap (Session 17A)

> **SAY:** "You came out of the mountain with *(a dead man's lamp in your hands / a dead man's warning in your ears)*, and four riders asked you where you were taking it. From the ridge you saw two things: a red skull, and a long white hall that hummed. So. Which way are you walking?"

- **Then hand it over:** let them answer the question from last week. If it's Yenna, they'll still have to walk through the Ring to reach her.

### Scene 1: The Thane's welcome

**Where and why.** The Ring, at the middle of the camp, in the evening. Everyone's there.

> **SAY:** "The tents are white, with horse-skulls on their poles. Drums beat slow and steady. Riders cross the camp at a gallop, shouting names you do not know. The air smells of smoke and horse-sweat and something sharp like lightning. A huge man with braids of bronze wire rises from a fire and grins at you."

**What's really going on.** Kessa loves guests. He feeds them first, always. He's also seen a blue light come down off the ridge, and something in him turned to look at it.

**Kessa.** Huge, bearded, a face like a good day. Calls everybody "rider". Sits on a camp stool that's far too small. *Lines:* "Riders! Sit. Drink. Tell me something true and I'll tell you something useful." · "The Court taxes my clans in memories. My grandmother's songs sit on a shelf in Hallowmere. I want them back, and I want the shelf." · Asked about his hand: "Nothing. A tickle from the Heart. Don't tell the clans, rider, or I'll tell them you cheat at dice."

**Go round the fire: "He wants one true thing from each of you."** Anything true earns one useful thing back:
- The Court's knights have been seen on the Brass Road, counting bridges.
- The Heart hums louder when there's a storm at sea.
- Yenna's the only one in the camp who tells him no.
- Nobody's been down the Old Works and come up laughing. Until you.

**If they…**
- **Have the lamp:** his right hand comes up by itself, black to the second knuckle, and sparks crawl across it. He puts it in his belt.

> **SAY:** "Kessa's black fingers hover over the lamp. 'That hums like a Heart. My Hall needs a stone like that. Name your price.'"

- **[Give it to him:](play:s17a-give-kessa-the-lamp)** he opens the Heart-Hall at once, no contest, and his bargain's gentler. Garrow's tether burns away into the Hall with one last joke. Pick it yourself, or use: "Is that it? I've had worse shifts." Then somebody has to tell Yenna. Let them do it, in Scene 2.
- **[Refuse him:](play:s17a-lamp-to-yenna)** he nods, and he means it. "Good. I'd have thought less of you. She's by the horses. Come back after. We'll eat."
- **Lie to him,** and he catches it: the drums stop. "You lied to me in my own tent." He makes it the price of the contest: a bigger stake.
- **Offer a storm-stone from the vault:** he'll take it, and it counts as one success in the contest.
- **Ask about Isolde:** "The lady in grey. She sold us back our own dead, a jar at a time. One of the jars talked about a Key."

**Bring it back.** He throws somebody a bread roll without looking. See if they catch it.

**How it ends.** "Go and see to the dead. Then come and play."

### Scene 2: The red skull

**Where and why.** The horse lines, away from the noise. It's the quiet heart of the session. Don't hurry it.

> **SAY:** "A sun-darkened rider is brushing down a horse outside a tent with a red horse-skull over the door. A long bronze braid hangs down her back. She gives you a short nod and goes back to work."

**What's really going on.** She's known for a year that he's still down there. She's never gone, because if she went he might stop.

**Yenna.** Quiet and gruff, like him, but quicker to smile. *Lines:* "You're not clan. Did the Thane send you, or are you lost?" · "My father dug the Old Works. Died there when I was small. People say the mine's haunted. People say a lot of things." · "Every month the Thane wants more riders. Every month fewer come back. Somebody has to say it to him." · When they tell her: "He said that? He said sorry? Give me a moment."

**If they…**
- **[Give her the lamp:](play:s17a-lamp-to-yenna)** ask the player who carried it to say the words. All of them: sorry about the collapse, and proud. Then read:

> **SAY:** "Yenna takes the lamp in both hands, and for a moment the blue flame steadies, and somewhere very far away an old man laughs."

- **Then be quiet.** She hangs it inside the tent. When she comes out she offers one thing: a horse, a bow, or a bargain with her clan. If they ask for the Heart, she knows how.
- **[Have no lamp:](play:s17a-meet-yenna-without-lamp)** "Garrow sent you? Then where is he?" She's hurt. Influence, Persuasion DC 15, with Advantage if they repeat one of his jokes, or tell her about the boots. She'll lend a horse; she won't risk the Heart. The lamp's a day away, and it's still there.
- **Set him free with Dispel Magic:** tell her that. She thinks. "Lighter. He'd have hated that. He'd have wanted to finish the shift." Run her as DC 15 with Advantage.
- **Gave it to Kessa:** she's already heard the drums stop. "He'd rather be somewhere he's useful." She means it, and she cries anyway. She won't ride for them unless they make amends.
- **Ask about her sister:** "She's fine. She's fast." She looks at the sky.

**Bring it back.** A horse puts its whole head on somebody's shoulder and sighs.

**How it ends.** The drums pick up. "That's for you. He's decided on a game. Let him win *something*, or he'll sulk."

### Scene 3: The contest

**Where and why.** Back at the fire, with the whole camp shouting. Skip it if they gave him the lamp.

> **SAY (Kessa):** "Welcome, rider. You've carried a dead man's lamp into my camp. That makes you my guests, and my guests win a wager or buy their dinner. I'll give you a steppe contest. Win, and you may speak to my council about the Court. Lose, and you'll leave me something to melt."

*(If they didn't bring the lamp, start from "My guests win a wager".)*

**What's really going on.** [The steppe contest.](play:s17a-contest) He's not trying to win. He's trying to see who they are when they lose.

**First, the stake.** Agree it before anybody rolls. One metal thing, specific, that they'd miss but can do without. Use their list from last session. Don't take anyone's magic weapon unless they offer it.

**Then, three events. Offer them; take theirs if they've got better:**
- **The hide:** two teams, one greased goatskin, a line in the dust. Athletics.
- **The wild one:** a mare nobody's ridden. They needn't ride her. They need to bring her to the fire. Animal Handling.
- **The long toast:** a song or a boast, to the whole clan, that has to mention a horse. Performance.
- **Anything else that'd make a drunk crowd cheer:** DC 14. Let it work.

**If they…**
- **Win:** the roof comes off. Kessa lifts somebody. He keeps his word: they speak to the council, and the Hall's open.
- **Lose:** he melts the stake himself, at his own fire, and hands back a bronze bead of it for each of them. The Hall stays closed. He thinks better of anyone who laughed.
- **Cheat:** Tolla sees (she's got a Passive Perception of 11, so they might get away with it). If she does, it's a failure and a joke at their expense.
- **[Want a duel instead:](play:s17a-duel-kessa)** "Now you're talking." See the fights.

**Bring it back.** The council's oldest rider bangs a cup and bets *against* the party, loudly, in gold.

**How it ends.** The crowd's eating. Kessa tips his head at a small tent with nobody round it. The drums stop.

### Scene 4: The real offer

**Where and why.** A plain tent, one lamp, one stool. Kessa and the party. If they lost the contest, he still makes the offer, only later, and a bit more tired.

> **SAY (Kessa, in private):** "I'm dying, rider. Thrane's Heart has been eating my hands since I took it from the Brass Road. I've heard the Court's Key can cure lightning-sickness. I don't want to die in a tent with a headache. I want to ring that bell and bring the storms back. I want Maudrin Vesk's head on a spear. Help me with one, and I'll help you with the rest. That's a fair bargain, isn't it?"

He takes his gloves off while he's talking. Both hands are black to the wrist.

**What's really going on.** He believes the Key can cure him, because a Tithed memory that Isolde sold him spoke of it. **It can't.** He can't ring Thrane himself: whoever rings it is marked by the lightning, and a man who's already this sick wouldn't live through the first stroke. And his council won't follow a Thane who's gone deaf or blind.

**If they…**
- **[Accept:](play:s17a-accept-kessa)** he lends them the Heart, for the ringing, and asks for an oath: **it comes back to the Heart-Hall afterwards.** Say the oath slowly. Ask each player if they're swearing it too. Write it in the Bargain log, exactly.
- **Refuse:** he laughs. "Fair enough. Then we meet at the Tor, rider. I'll bring the horses." The Hall stays closed. Go to Scene 5.
- **Tell him the Key won't cure him:** only if they know it, or guess. He laughs, then stops. "Who told you that?" Let them earn it. If he believes them, he's quiet for a long time; he still wants the bell rung. (If they don't tell him tonight, [someone will have to](play:s22-tell-kessa) at the Tor.)
- **Try to heal him:** Lesser Restoration stops the sparks for an hour. It doesn't touch the black. He's embarrassed by how good the hour feels.
- **Show him other Hearts:** his eyes narrow. He'd like them too. He'll ask for a wager, and he'll only get rough if they've lied to him.
- **Promise him Maudrin's head:** write it down. He'll remember.
- **Ask who follows him:** "Tolla, if she's fast. Yenna, if they've any sense."

**Bring it back.** He tries to pick up his cup, and misses. He waits to see whether anyone'll pretend not to have noticed.

**How it ends.** They've sworn, or they haven't.

### Scene 5: The Heart-Hall

**Where and why.** The long white tent. They come as guests, or in the dark.

> **SAY:** "The grass runs to the horizon in waves. Tents of white hide are pitched in rings. At the center, a long hall of white hide holds a bronze clapper hung from a horse-skull, and it hums."

**If they…**
- **Are given it:** Kessa lifts it down himself, with his gloves on, and doesn't look at anybody. It weighs about as much as a sleeping child. Ask who takes it.
- **[Steal it:](play:s17a-take-thrane)** Dexterity (Stealth) DC 15 each, past the riders. They get away with it or they don't, but **Kessa knows by morning, and he knows who.** If they're caught, the Champion steps out, and Kessa offers a challenge rather than a hanging.
- **[Ask Yenna:](play:s17a-yenna-smuggles)** only if she has the lamp. The Heart comes out either way. What they roll for is the distraction: a group Dexterity (Stealth), DC 14, or anything loud enough to be better. They owe her a favour, and she's in trouble.

> **SAY:** "Yenna lowers her voice. 'Tonight, when the third drum stops. Don't be where he looks.'"

- **[Fight for it:](play:s17a-duel-kessa)** a duel opens the Hall. A raid's the other fight, below.
- **[Kill him:](play:s17a-kill-kessa)** read it, and then let it be quiet. The oath's void. Yenna splits the clan, and half the riders go over to Tristan Vesk.

> **SAY:** "The Thane sits down in the dust with his axe across his knees. 'Tell Yenna it was weather,' he says."

**Bring it back.** The Heart hums, and every bead of bronze in the camp answers, in order, outward.

**How it ends.** They've got the Heart, or they know what it'll cost. Either way, it's nearly dawn.

Ask: **"What did you promise the Thane?"** Have each of them answer out loud, in their own words. If the answers are different, that's the best thing that could happen. Write them all down.

### Running the fights (Session 17A)

#### The duel

*Kessa (AC 17, 136 HP), the Champion and one rider. First blood. It should be loud and fair, and end with somebody laughing.*

- **Setup:** the Ring, cleared, about 40 feet across, the whole camp round it. Kessa takes his shirt off; black scars like lightning run across his chest.

> **SAY (Kessa):** "First blood or the last? Name it, rider."

- **What happens:** Kessa fights whoever challenged him. Tolla watches their strongest fighter and only joins if somebody else does. The rider keeps people out of the ring. If the whole party wants in, so do those two.
- **Kessa's turn:** three axe blows (+8, 11 damage each). Once he's Bloodied he has Advantage. He uses the Heart then: a 30-foot line, Dexterity DC 15, 18 Lightning or half, and 5 to himself. On a critical hit, the lightning bites both of them for 7.
- **When it ends:** Kessa's Bloodied (68 HP or fewer) and yields, or the challenger is and does. He honours a yield, always. He never hits anyone who's down.
- **If he wins:** he takes the stake, and pulls them up.
- **Scale on the fly:** a lighter duel leaves out Tolla. A harder one adds a second rider.
- **Non-lethal:** assume it, and say so. If somebody wants to kill him, ask twice.

#### The raid

*The Champion (AC 16, 112 HP), five riders and five Warhorses. Only if they try to take the Heart by force.*

- **Setup:** outside the Heart-Hall, at night, on grass.
- **What happens:** Tolla calls out their strongest fighter by name, for single combat. The riders ride the others down: a 20-foot charge with a lance adds 7 damage. Kill the horses' room to run and they're ordinary spearmen.
- **Who to worry about:** casters in the open. Let them find tent ropes.
- **When it ends:** Kessa stops it the moment anybody proposes a fair wager. He's hoping they will.
- **Scale on the fly:** drop three riders and their horses for a duel with an escort.

- **Running short:** let the council talk about peace, and let the party speak to them.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop at the end of the real offer, before anyone answers.

### If you get stuck (Session 17A)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Kessa proposes a wager** about the next thing that happens, whatever it is.
2. **Dry lightning** on the skyline, and a small shape riding along it.
3. **Somebody's dinner sparks.**

**If they can't agree about the oath:** Tolla says, not unkindly, "You can swear *separately*. He won't mind. He likes knowing who."

**Names for riders** (cross one off when you use it): Hesh, Old Marrow, Pelly Two-Horse, Ansgar, Kit Redhand, Auda, the twins Bo and Bee, Grandmother Sull who bets.

### End of session 17A

**The closing question:** "What did you promise the Thane?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the lamp, the contest, the bargain, how they got the Heart.
- **World-state facts to check:** the lamp (Yenna, Kessa, or still carried), Thrane's Heart, Kessa's oath (sworn or not), Kessa (alive or dead), the Ironhorn Clock.
- **Bargain log:** the oath, word for word, and anything each player added to it.
- **A favour owed to Yenna,** if she smuggled it.

**Rewards:**
- **Thrane's Heart:** lent, won, smuggled or stolen.
- **Yenna's thanks:** a horse, a bow, or a word with her clan.
- **The council's ear,** if they won the contest.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to whoever said Garrow's words, and to whoever lost best.
