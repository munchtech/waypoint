### Before you start (Session 27)

**Level:** four characters at level 11.

**Check the world state first.**
- **Ysh.** Unstruck, or struck last time? **Struck:** go from the recap straight to Scene 4, at Oum's question.
- **The Key.** With the party: they choose. With Maudrin or the Court: is the group's resistance **ended,** or not? Ended: the Empty Choir (Scene 5). Not ended: the rescue chapter (Scene 5).
- **The six bells.** Read the ring ledger out to yourself, in order, with who rang each one. **Every ending uses it, and nothing unearned comes back.**
- **The Ringer.** Who, and have you spoken to that player alone? If it might be **Corin** (ally) or **Maudrin** (here or captured), that's decided by the table.
- **Mirel.** Unrung: the Sleepless gather if somebody seals the Key.
- **Dreams.** Lost in Session 17B? They come back tonight, in every ending but one.
- **The release verse.** Did they learn it in Session 2, and does somebody remember the words?
- **The Cult.** If they've ceased or been turned, their hum ends the Sleepless.

**What this session is about.** What you keep, when you can't keep everything.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Ask once more, privately if it's the Concord. A no costs nothing at all.
- Let whoever's hand it is do it themselves, out loud, in their own time.
- For the Unbound: **Unmake only if every single player says yes.** Anything less is Remake.
- Read out what's restored, bell by bell, with the name of whoever rang it.
- End on the question: **"What will survive?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **The ring ledger and the Memory ledger,** copied out by hand. You'll read from paper.
- **Blank cards, and a pen each.**
- **For the Concord:** something to seat. A key into a bowl. Let them do it with their own hand.
- **For the Unbound:** six cards headed Memory, Year, Sense, Name, Fear, Gift.
- **For the Second Dawn:** ten counters and, if Mirel's unrung, seven Sleepless.
- **The verse,** from Session 2.
- **The lullaby,** all of it. You'll hum the last note tonight.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **[The Concord](play:s27-concord):** six bells rung, the Key in hand, Ysh unstruck, one willing voice. No ordinary Toll. The **Great Toll** is the voice's whole name.
- **[The Second Dawn](play:s27-second-dawn):** one uninterrupted minute at the recess. In a fight, that's **one action a round for ten rounds, by one character.** Nobody's in a fight? Then it's a minute. Let it be one.
- **[The Unbound](play:s27-unbound):** one action. Whoever strikes pays all six kinds at **Ruinous.** Warn them of the kinds and the weight, never the exact losses.
- **[Refusing](play:s27-refuse-a-toll-mid-rite):** before the strike, it cancels everything, and nothing's lost. After it, the Toll's owed.
- **[If the Ringer dies](play:s27-ringer-dies-at-rite)** before it's done: nothing transfers, no Toll. Revivify has a minute. Somebody else may step in. The dead don't pay.
- **[Wish](play:any-wish)** can't pay a Toll, ring a bell, strike Ysh, or undo what comes next.

### Opening recap (Session 27)

> **SAY:** "Last time, you stood in front of a bell, and you told each other what you wanted. *(Say what they chose, in one sentence, and whose hand it is.)* Nobody's moved since. It's still on the note before the last one. Somebody upstairs is sitting on a step with a lamp, waiting to find out what sort of morning it's going to be."

*If it was struck last time, read instead:* "Last time, somebody struck the bell that's never been struck. The Hush cracked. You've been standing in that silence ever since. And it's just been broken."

- **Then hand it over:** "What's the last ordinary thing you think of?"

### Scene 1: Once more round

**Where and why.** At the bell. *(Skip it if it's been struck.)* They've slept on it, really slept on it, in their own beds. People change their minds.

> **SAY:** "Nobody says anything for a moment. The Key's in your hand. It's gone warm, the way a cup does. All you've got to do is decide to have decided."

**Ask each of them:** "Is it still the same answer?" It's all right if it isn't.

**Then, for the Concord, take the Ringer's player out of the room.** Really do. Two minutes in the kitchen.
- "It's their name. Nobody who's ever known them will know them. You retire them: they're the voice in the bell, for as long as there's a bell. They won't be in pain. They won't be alone; it's a song for company."
- "You can say no now, and it costs nothing. You can say no with your hand on the Key. Nobody'll be told you were asked, if you don't want."
- If yes: **"What are your last words?"** Let them write them down.

**What's really going on.** This is the only scene all campaign that's about the players. Treat it that way.

**If they…**
- **[Say no](play:s27-refuse-toll):** "The bell waits. It has waited four hundred years; it can wait for you to be sure." Go back in and say, "There's no voice for the Concord yet." Nothing else. No Toll, no price, no reason.
- **Want someone else to do it:** ask. Anyone willing, in the party. Then Corin, if he's an ally. Then Maudrin, but only if she's the one who says it.
- **All offer at once:** oh, that's a lovely thing. Give them five minutes. They pick.
- **Have changed the plan:** say the new one back in a sentence. Check the prices. Get a nod from everyone.
- **[Want to wait](play:s27-postpone):** they leave with the Key. It's a bit louder every day. No clocks, no punishment. Finish on Scene 5's question and play something else next week.
- **Want to say goodbye first:** yes. One person each, whoever's down here, or by Sending. Two lines.

**Bring it back.** The spare word. Something this table's been saying since Session 1 that isn't in any book. Use it in the next sentence you read.

**How it ends.** Somebody takes a breath. Go to **one** of the next three scenes.

### Scene 2: The Concord

**Where and why.** *All six bells rung; the Key with the party; somebody's said yes.* Under the lip, at the notch.

**Let them seat it.** With their own hand, on the table. Don't speak until it's in. Then, slowly:

> **SAY:** "You hear the six bells, each in its own voice: the dawn bell's clean light, the memory-bell's deep sorrow, the frost-bell's thin ice, the harvest's warm round sound, the storm's crack, the dream's soft lullaby. Together they form a chord that has not existed for four centuries. The chord rises and wraps around the Hush."

**After each bell's name, look at whoever rang it,** and have them say one word: what it took. Then ask the Ringer, in front of everyone, quite gently: **"Do you give it?"**

*If they say yes, and only then:* they say their last words.

> **SAY:** "The Dreamer hears it, sighs, and begins to dream more gently."

**Hum the last note.** Finally.

**What's really going on.** There's a new voice in the song. It's theirs. Everyone in the room's looking at a person they've never seen before, who's smiling at them, and then there's nobody there. They don't know why they're crying.

**Then, round the table,** everyone but the Ringer's player: **"Somebody once…"** and finish it. A thing that character did. No name. "Somebody once carried me up a hundred stairs." "Somebody once lied to a tax collector about a herring." Nobody in the world knows who. Let it go round twice.

**If they…**
- **Say no with a hand on the Key:** it comes out of the notch as easily as it went in. The chord fades. Nobody's lost a thing. Back to Scene 1.
- **[Let Corin do it](play:s27-corin-voice):** "'Three generations,' Corin says. 'It's an old song.' He touches the Key." Ask the table, not the dice. He pays it himself. Nobody in the party retires. Nobody remembers who brought the hammer.
- **[Let Maudrin do it](play:s27-maudrin-voice):** only if she offers, freely, and they agree. "'I kept it as it was,' she says. 'Let me keep it as it should be.'" The Court's got no head, and nobody can quite say who it used to be.
- **Want to write the name down first:** let them. The ink's there. It just doesn't say anything.
- **Ask if the Ringer can hear them:** "Yes." Say that one straight away.

**Bring it back.** The first thing that character ever said at your table. If you've got it written down, hand it to their player, folded.

**How it ends.** It's quiet, and it's a good quiet. Go to Scene 5.

### Scene 3: The Second Dawn

**Where and why.** *Any number of bells; the Key with the party, unstruck.* At the recess. One person kneeling.

**Count it.** Ten breaths, or ten rounds. Put a counter down for each. On each one, name a bell, or a place, or a person, from the ring ledger. After it:

> **SAY:** "You seal the Key into the Hush, unstruck, and the lullaby steadies. Above ground, the bells you rang are still ringing in the world. They are not enough to save everything, but they are enough to begin."

**Then read what's theirs,** only the ones that rang, with the name of who rang it:
- **Ossa:** the dead rest.
- **Vaer:** the sun comes up over Hallowmere, and it'll come up tomorrow.
- **Calla:** the ash fields bear, and next year too.
- **Hieme:** the thaw goes on.
- **Thrane:** the storms are out on the steppe where they belong.
- **Mirel:** the Sleepless are quiet.

**And then the ones that didn't.** Say them too. Say it plainly, in the same voice: "There's no dawn in Hallowmere." They chose with their eyes open.

**What's really going on.** Nobody's won. Nobody's paid, either. It's the ending where everyone walks out.

**If they…**
- **Haven't rung a single one:** tell them **before** the first counter. It's a stalemate. The song holds, and nothing's mended. If they still want it, it's theirs.
- **Are interrupted, with Mirel unrung:** [the Sleepless gather](#running-the-fights-session-27). Protect the minute.
- **Are interrupted by what's left of the Court:** one action a round, ten rounds. Nobody's heart's in it.
- **Stop halfway:** nothing's lost. Begin again.
- **Have Maudrin with them:** her gloves go quiet. She takes the other one off. She flexes her hand as if it's just been given back to her.

**Bring it back.** The bells' first keeper: whoever rang Ossa, on Ossa's counter, says what they forgot. Or that they can't.

**How it ends.** **Hum the last note.** It's quiet. It's a bit thin. It'll do. Go to Scene 5.

### Scene 4: The Hush Unbound

**Where and why.** *Somebody's striking it, or somebody did.* If it's a player, warn them once more, all six kinds, at Ruinous. "Do you?" Then let them bang the table.

> **SAY:** "The Hush cracks. Oum opens an eye, and the dream begins to unravel."

**The six Tolls.** Six cards, in front of whoever struck. One at a time, they turn one over and you settle it together: the **Memory** (which year?), the **Year** (twenty; show me), the **Sense,** the **Name** (which town?), the **Fear,** the **Gift** (who gets it?). Write each in the Memory ledger. If it was Maudrin, do it in six sentences. She doesn't make a sound.

**Then the light changes.** Read this very gently. It isn't a monster.

> **SAY (Oum):** "Who is ringing my lullaby? I have been dreaming a very long time. Is the dream finished? Is it time to wake?"

**[Pause.](play:s27-oum-answers) Out of character, all together.** Explain both:
- **Remake:** Oum dreams it again, from the parts it loved best. Some of what's here will be gone. **Each of you names one thing that won't be.**
- **Unmake:** the dream's over. It's kind, and it's the end. You'd have one last night, with everyone you love, and then the light. **It only happens if every one of you wants it.** If anybody isn't sure, it's Remake, and nobody has to say who.

**Vote on paper, folded, so nobody can be leaned on.** You read them alone.

**Oum.** Enormous, and interested, like a child at a window. *Asks direct questions. Never, ever lies.* **Wants** to know how it goes.

*Lines, when they talk:*
- "You're very small. I made you, didn't I? Or you made me a song."
- "Which part hurt?"
- "Was it good? I couldn't always see. I was asleep."
- "The one with the hammer is frightened. I don't want anyone to be."
- *("Are we real?")* "You're the realest thing I ever thought of."
- *("What happens if you wake?")* "Then it's morning, and you were a very good dream."
- *("Can you bring somebody back?")* "I can dream them. I don't know if that's what you mean. Is it?"
- *("Were you angry?")* "No. Did someone tell you I was angry?"

**What's really going on.** Nobody's ever asked it anything before. All anybody's done for four hundred years is sing louder.

**If they…**
- **Ask it to go back to sleep:** "I could. Not the same way. It wouldn't be the same dream." That's Remake.
- **Fight it:** there's nothing to hit. It watches them with great attention. "Is that a game?"
- **Let Maudrin argue,** if she's here: she gets one minute, honestly made: she wants the world exactly as it was. Oum listens. "But you weren't happy," it says. She doesn't decide. Nor do you.
- **[Choose Remake](play:s27-oum-remake):** "The bells ring once more, and each of you names something that will not be lost." Go round. One each, out loud. Say each one back, and add one detail that's better than it was.
- **[Choose Unmake, all of them](play:s27-oum-unmake):** "The light is soft, and everyone you loved is standing in it." Stop there. The last night is Session 28, and it's all goodbyes.

**Bring it back.** The drawing, the song, the story: whatever one of them told or sang to a sleeper along the way. Oum knows it. "I liked that one."

**How it ends.** The bells, or the light. **Hum the last note,** and let Oum join in.

### Scene 5: The first morning

**Where and why.** All endings. Up the stairs or up the pit. Coming up is free.

*The Concord, the Second Dawn or a Remake:*

> **SAY:** "Sela Pask's on the top step with a lamp, where you left her. She looks at your faces. She doesn't ask. She blows the lamp out, because, look. You don't need it."

**[Sing the verse](play:s27-release-choir),** if they've got it and Ossa's rung. They only have to mean it, for one dead person, by name.

> **SAY:** "Far away, under a lake so still it holds the sky, a woman sings her own name, and stops singing."

*(If they never learned it, Mother Sallow can rebuild it from the ledger. It can wait for Session 28.)*

**[That night, if they'd lost their dreams](play:s27-dreams-return):**

> **SAY:** "That night, for the first time in weeks, you dream."

Ask each player: **"What of?"** Ten words. This is often the best minute of the campaign.

**The other three ways it can go:**
- **[The Empty Choir](play:s27-empty-choir),** only because they all said they'd finished fighting. "The Hush falls quiet again, not because you saved it, but because the Hushmen have sealed the stairs. The places you changed still bear your mark. Elsewhere, the collectors return to their rounds." Then say what they changed that's still changed: the thaw, the storms, the quiet Sleepless. Dreams stay lost. Dawns and harvests run out when they run out. Ask if they're sure. A crew, the Choir or the Stilled would still come for them.
- **[The rescue chapter](play:s27-rescue-chapter),** if the Court's got the Key and they're still fighting. "The Court's sealed stairs are a vault of grey stone. Somewhere inside is the Key." Make it a whole session: [one way in](play:any-key-back-from-court) (Ansel's schedule, the Stilled's tunnel, the Choir by water, a Stormborn crew over the top), **one** thing that goes wrong, and one person on the Court's side who lets them past. When it works, the Key's theirs, and you're back at Session 26, Scene 4.
- **They waited:** breakfast. Somebody makes it. The hill's humming.

**What's really going on.** It's a morning. They'd forgotten what those are like.

**If they…**
- **Ask what happens to somebody they love:** next time. They'll see them. Promise it.
- **Want to know if it was right:** nobody's going to tell them. Ilam says, "Well. It's done," and hands them some bread.
- **Can't remember who they're missing** (the Concord): that's a feeling. Let them have it. There's one more cup on the table than there are people.
- **Go looking for Maudrin:** she's sitting on the hill with her bare hands in the grass.

**Bring it back.** The lamps. Yenna's, the camp's, the ones they've left burning in every window since Session 1. Down the hill somebody's going along putting them out, one by one, because it's light.

**How it ends.** Ask: **"What will survive?"** One answer each. Then stop.

### Running the fights (Session 27)

**There's one, and only if they're sealing the Key and Mirel never rang.** It's a minute long. They don't have to win it. They have to not stop.

#### The sealing minute

*Seven Sleepless (AC 13, 67 HP). Lighter: five. Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing and Psychic damage.*

- **Setup:** one character's on their knees at the recess with the Key, using their action every round. The rest are round them. The Sleepless come up through the floor, thirty feet out.
- **What happens:** they're made of what the **sealer's** afraid of. Ask that player for three things, and use them. They go for the sealer, every turn, unless somebody's in the way.
- **A Sleepless's turn:** a gaze (30 feet, Wisdom DC 13, 10 psychic and Frightened), or a touch (+6, 16 psychic, and their Hit Point maximum drops).
- **The count:** put a counter down at the end of each of the sealer's turns. **If they skip a round or drop to 0, it starts again,** with them or with somebody else.
- **Being Frightened doesn't stop it.** They can seal with their eyes shut.
- **What helps:** standing in the gaze's way; bright light; saying out loud what one's made of (an action, no roll: that one hesitates for a turn); **humming.** If somebody from the Cult's here, or a player hums it all the way through at the table, the lot of them stop and listen, and fade.
- **Who to worry about:** whoever's got the fewest Hit Points next to the sealer. Maximum Hit Points come back after a Long Rest; say so.
- **When it ends:** ten counters. They go out like candles.
- **Scale on the fly:** easier, the count carries on when someone else takes over. Harder, two of them were never afraid of anything until now.
- **Non-lethal:** the Sleepless don't kill a sealer. They sit down on top of them and keep them company. It's worse.

#### What's left of the Court

*Only if a fight's still on from last time. Use what's standing; no new arrivals.*

- **What happens:** they can see somebody sealing it. Half of them have stopped to watch. A Sergeant says, "Let them."
- **When it ends:** by the fifth counter, whatever happens.

### If you get stuck (Session 27)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Somebody's allowed to laugh.** Have Corin, or Ilam, or Sela sneeze at the wrong moment.
2. **The bell hums one person's name.** Just to see.
3. **Ask, "What's your character's hand doing?"** It always works.

**If they want a fifth ending:** check three things. Is the Key struck, sealed, seated, or theirs? Did anyone pay who didn't choose to? Is it more than they rang for? If it passes, let them have it.

**If somebody's crying:** that's fine. Ask if they want to stop. Usually they don't.

**If somebody wants Unmake and others don't:** it's Remake. They get to name the thing that survives first.

**If the Ringer's player regrets it next week:** in the story, a Toll doesn't come back. At your table, people matter more than rules. Talk to them, and fix it together.

**Names, if you need one** (cross one off when you use it): Juniper, Sorley, Brock, Yarrow.
