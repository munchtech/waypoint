## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. Kind, tired people offer the party a good night's sleep. They mean it. It's the most dangerous offer in the act, and nobody raises their voice. Play the Cult as the nicest people the party's met this month.*

### Before you start (Session 17B)

**Level:** four characters at level 7.

**Check the world state first.** Four facts change tonight:
- **The Cult:** feeding, or ceased? It's nearly always feeding.
- **Mirel:** if she's rung, nobody's humming. They're sitting round the fire, crying, and not sure why. Start [there](play:s19b-cult-after-mirel) instead.
- **Did the party stop the song last night?** If so, the circle's frightened, and it knows who did it.
- **What evidence have they got?** Edda's Notes, something the Librarian said, or the Hush's own words. Any of those makes the argument easier.

**What this session is about.** The Cult's right about the danger. If the Seventh wakes badly, everyone dies. It's wrong about the cure: it feeds the Hush its own people's dreams, and that's why the Hush is failing. The party has to tell kind people they're doing harm.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Make the offer tempting. The party's exhausted. Say so.
- State the price in the offer, before anyone says yes.
- Give them three good ways through: argue, sneak, or fight. And one way in: say yes.
- End on the question: **"What did the Hush take from you?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; eight Cultists; the Leader.
- **Maps:** a ring of people round a small fire, on the side of a hill; behind them, a low stone arch and the top of a stair.
- **Props:** a soft blanket. Put it over the back of somebody's chair without saying anything.
- **If you're comfortable humming, hum.** One low note, whenever the Cult's on stage. Stop when something matters.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Influence** on the Leader: Persuasion DC 16. Advantage with evidence.
- **The price of the rest:** a dream from each sleeper. They **can't dream again until the Hush is struck, sealed or tuned** (the end of the campaign). Anyone can refuse. Write it as **Other** in the ledger; it isn't a Toll.
- **Characters whose players are away** lose nothing.
- **Anyone who doesn't sleep** (Trance, keeping watch) loses nothing, and nobody minds.
- **Command** in 2024 allows Approach, Drop, Flee, Grovel and Halt. The Cult uses Halt and Grovel. "Rest" isn't a command. It's an offer.
- **Sleep** (2024): a Wisdom save; a failure and they're Incapacitated until the end of their next turn, and then a second save or they're Unconscious for the spell. Damage, or someone shaking them, wakes them.
- **Zone of Truth and Detect Thoughts** both work. See Scene 3.

**Places at a glance:**
- **The circle:** a small fire, nine people, a kettle. Nothing walks within thirty feet of it.
- **The arch:** waist-high, worn smooth, and behind it a stair going down.
- **The wall of nails:** each nail's got something hanging on it. A ribbon, a spoon, a whistle. One for every dream.

**Rumours (d6)** round the fire, said kindly:
1. "You'll only miss them for a week." (They believe it.)
2. "The song used to be louder." (True.)
3. "Somebody's been walking on the stair at night. From *below*." (True: Corin, if they haven't met him.)
4. "There's a lady in the city who keeps a key on a chain and cries." (True.)
5. "If it stops, we all stop." (True, as far as anyone knows.)
6. "Mother doesn't eat." (True.)

### Opening recap (Session 17B)

> **SAY:** "You spent a night on Vael Tor. *(Say whose fear it was, if that player doesn't mind.)* You've seen hundreds of people asleep in rows. You've met *(the courier / Brother Lull / a child with no face)*. None of you slept well. And on the far side of the hill, all night, somebody was keeping a very small fire going."

- **Then hand it over:** "How long is it since your character properly slept? What do they look like?" One answer each.

### Scene 1: The circle

**Where and why.** The side of the hill, at first light. Warm, after a horrible night.

> **SAY:** "A group of robed figures sits in a circle around a dying fire, humming very softly. When they see you, they smile, and their smile is sad and kind and terribly tired. 'Rest, friends,' one says. 'You're so tired. Why not rest here, where the song is kind?'"

**What's really going on.** They mean it. There's tea. They've been watching the party's camp all night, worrying. Nobody's going to attack anybody.

**Mother Lumen.** Sixty; thin as a rake; her voice goes down at the end of everything, like being tucked in. She holds people's hands. *Lines:* "There. There you are." · "You needn't decide anything. You can just sit." · "We don't take. We're given." · "I'll tell you exactly what it costs before you say yes. I wouldn't do that to you."

**If they…**
- **Sit down:** they're given tea, a blanket and a place. Nothing happens. It's lovely. Ask each of them what it's like to be asked how they are.
- **Ask who they are:** "We keep it asleep. That's all. If it wakes, we're what it was dreaming."
- **Ask about the humming:** they're keeping the tune going. It's the Hush's own lullaby. They learned it by listening.
- **Stopped the song last night:** Lumen asks, without any anger, "Was it you? It's all right. It was so *quiet*. Please don't do that again."
- **Threaten them:** the humming stops for a second and starts again. "You're frightened. Of course you are. Drink that."
- **Go straight for the arch:** Sister Nod's sitting on it. "Not yet. Please."

**Bring it back.** Pip wakes up, sees the party and starts to cry. Wat rocks him. It's the only sharp noise on the hill.

**How it ends.** "Stay the day," Lumen says. "See what we do. Then I'll tell you what we're asking."

### Scene 2: A day with the Cult

**Where and why.** All round the hill. Three short bits; each player picks one person to spend the morning with. It's where they learn to like them.

**Pick one each:**
- **Brother Lull, doing the beds.** He knows every name. He talks to them while he tucks them in. *If they ask if he's given a dream:* he drops a pillow. "I've been meaning to."
- **Sister Nod, at the wall of nails.** A nail for every dream. She can say whose each one was. She can't say what it was. Her own is a paper boat. *"I think I was going to go somewhere."* She doesn't sound upset. That's what's wrong with it.
- **Wat Furrow, chopping wood.** He came up in the autumn to fetch his wife. Bed two hundred and twelve. He sat down for a minute. *"I was going to take her home. I expect I still am."*
- **Pip, throwing stones.** He's six and he's furious. *"There's a man on the stairs. He's got a hammer. Nobody believes me because they're all asleep even when they're not."*

**If they…**
- **Offer to help:** they get on better with everybody. Give one free Advantage on any Influence roll tonight to whoever worked hardest.
- **Ask to see bed two hundred and twelve:** she's young, and there's a child's drawing under her hand. [They can wake her, for one minute.](play:s16b-wake-a-sleeper) If Wat's there, let him talk to her.
- **Look for what's wrong:** Wisdom (Insight or Medicine) DC 13. Everyone who's given a dream is a little *less.* They finish each other's sentences because there's only about a sentence each.
- **Look at the nails:** Study, Investigation DC 13. They go back sixty years, and there are more and more of them, closer and closer together. The Hush has needed feeding more often every year.

**Bring it back.** Round the hill, the humming drops by half a note. Everybody looks up, then down, then goes on.

**How it ends.** Dusk. The kettle's on. Pip won't go to sleep.

### Scene 3: What they're feeding

**Where and why.** The fire, before the offer. Give them the chance to work it out. This is the argument.

**What's really going on.** The Hush is made of Ysh's own song. It doesn't need dreams. Every dream it swallows is one more thing it has to hold, and it's getting tired. The Cult's been keeping it up by putting stones in its pockets.

**If they…**
- **[Use magic:](play:s17b-detect-the-cult)** Zone of Truth shows that every one of them believes every word. Detect Thoughts on Lumen shows two things: **she's dying, and she goes first.** Every time. That's evidence.
- **Show them Edda's Notes,** or say what the Librarian told them, or what the child on the stairs asked: also evidence.
- **Show them the nails:** it counts too. Nobody's ever stood back and looked at the whole wall.
- **[Say it:](play:s17b-talk-cult)** Influence, Persuasion DC 16 against Lumen, with Advantage if they've got any of that. Let the player say it in their own words first. If it's good, don't roll.

> **SAY:** "The Cult Leader listens with her eyes closed. When she opens them, she looks very young and very tired. 'Then what have we been doing?'"

- **If she believes them:** the Cult stops. Some of them cry. Two of them stand up, straight away, as if they'd been waiting, and Wat goes to bed two hundred and twelve and starts looking for his wife's shoes. They know the shrine better than anybody living, and [they'll stand with the party at the end.](play:s22-ally-cult) **The offer's off. Go to Scene 5.**
- **[If she doesn't:](play:s17b-cult-unconvinced)** nobody's angry.

> **SAY:** "'That's kind of you to say,' says the Leader, and she means it. 'But we have been so tired for so long.'"

- **They try again later with better evidence:** she'll listen again. She always listens.

**Bring it back.** Lumen's hand shakes. She tucks it under the other one, and sees that somebody saw.

**How it ends.** She puts the kettle down.

### Scene 4: The offer

**Where and why.** The fire. Put the blanket on somebody's chair, if you haven't. Read the price slowly, all of it, **before** anyone answers.

> **SAY (Mother Lumen):** "One night, by the shrine. You'll sleep, properly, all of you, and nothing will walk. You'll wake up mended. And it'll take a dream from each of you. Not a memory. You'll still know everything you know. But you won't dream again. Not until it's over, down there, one way or the other. I can't tell you when that is. That's all of it. I've not left anything out."

**What's really going on.** She hasn't. Say, out of character: "A full rest, no nightmares, nothing walks. Anyone who sleeps loses their dreams until the end of the campaign. You can each choose separately."

**If they…**
- **[Say yes:](play:s17b-accept-rest)** whoever sleeps, sleeps. A Long Rest, perfect, no saves. Go to Scene 5. The Hush stirs.
- **Some do, some don't:** that's fine. The ones who stay up hear something on the stair.
- **Say no, kindly:** nobody minds. They can camp outside the ring; last night's rules apply.
- **[Slip past to the stair:](play:s17b-sneak-past-cult)** a group Dexterity (Stealth) check, DC 13. If it fails, they're seen, and offered tea.

> **SAY:** "The humming never stops. Nobody turns their head as you slip past the edge of the firelight."

- **[Draw a weapon:](play:s17b-refuse-cult)** ask once: "These people have been nice to you all day. Are you sure?" Then:

> **SAY:** "The humming stops. Every robed figure turns at once, and their smiles do not change."

- **Try to take Pip away:** Wat puts himself in between. He doesn't fight. "He'll sleep in a bit. He always does in the end." If they offer to help get his wife out, he'll come too.

**Bring it back.** Someone in the party yawns, and all nine of them smile at once, the way you would at a baby.

**How it ends.** They've slept, or they haven't; or there's a fight; or the fire's gone out for good.

### Scene 5: What was taken

**Where and why.** Dawn. Quiet. A few minutes each.

**If anyone slept:** go round them, one at a time. Ask what the last dream was. Then tell them it's gone.

> **SAY:** "You wake up. You're warm. Nothing hurts. It was one moment and then it was the next, with nothing in between, like a page that's been cut out very neatly. There's a new nail in the wall. There's something of yours on it, and you don't remember giving it to anyone."

Ask each player what's hanging on their nail. Write it down.

**If the Cult stopped:** it's the first silence on the hill for sixty years. Lumen's asleep, on her own, with her mouth open, dreaming. Her face is doing all sorts of things. Nobody's going to wake her.

**If nobody slept and nothing's changed:** the circle's still humming. Pip's asleep at last, on somebody in the party. He's drooling.

**If there was a fight:** there's nobody on the arch. The kettle's still warm.

**Bring it back.** From under the arch, quite a long way down: one small, clear, metal tap.

**How it ends.** Ask: **"What did the Hush take from you?"** If it took nothing, ask it anyway.

### Running the fights (Session 17B)

#### The Hush Cult defends the shrine

*Eight Cultists (AC 13, 44 HP) and the Leader (AC 15, 81 HP). It's optional and it should feel bad. They fight to get people to lie down.*

- **Setup:** the circle, night, with the arch behind it. The Cult's between the party and the stair.
- **What happens:** in the first round Cultists cast Command (Halt) and Sleep at the front line. They step around anybody who's down. They keep saying kind things. They follow nobody away from the arch.
- **A Cultist's turn:** one knife (+4; 6 and 7 Psychic), or Command, twice a day, or Sleep, once (saves are Wisdom DC 12).
- **The Leader's turn:** three soft bolts at up to 120 feet (+6, 16 Psychic each). Or Hypnotic Pattern or Sleep, twice a day each (Wisdom DC 14). **She drains a dream only from someone who's Charmed or Incapacitated:** Wisdom DC 14, 13 Psychic, and Disadvantage on their next check. (That one comes back after a Long Rest off the hill.) She can Counterspell or Shield twice. **Wake your friends up.** It's an action to shake somebody.
- **Who to worry about:** two characters asleep in round 1 is a bad start. Elves and others who can't be put to sleep by magic are the party's best hope; say so.
- **They never use "Rest" as a Command.** It isn't one. They just say it.
- **When it ends:** the party steps away from the arch; or Lumen's convinced (Persuasion DC 16, and they can try it in the middle of the fight, as an action); or she's down, and the rest sit on the ground and hum.
- **Scale on the fly:** four Cultists and the Leader for a lighter fight.
- **Non-lethal:** they'll only ever put people to sleep. Any character who drops wakes up at dawn in a bed in the Hall, with a card.

### If you get stuck (Session 17B)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Pip wakes up screaming,** and this time everyone can hear what he heard: footsteps, below.
2. **The fire goes out,** and all nine of them keep humming in the dark.
3. **Lumen faints.** Nobody in the circle knows what to do about it. They tuck her in.

**If the players hate the Cult:** let them, and then have Brother Lull ask one of them, privately, whether it hurts.

**If the players all want to say yes:** let them. It's a real choice with a real price, and they'll be glad of the rest. The dreams come back in every ending but the Empty Choir.

**Names for Cultists** (cross one off when you use it): Sister Doze, Brother Linen, Old Nan Feather, Goodman Hush, Maud Candle, Tibb, Ansel Drift.

### End of session 17B

**The closing question:** "What did the Hush take from you?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the rest, the argument, the sneak or the fight; anything they read in Lumen's mind.
- **World-state facts to check:** the Cult (feeding or ceased), dreams (lost, and whose), the Hush Clock.
- **Ledger:** each lost dream, as Other, with what's on the nail.
- **Wat, Pip and bed two hundred and twelve:** where you left them.

**Rewards:**
- **A perfect rest,** for those who took it.
- **Friends at the shrine,** if the Cult stopped. They'll stand with the party at the end.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to whoever changed somebody's mind without raising their voice, or said no to something they wanted.
