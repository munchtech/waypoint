## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. Tonight is a trial without a judge: two knights, one oath, and a crowd that asks the questions. Evidence wins it, and magic can only support evidence, never replace it. Then a spy confesses, and a child needs getting out.*

### Before you start (Session 6)

**Level:** four characters at level 3. They reach **level 4 at the end of tonight**, after the city investigation.

**Check the world state first.** Five facts in Foresight:
- **The Ledger:** do they hold the original, Ansel's copy, or neither? That decides whether Rook can win.
- **Ossa's Heart:** if it's in the Dawn Order armory, whoever wins the duel holds it.
- **Wren Caddock:** still the Court's spy. She confesses tonight (Scene 3).
- **Tessa Caddock:** "Not used at this table" if your table hasn't agreed to child peril. Then Tessa is Wren's grown sister, and the rescue is the same.
- **Ila Harrow:** if she travels with the party, she hears Wren's confession. That matters: Wren gave the Court her father's name.

**What this session is about.** Captain Rook of the Dawn Order knows her senior knight sells the Order's protection to the Court. She's challenged him to an oath-duel: a public hearing where each swears one oath and the crowd asks questions. She asks the party for proof. Afterwards Wren, who has been giving the Court names for two years, confesses at Bet's bakery. One of those names was Tam Harrow's. The Court holds her daughter.

**Your goals tonight:**
- The duel is won or lost on what the players bring and what they ask. Let them choose the three questions.
- Wren's confession is a quiet scene. Protect it.
- The players decide whether Tessa gets out, and how.
- End on: "What will the defeated side do next?"

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Captain Rook; Ser Haldric and a Dawn Order knight; a crowd (coins will do); Wren; Bet Ostrow; two corridor Hushmen; Tessa.
- **Maps:** the Dawn Steps are a wide flight of grey stairs below the Order's hall, with a landing at the top for the two knights and the crowd below. The clerks' dormitory in the Hall of Tithes is one corridor, three doors, a stair-head with an alarm bell, and a kitchen at the bottom.
- **Props:**
  - *The armory-payment entry:* write it on a scrap. "Dawn Order armory, protection, quarterly: received of the Chancellor's purse. H.M." Hand it over when they find it.
  - *The oath, on a card:* "I have not sold this Order's protection to the Court."
  - *A small bell* for Bet's scene, if you have one. A spoon on a glass will do.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **The oath-duel.** Each knight swears the oath, then answers three questions from the crowd. Whoever breaks the oath or refuses to answer loses. No magic automatically detects a lie.
- **Zone of Truth and Detect Thoughts.** Haldric stands in a 10-foot silence-stone field on the Steps, and his shield stops the first spell aimed only at him each day. Area spells still work. Magic can find evidence and support it. It never wins the hearing alone.
- **Finding the entry.** With the Ledger or Ansel's copy, the entry takes ten minutes and no roll. Study, Intelligence (Investigation) DC 15 to have it ready before the hearing starts. Failure means a recess or another witness, not losing the evidence.
- **Heroic Inspiration** for generosity: giving Bet back her own memory freely earns it.
- **The Tithe Clock:** one more night in the city is +1.

### Opening recap (Session 6)

> **SAY:** "Last time, Lysse Carrow sent you to the vault, and the Librarian of the Sealed Book traded you a label: MIREL, LOST. *(Say who gave it what.)* In a room without a sound, you found Edda Thorne's diary and heard the truth about the Quiet Night. *(Say what they opened and what they carried out, in one line each. If a memory came back, name it.)* And this morning a knight in a faded dawn-coloured cloak is waiting outside your door."

- **Then hand it over:** "She doesn't introduce herself. Who opens the door?"

### Scene 1: Rook's commission

**Where and why.** Outside the party's lodging, early. Read Rook's box from the plan above.

**What's really going on.** Rook needs the Ledger, or the entry in it, before the hearing in two days. She'll give them a Dawn Order escort for a day, which keeps the Hushmen off. She's honest, and she's also using them.

**Rook.** Clipped, military, says "we" when she means "I." *Wants:* the dawn back, and her men alive. *Lines:* "We don't have time for pleasantries." · "Paperwork wins hearings. Swords lose them." · "We've compromised. We'll say so. He won't."

**If they…**
- **[Take the commission:](play:s06-take-commission)** the escort for a day; Influence DC 15 for a second day. Haldric knows by evening.
- **Already hold the Ledger:** she reads it on the spot and goes quiet. "Ten minutes. That's all I needed."

**Bring it back.** A horn sounds from the Order's hall: the hearing's been called for tomorrow at noon. Rook straightens. "We're out of time."

**How it ends.** They have a day to find, copy or prepare the evidence.

### Scene 2: The oath-duel on the Dawn Steps

**Where and why.** The Dawn Steps at noon under a grey sky. The crowd fills the stair. Rook and Haldric stand on the landing.

> **SAY:** "Rook speaks first. 'I have not sold this Order's protection to the Court.' Haldric smiles at the crowd and says the same words, as if they were a kindness. A herald calls for the first question. The crowd turns, slowly, to look at you."

**What's really going on.** Haldric denies payment until he's confronted with the armory-payment entry, Ansel's copy, or a corroborated witness. Rook admits her compromises and denies selling protection, which is true.

**Run the procedure:** each knight answers three questions. The players choose the questions and may present evidence. Give the quietest player one of the questions.

**If they…**
- **[Present the evidence:](play:s06-present-evidence)** Haldric breaks. Rook wins the Order and the armory's Hearts: Vaer's, and Ossa's if it's there. [Rook returns Ossa's Heart](play:s06-rook-returns-ossa) to the party without being asked.
- **[Have no evidence:](play:s06-no-evidence)** Haldric keeps his office for now (Insight DC 15: relief, not confidence). They can reopen the case later.
- **[Back Haldric:](play:s06-back-haldric)** he wins, and asks for Bellwright names. Only names they actually give are endangered.
- **[Stay out:](play:s06-stay-out)** Rook's case fails, and the city tightens (Group Stealth DC 15 for the rest of the Act).
- **[Use spells:](play:s06-spells-on-the-steps)** see the rules above. A Zone of Truth helps a witness; it doesn't convict a knight.
- **[Attack Haldric:](play:s06-attack-haldric)** the hearing is void. See the brawl below. [Killing him](play:s06-kill-haldric) or [capturing him](play:s06-capture-haldric) has its own play.
- **[Steal Vaer's Heart while everyone watches:](play:s06-steal-vaer-at-the-steps)** Thieves' Tools DC 17, Stealth DC 15 through the yard.

**Bring it back.** The herald strikes his staff on the stone, three times, and the crowd hushes. "The next question."

**How it ends.** One knight walks down the Steps without his sword. Ask the closing question now if you're near time; otherwise, go to Bet's.

### Scene 3: Wren's confession

**Where and why.** Bet Ostrow's bakery that evening. Bet is pulling loaves. Wren is waiting at the back table with her hands round a cup she hasn't drunk.

Read Wren's box from the plan above. Then stop talking.

**What's really going on.** Wren is telling the truth (Insight DC 12) and is terrified. She has decided to confess before the Court notices Ansel's copies. She isn't asking forgiveness. She wants Tessa out.

**Wren.** Warm, chatty when she's not frightened; tonight she isn't chatty. *Wants:* her daughter safe, and to stop. *Lines:* "I'd stop if I could." · "I warned them, too. Some of them. It wasn't enough." · "Don't tell me it's all right. It isn't."

**This is a heavy moment.** If Ila is here, or the party knew Tam, the players lead now. Let them be angry. Answer questions; don't steer. If someone is cruel to Wren, she breaks, and leaves.

**If they…**
- **[Hear her out:](play:s06-wren-confesses)** they learn who gave up Tam Harrow, and how to reach Tessa: the room, the evening shift change, and that a Hushman named Ilam Bray knows the service entrance.
- **[Hand her over to the Court:](play:s06-hand-wren-over)** she's exposed, and Tessa is moved.
- **[Bet's bread:](play:s06-bet-bread)** if anyone is kind to Bet, she gives them bread, then freezes at the sound of a small bell from a child's pocket, and cries without knowing why. Giving Bet back her own memory freely earns Heroic Inspiration.

**Bring it back.** Bet sets a warm loaf in the middle of the table without a word, and breaks it.

**How it ends.** They decide whether to go for Tessa tonight.

### Scene 4: Getting Tessa out (optional)

**Where and why.** The clerks' dormitory in the Hall of Tithes, at the evening shift change.

**If they…**
- **[Get her out quietly:](play:s06-rescue-tessa)** Deception or a Forgery Kit DC 16 for a forged transfer order; Stealth DC 15 in the corridor (Pass without Trace works; no silence-stones here). Or a deal with Ansel, or a kitchen evacuation. Bet shelters her; Wren has a cart at dawn.
- **[Bring Ila too:](play:s06-rescue-tessa-with-ila)** the same checks; both are out.
- **[Go without Wren's help:](play:s06-rescue-tessa-hard)** DC 19 and Stealth DC 17. Tessa's out; Wren is gone.
- **Fail a check:** the guards are alerted, and it's a chase, not a Tithe. See the guards below.

**Bring it back.** The shift-change bell (a clapper wrapped in felt) thumps once at the stair-head. Ten minutes.

**How it ends.** Tessa is at Bet's, or the Court still has her. Ask the closing question.

### Running the fights (Session 6)

#### The Dawn Steps brawl

*Ser Haldric and a Dawn Order knight. Only if someone attacks Haldric.*
- **Setup:** the landing and the stair, with the crowd scrambling clear. The encounter is written for level 4; at level 3, have Rook step in on round 1 instead of round 2.
- **Each round:** the knights flank and fight to disarm and arrest, using Parry. Haldric hangs back behind his shield and calls out to the crowd. Rook interposes and won't fight her own knights.
- **When it ends:** standing down ends it at once. Haldric accepts surrender in front of the crowd; it makes him look merciful.
- **Scale on the fly:** three characters: Haldric alone. Harder: a second knight.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** anyone dropped wakes in the Belfry cells. Rook is furious, and comes to see them.

#### Tessa's guards

*Two corridor Hushmen.*
- **Setup:** one blocks the door; the other runs for the alarm bell at the stair-head.
- **Each round:** they grapple and shout. They don't hurt a child.
- **When it ends:** stop the runner within two rounds and there's no alarm. Once the bell rings, a Sergeant and two Hushmen arrive in three rounds. Leave.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** a captured character is held for a night; Wren comes for them.

- **Where to break:** after the duel's verdict, before Bet's bakery. The confession lands better after a breath.
- **Running long:** move Tessa's rescue to the start of Session 7.
- **Running short:** let the defeated knight's men come to the party that night with an offer or a threat.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop at the verdict and ask the closing question.

### If you get stuck (Session 6)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **The crowd asks its own question.** A woman at the front shouts, "Where did my husband go?" Haldric has to answer something.
2. **Rook asks them straight.** "Are we going to win this?" She wants the truth.
3. **Haldric offers them something.** A quiet word on the stair: "Whatever she's paying you, I'll double it."

**Names for the Dawn Order** (cross one off when you use it): Ser Avis Coyle, Ser Brannoc Hewe, Squire Tamsin Lark, the herald Odo Prentice, Ser Wynn Garrick (one of Haldric's).

### End of session 6

**The closing question:** "What will the defeated side do next?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember from tonight."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the commission, the duel play, Wren's confession or betrayal, Bet's bread, and the Tessa play.
- **World-state facts to check:** the oath-duel, Ser Haldric, Captain Rook, Vaer's Heart, Ossa's Heart, Wren Caddock, Tessa Caddock, the Tithe Clock.
- **Bargain log:** anything Haldric promised or was given; Tessa's rescue, success or failure.

**Rewards:**
- **Level:** **level 4** for everyone, after the city investigation.
- **If Rook won:** Rook's writ (Dawn Order knights stand aside for its bearers, which matters at the chain-room in Session 9), Vaer's Heart within reach (Session 9), and 150 gp from the Order's own chest "for expenses."
- **If Haldric won:** his favour, which is worth nothing in a week, and 200 gp in Court silver.
- **If Tessa is out:** Wren's ally, and her Bellwright contacts all the way north.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to whoever asked the question that broke the hearing, and to anyone who was kind to Wren when they didn't have to be.
