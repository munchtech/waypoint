## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. A bell, a storm, a battle in the rain and a promise. It's the loud end of the Brassfall road, and the quiet part at the end is the one they'll remember: somebody walks back into a white tent with a bell's Heart in their arms, or doesn't.*

### Before you start (Session 19A)

**Level:** four characters at level 7.

**Check the world state first.** Five facts change tonight:
- **Thrane's Heart:** do they have it? If not, the first scene is getting it. Go back to [Yenna](play:s17a-yenna-smuggles) or [the duel](play:s17a-duel-kessa).
- **Kessa's oath:** sworn, or not? Read the exact words in the Bargain log.
- **Kessa:** alive, or dead? If he's dead, use [the quieter ringing](play:s19a-ring-thrane-kessa-dead).
- **Who won the duel in Act III: Haldric or Rook?** It decides which strike force comes up the road.
- **How many bells have they already rung?** That sets the Toll's weight: none or one, **Heavy**; two or three, **Grievous**; four or five, **Ruinous.**

**What this session is about.** A word given is worth something when keeping it costs you. They'll be stronger if they keep Thrane's Heart. They'll be trusted if they don't.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Give the bell its warning, honestly, before anyone lifts a hand.
- Make the storm the biggest thing that's happened in the campaign so far. It's the weather coming back.
- Run one good battle in the rain, and let the horses matter.
- End on the question: **"Did you keep your word to the Thane?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; three Wardens; three Sergeants; three knights; eight Hushmen; a handful of riders. You'll need only some of them.
- **Maps:** a round shaft with a ledge and a bell; a straight mountain road with a ridge above it.
- **Props:** a tray and some dried rice or lentils, for rain. Pour it slowly when the bell rings.
- **The Toll Deck,** and the Toll rules in Part 2.7 and 2.11.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **A Sense, dulled:** what it means is agreed between you and the player, and it's never worse than they signed up for. **Sight or hearing only if that player said yes at session zero.** Taste, smell and touch are always fair.
- **A Sense Toll can't be ended early by magic.**
- **Lightning-Marked:** once before the next dawn, when the Ringer takes Lightning damage, they can use their Reaction to deal 1d6 Lightning to someone within 10 feet.
- **Warden's Stone:** while it's out, nobody can cast a spell within 10 feet of the Warden, and spells already there are suppressed. **It's a thing:** AC 12, 10 HP. Or knock it off its chain: a Utilize action and Dexterity (Sleight of Hand), DC 15.
- **Arrest:** Strength save, DC 12, or they're Grappled and can't cast anything with a Verbal part.
- **Heavy rain:** things more than 60 feet away are Lightly Obscured. That means Disadvantage on Perception checks that use sight.

### Opening recap (Session 19A)

> **SAY:** "You left the Dry Thunder with *(a bundle of old pages / a bronze dog that isn't very good at hiding / an argument)*. Ahead of you is a hole in the ground as wide as a house. Around the lip, sitting very still on two hundred horses, are people who have never seen rain. They've come to see if it's true."

(If Kessa's dead, there are far fewer horses, and nobody's singing.)

- **Then hand it over:** "Corin asked if you'd help him finish the song. What did you say, and what did you say to yourself afterwards?" One answer each.

### Scene 1: Down the deepest shaft

**Where and why.** The stair. It's a held breath: ten minutes to talk about who's going to ring it.

> **SAY:** "The stair goes round and round the wall. The air gets warm, and then it gets sharp, like the taste of a coin. Every buckle and blade you've got leans a little toward the middle. And there it is, hanging in the dark on chains as thick as your waist: a bell the colour of a bruise, with a ring inside it where something ought to be."

**What's really going on.** The bell wants its Heart, the way a tooth wants its socket. Whoever's carrying it can feel it pull, and can put it down any time they like.

**If they…**
- **Carry Old Garrow's lamp:** he's out of it and floating ahead before anyone can stop him. *Lines:* "Mind your feet. I dug that. Mind that one as well, I dug it *badly.*" · "Four hundred years. You might wash your hands first."
- **Want to check it's safe:** Study, Arcana DC 13. The shaft's going to be hit by lightning, several times, about a second after the bell. The ledge is the safe place. It was built for it.
- **Bring Kessa down with them:** he stops at the last turn. He can't go nearer; his hands are sparking. He sits on a step. "Go on. I'll hear it from here."
- **Put off deciding who:** let them. The bell hums at each of them in turn, a different note. Ask each player what their character hears in it.

**Bring it back.** Somebody's coins slide out of their purse, across the ledge, and stop in a neat line under the bell.

**How it ends.** The Heart's in somebody's hands, and everyone's looking at them.

### Scene 2: Ringing Thrane

**Where and why.** The ledge. This is the Toll. Do it slowly and do it straight.

**Say the warning first, out loud, to everyone:**

> **SAY:** "Thrane will take a sense from you, and it will be *(Heavy / Grievous / Ruinous)*."

**Then explain it, out of character:**
- **Heavy:** one sense is dulled for a year: Disadvantage on every check that relies on it.
- **Grievous:** dulled for good.
- **Ruinous:** gone for a year (sight means Blinded, hearing Deafened), then dulled for good.
- You pick which, after it rings. **It won't be sight or hearing unless that player agreed to it at session zero.**
- Anyone can ring it in anyone's place. Anyone can say no. Nobody has to say why.

**If they…**
- **[Ring it:](play:s19a-ring-thrane)** ask the Ringer to hit the table with a flat hand. Pour the rice. Read this over it, as loudly as you dare:

> **SAY:** "The bell cracks like a whip. The sky above Brassfall splits open, and for the first time in four centuries a real storm rolls over the mountains. Rain falls like bronze filings. Lightning strikes the peaks in long white bars. The storm-stones blaze, and every metal object you carry hums in answer."

- **Then go quiet, and give the Toll.** Choose a sense that character has enjoyed at your table. Say exactly what's gone first. "You can smell rain. Everyone's always told you about it. It smells of nothing." Record it: kind Sense, bell Thrane.
- **The Ringer's Lightning-Marked** until dawn. White lines run up one arm, like a drawing of a tree.
- **Have Corin as an ally, and ask him:** he can put the Toll off one hour, once a day. It's never less.
- **[Ring it with Kessa dead:](play:s19a-ring-thrane-kessa-dead)** it's the same bell. Nobody cheers. Yenna stands in the rain with her hands open, and doesn't say anything.
- **[Won't pay, any of them:](play:s19a-refuse-thrane-toll)** that's allowed. Read the line below. Kessa nods: he couldn't do it either. The Ironhorn grow restless, and the bell waits.

> **SAY:** "The bell hangs in the dark, and your hands stay at your sides."

**Bring it back.** After the thunder there's a small sound: drops, hitting the ledge. Nobody in this shaft has ever heard it before.

**How it ends.** They climb up into the weather.

### Scene 3: The first rain

**Where and why.** The lip of the shaft. A reward. Let it be happy for as long as it wants.

> **SAY:** "Two hundred people are standing in the rain with their mouths open. A child's holding out both hands and crying, because it's cold, and it won't stop landing on her. Somebody's grandmother has got down off her horse and is lying on her back in a puddle, in her good coat, laughing."

**What's really going on.** For one hour nobody's fighting anyone. Kessa's sickness eases; for a season he'll have his hands.

**Kessa.** *Lines:* "Look at that. *Look* at it. I'd have paid twice." · He picks up a cup, with two fingers, easily, and drinks rain out of it. · "Two days, rider. I'll wait two days for it. Then I'll start to wonder."

**Go round the table:** "What does your character do in the rain?" Ask the Ringer last.

**If they…**
- **Dance, race, drink, sleep:** let them, and give Heroic Inspiration to whoever makes the table smile.
- **Stay with the Ringer:** ask what they say. Don't hurry.
- **Ask what it means for the mines:** the Shafts'll flood, and the ore will wash down the hillsides. People can pick it up. It's good news and it's dangerous.
- **[Meet the courier:](play:s19a-news-from-pellmoor)** Tamsin Reed, up from the coast, mud to the knees. The storm at sea's broken up.

> **SAY (Tamsin):** "The sea opened. Three ships came in on the tide, none flying a Corvane flag."

- **What she's asking:** Brannagh wants to know what to tell them. It's a real question; write down what they answer. The Landing Charter won't hold for ever now.

**Bring it back.** Old Garrow tries to feel the rain. It goes through him. He thinks that's very funny, and then he doesn't.

**How it ends.** A rider comes in at a gallop from the Brass Road. "Grey coats."

### Scene 4: Grey riders

**Where and why.** The Brass Road, two days after the bell. It's still raining.

> **SAY:** "Under the first rain in four hundred years, a line of grey riders comes up the Brass Road, and their gloves are dry."

**What's really going on.** [They want the Heart, not a massacre.](play:s19a-strike-force) Warden Ostrel rides out alone first, and asks for it nicely.

**Ostrel.** Even, tired, correct. His horse makes no noise. *Lines:* "The Heart, please. Then everyone goes home in the wet." · "I have rung nothing and I have lost a great deal. Don't tell me about prices." · "I'll ask three times. That was one."

**If they…**
- **[Give it to him:](play:s19a-hand-over-tongue)** he means what he says. Nobody's hurt, and the grey riders turn round. Kessa's oath just got very hard to keep.

> **SAY:** "The Warden holds out a gloved hand. 'Thank you. It will be safe.'"

- **Talk to Dame Ines,** if Haldric's knights are here: Influence, Persuasion DC 14. She won't change sides. She will keep her three knights out of it for two rounds, "to see to the horses."
- **Run for the hills with it:** the road's narrow and the Hushmen don't gallop. They can get to the Hall ahead of them; the fight happens at the tents.
- **Won't, three times:** he opens the lead case. The light goes flat. Run the fight.
- **[Kept their oath already,](play:s19a-ironhorn-ride-in)** by taking the Heart back before this: the Ironhorn ride in on round 3.

> **SAY:** "The drums stop. Over the ridge come horses, a hundred of them, and Yenna at the head with her father's lamp."

(If Yenna hasn't got the lamp, she's carrying a torch that won't go out in the rain.)

- **Haven't yet, but swore it:** Kessa and six riders hold the road behind them, so no one gets past. They won't join in. They're waiting to see.

**Bring it back.** Lightning. For one second there's not a shadow anywhere on the road, and everyone can see exactly who's who.

**How it ends.** The grey riders go back down the road, with the Heart or without it.

### Scene 5: The Heart goes home

**Where and why.** The Heart-Hall, at dusk, with rain on the roof. This is the point of the night. Leave twenty minutes for it.

> **SAY:** "The tent's loud with rain. There's a horse-skull on a pole, with an empty hook in it. Kessa's sitting underneath, on the ground, mending a bridle. He can do that, this week. He doesn't look up."

**What's really going on.** He's told his council they'd come. He's told himself they wouldn't.

**If they…**
- **[Give it back:](play:s19a-return-tongue)** ask whoever's carrying it to say what they say. The oath's **Kept.** Mark it.

> **SAY:** "Kessa hangs the Heart back on its horse-skull and doesn't look at his hands. 'You kept your word, rider. The Ironhorn remember that.'"

- **[Keep it:](play:s19a-keep-tongue)** he doesn't chase them, and he doesn't shout. He just tells everybody, for ever. The oath's **Broken.**

> **SAY:** "Kessa watches you ride out with his Heart and says nothing at all, which is worse than shouting."

- **Argue that the world needs it:** he listens. "It does. And it needed my word to be worth something, so I gave it to you to hold." He won't change his mind. He won't stop them leaving.
- **Never swore:** then it's theirs, honestly, and he says so. He'd like it left where it's safe. He'll offer a hundred riders for that. "That's not an oath. It's a trade."
- **Lost it to the Court:** they've got to come and say so. He's quiet. "Then you'll help me get it back." [They can, at the Strike.](play:s23-recover-thrane) The oath stays open.
- **Kessa's dead:** it's Yenna under the skull, and half the cushions are empty. The oath's void. She'd still like it home. "It'd be one thing that went right."

**Bring it back.** Tolla, by the door, who's been holding her breath, lets it out.

**How it ends.** Ask: **"Did you keep your word to the Thane?"** Each player answers for their own character. Remember they may not all have sworn.

### Running the fights (Session 19A)

#### The strike force, with Haldric's knights

*Two Wardens (AC 16, 82 HP), one Sergeant (AC 15, 52 HP), three knights (the Knight block), six Hushmen (AC 12, 32 HP). Use this if Haldric won the duel.*

- **Setup:** the Brass Road, ten paces wide; a drop on one side, the ridge on the other. They start 60 feet apart, in the rain. The Wardens are in the middle of the line, one each side.
- **What happens:** the knights come straight on. Hushmen go for whoever's carrying the Heart and try to arrest them. The Wardens walk toward the casters and open their cases. The Sergeant signals, and somebody gets an extra attack.
- **A Warden's turn:** two blows with the maul (+5, 12 damage, and a 10-foot push) or two crossbow shots (+4, 13).
- **Who to worry about:** anyone near the drop. A maul pushes 10 feet. Before the first push, say where the edge is. Anyone going over can make a Dexterity save (DC 12) to catch it.
- **Show the case before it opens.** Give casters one turn to move, or to shoot it.
- **When it ends:** the Ironhorn ride in; or both Wardens fall; or they've got the Heart, in which case they leave at once, in good order, and the party can chase them.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out the Sergeant and two Hushmen for a lighter fight.
- **Non-lethal:** Hushmen arrest. They carry their own wounded, and they'll carry the party's.

#### The strike force, if Rook won

*Three Wardens, three Sergeants, eight Hushmen, and no knights; the knights are hers now and they've stayed at home.*

- **What happens:** all three Wardens open their stones, 30 feet apart, so there's a wall of no-magic across the road. The Hushmen pin anyone they can.
- **The clever way through:** the stones are the fight. AC 12, 10 HP, or a hand on the chain. Every stone broken gives the casters a lane.
- **When it ends:** two Wardens down, or the Ironhorn.
- **Scale on the fly:** two Wardens and six Hushmen for a lighter fight.

#### If the Ironhorn ride in

Either take away two Hushmen and one Warden, or leave the fight as it is and let the riders hold the road, so nobody else joins it and nobody gets away with the Heart. **The party should still be the ones who beat the Wardens.**

### If you get stuck (Session 19A)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Lightning hits something made of bronze,** and it starts ringing.
2. **A flash flood** comes down the shaft road, ankle-deep, full of blue stones.
3. **A horse lies down** in a puddle and won't be moved.

**If nobody wants to pay the Toll:** don't push. Say, "You can ring it later. Kessa gets sicker and the clans get restless, and the bell waits." It's true, and it's not a punishment.

**If a player's upset by the sense you chose:** change it. You've both got the Toll Deck. Pick again together.

**Names for riders and Hushmen** (cross one off when you use it): Wet Hannock, Little Orsa, Mads Underhill, the widow Kell, Sergeant Pryce, Hushman Loe, Hushman Dace.

### End of session 19A

**The closing question:** "Did you keep your word to the Thane?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the ringing (or the refusal), the road, the Hall, the news from the coast.
- **World-state facts to check:** Thrane (rung), the Stormwall (loosened), Kessa (eased, if he's alive), Thrane's Heart (party, Kessa, Court), the Court, Landing and Ferry Clocks.
- **Toll log:** the sense, and its weight.
- **Bargain log:** the oath: Kept, Broken, or still open.
- **What they told Brannagh** about the new ships.

**Rewards:**
- **A bell, rung.** The Concord's one step closer.
- **A hundred riders at the Tor,** if the oath was kept.
- **Level 8.** Session 20's fights are written for it, so tonight's a good time.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to whoever kept a promise that cost them.
