## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. It's the big battle, and it's the first time most of them will have stood in front of Maudrin. Give her ten good minutes before anybody rolls anything. She'll be polite the whole time.*

### Before you start (Session 23)

**Level:** four characters at level 9. They go up to **level 10** when it's over.

**Check the world state first.** These five answers pick tonight's version.
- **The oath-duel.** Rook won: no Haldric on the line, and Rook's knights are yours. Haldric won, or it failed: he's here, with two Knights.
- **Maudrin.** Alive: she leads. **Dead:** run Tristan's column instead.
- **The Key.** With her, on the chain. Or with the party; then Haldric hesitates.
- **Maudrin's bargain** (the favour from Session 4). Owed, repaid, broken or refused. If it's **owed,** tonight's when she calls it in.
- **Jonah.** In custody: she's brought him. Safe, with Ilam an ally: Ilam's on the south road.
- **Also:** the oath to Kessa (if it's broken, he's coming too); whether Imber's been freed; which Hearts the Court's got; the allies clock; what they broke in the ambush or at the Quiet Quarry.

**What this session is about.** Being offered a way out by someone who means it. Then, a battle where the friends they've made matter.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Let them put their own friends on the field, one each.
- Make Maudrin's offer sound like a good one.
- Fight the Strike, with a count of what each ally does.
- Open the baggage. Let them win something back.
- End on the question: **"The light at the top of the Tor is the colour of a dream you have almost forgotten. Who goes up first?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **A big map:** a hillside with a road up it, a ridge on the north side, gorse, and a few standing stones. Forty squares across if you've got it.
- **Tokens or minis** for your version (see the fights).
- **The ally cards** from last session, with one job on each. Lay them on the table, face up.
- **Maudrin's Hit Points,** on a card of their own. It goes with you into Session 24.
- **A grey glove,** if you've got one. Put it on when you speak as her. Take it off when you don't.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Silence** (the spell): a 20-foot sphere. Nothing in it makes a sound, and no spell with words in it can be cast there. **Walk out of it.** It needs Concentration, so hitting her may end it.
- **Counterspell:** a Reaction to someone casting within 60 feet. The caster makes a Constitution save (hers is DC 15). If they fail, the spell's wasted, but **they keep the slot.**
- **Frightened:** Disadvantage on attacks and ability checks while you can see the thing, and you can't willingly go closer to it.
- **Grappled:** Speed 0, and Disadvantage on attacks against anyone but the grappler. To get out: an action, Athletics or Acrobatics, DC 12.
- **Bloodied:** at half Hit Points or fewer. **Maudrin leaves when she's Bloodied.** That's 55.
- **Knocking out:** when a melee hit would take someone to 0, the attacker can choose to leave them on 1 Hit Point, Unconscious. **Say so before they need it.**
- **Being Tithed is never a saving throw.** Consent's a player's answer, always.

### Opening recap (Session 23)

> **SAY:** "You read Edda's page, and you know what the Key is now. It's the heart that sings Oum to sleep, and it's on a chain round Maudrin Vesk's neck *(or: in your pack)*. Then people came up the road. *(Name each one.)* You said who you'd bring to the Tor. It's tomorrow. You're at the foot of it, the sun's going down, and somebody's moved all the sheep."

- **Then hand it over:** "What did you do with the last hour of daylight?" One thing each. If it's practical, give them something for it: a ditch, a rope, a better place to stand.

### Scene 1: The foot of the Tor

**Where and why.** Dusk. The party chooses the ground and puts their friends on it. It's a quiet scene with a map in the middle.

> **SAY:** "It's not a big hill. You could walk up it in an hour, if you were let. The grass is short, and the road goes up in three bends, like something dropped. There's a ring of stones on the top with the light behind it. It isn't sunset. The sun's on the other side."

**What's really going on.** They're a little ahead of her. She's letting them be: she'd sooner find them all in one place. She'll stop the column at the bottom and come forward to talk.

**Place the allies.** Put the cards out. Going round the table, [each player picks one company up and puts it on the map:](play:s23-allies-take-objectives)

> **SAY:** "The ridge fills with friends."

| Company | What it does tonight, once |
|---|---|
| **Rook's Dawn Order** | Holds the ridge. Nobody flanks from the north. |
| **The Ironhorn, or Yenna's riders** | Cut the retreat. The Hushmen can't come round behind. |
| **The Bellwrights** | Ring a screen. One caster ignores the Wardens' stones for a round. |
| **The Stilled** | A wall, 30 feet long, anywhere. |
| **The Blanks** | Walk into the line. Two Hushmen lose a turn, looking at them. |
| **Ilam** | Holds the south road. No second wave. |
| **Friends from over the sea** | Shut one Warden's stone, or give one person 4d8 Hit Points back. |
| **The Hush Cult** | Keeps the Sleepless down. Nothing comes out of the ground. |
| **The hymn** | Save it for the summit. |

**They never kill anyone.** If there are **three** or more, the party picks one flank nobody comes at all. If there are **six,** use the line below when the column arrives.

**If they…**
- **Have nobody:** the ground helps. Give them two of these: a drystone wall; a sunken sheep track; the sun behind them.
- **Want to go up before she comes:** they can start. She gets there behind them, and the Strike happens on the second bend. Same fight, narrower road.
- **Want to hide the Hearts:** where? With whom? Write it down. She'll ask.
- **[Find the mule's gone:](play:s23-hushmen-take-mirel)** use this only if they've an unrung Mirel Heart and left it with the baggage.

> **SAY:** "The mule is gone, and so is the pack."

  It's in her wagons by nightfall. They can win it back in Scene 5.
- **[Have Imber with them:](play:s23-imber-speaks)** he asks whether he might say something to the knights, when they come. He isn't fit for a fight. He knows that.
- **Ask what's up there:** Linnet's been to the first stone. "It's warm," she says. "I put my hand on it and it was *warm.*"

**Bring it back.** Someone's the first to see it: each stone up there's the shape of a bell, if you stop looking straight at it.

**How it ends.** The ridge line moves.

### Scene 2: Good evening

**Where and why.** The bottom of the road. Maudrin talks first. Give it time.

Put the glove on.

> **SAY:** "Hushmen on grey horses come over the ridge in a long line. Their gloves catch the light and throw it back as silence. Behind them rides a figure in dove-grey. 'Good evening,' Maudrin Vesk says. 'I'm so sorry to interrupt. But I believe you have something that belongs to me.'"

**[At six on the allies clock,](play:s23-coalition-breaks-the-column)** add:

> **SAY:** "Maudrin looks over the ridge, and her gloved hand tightens on the chain."

(There's no second wave. She's had to come herself, with whoever's in front of you.)

**What's really going on.** She wants three things: the **unrung Hearts** (rung ones are no use to her), the **Key** if they've got it, and **the Ringer.** She means to seal the stairs and go on keeping the lullaby, the only way she knows. She isn't lying about a single thing she says tonight.

**Maudrin.** Dove-grey, pale stone gloves. She uses everyone's name, and she never raises her voice. *Lines:* "Please, sit. The grass is dry enough." · "You've done something very brave, and it's hurt a great many people. Both of those." · "I'd like you to go home. I'd like it more than you'd believe." · "I don't want anyone to be frightened. That's all I've ever wanted. It turned out to be a great deal."

**If they…**
- **[Owe her the favour:](play:s23-favor-called)** she calls it in. Let the player who made the bargain answer.

> **SAY (Maudrin):** "One favor, dear, and I am calling it in now. Go, before I change my mind."

  - **[They keep it:](play:s23-favor-kept)** the Strike doesn't happen. She reaches the summit first, unhurt, with everyone. Their allies are bewildered. Skip to Scene 5's light; there's no baggage. **Level 10 anyway.**

> **SAY:** "Maudrin inclines her head. 'Thank you, dear. That is rare.'"

  - **[They break it:](play:s23-favor-broken)** no more bargains from her, ever.

> **SAY:** "'Then I will remember it,' Maudrin says, and the line moves."

- **Repaid it already, or never owed it:** she says, "You have repaid me. Go," and means it. It's a door. If they don't take it, the line moves.
- **[Surrender:](play:s23-surrender)** she's kind about it.

> **SAY:** "Thank you. That was wise. Now: the Hearts, please. And your Ringer will come with me to the Quiet Court."

  She takes the unrung Hearts, and the Key. Record each one. **Then ask the Ringer's player, out of character, and wait.** If it's yes, [she takes them;](play:s23-accept-tithe) if it's no, [it's an arrest, and they can get away:](play:s23-escape-captivity) Brannagh's pilots, Yenna's riders, or a ship lying off.

> **SAY (yes):** "'Thank you,' she says, gently. 'This will not hurt.'"

> **SAY (no):** "Four Hushmen take your arms. 'Please do not make us do this.'"

  The Empty Choir ending only opens if **the whole group** chooses to stop resisting.
- **[Ask to ride with her:](play:s23-march-with-maudrin)** she thinks about it.

> **SAY:** "Maudrin looks at you for a long time. 'Come, then.'"

  They end their resistance by choosing to. Their allies leave or turn. No Strike. It's a real road, and it goes to Session 24 with somebody else on the other side.
- **[Offer Haldric money:](play:s23-bargain-with-haldric)** it's quick and it's ugly. 500 gp, or a title. Take him and both Knights out of the fight.

> **SAY:** "Haldric weighs a purse. 'Fair price for a lie.'"

- **[Let Imber speak:](play:s23-imber-speaks)** the Knights hesitate: they lose their first turns, and so does Haldric.

> **SAY:** "The old man steps out onto the grass, frail and upright. 'I struck it. I am the one who did it.'"

  Maudrin doesn't look at him. It's the one time tonight she can't.
- **Call her a liar about the Key:** she's surprised. "I've never lied to you. Nobody ever asked."
- **Hold the Key up:** go straight to the fight, with Haldric hesitating.

**Bring it back.** She uses the Ringer's name, gently. If they've given a name away to a bell, she's the only one who still knows it. She says it, and watches it go past.

**How it ends.** She puts her gloves together. "Then I'm sorry," she says, and she is.

### Scene 3: The boy with the bird

**Where and why.** One beat, before the fight or just as it starts. Which version depends on where Jonah is. **Check your session-zero list first.** If harm to children's a Line at your table, Jonah's a grown cousin of Ilam's, or leave this scene out.

**If Jonah's safe, and Ilam's a friend:** [it's a gift.](play:s23-ilam-holds)

> **SAY:** "Ilam Bray stands in the road with his grey gloves off. Behind him, Jonah holds the wooden bird in both hands and watches his father, and knows him."

The boy's a long way back with somebody sensible. Nobody goes near him. Ilam's holding the south road, so the Court's second wave doesn't come. Let one player see it, and give them the moment.

**If the Court's got him:** [she's brought him.](play:s23-jonah-hostage)

> **SAY:** "A Hushman lifts a thin boy onto his saddle. He is holding a wooden bird."

**What's really going on.** Nobody's going to hurt him. The Hushman's been told so and is glad of it. He's there because Maudrin thinks the party will stop for him, and she'd sooner that than a battle.

**If they…**
- **Hand over a Heart:** he's let down, straight away, and walks across by himself. The Hushman waves.
- **Fight:** he's taken back to the wagons and kept well out of it. He's free when they win.
- **Have Ilam:** he goes. Nobody needs to ask. The Hushman on the horse used to share a watch with him, and opens his hands.
- **Talk to the Hushman:** Persuasion DC 13. He wants somebody to tell him it's all right to let go.
- **Ask Maudrin how she could:** "He's warm, he's fed, and he's been told the truth. I've done worse things to keep people alive. So have you."

**Bring it back.** The bird. His father carved it, and one wing's shorter than the other. Somebody notices that the boy's holding it the way you'd hold a real one.

**How it ends.** He's off the field. Say so, plainly: "He's safe, and he'll stay safe."

### Scene 4: The Strike

**Where and why.** [The battle.](play:s23-fight) An hour. Pick your version from the fights section and lay it out.

> **SAY:** "The first thing that goes is the wind. You can see the grass moving and you can't hear it. Then the line comes down the hill, all together, like a page turning."

**What's really going on.** The Wardens and knights hold the middle. The Hushmen go wide. Maudrin sits forty feet back, silences the casters, and shoots whoever's hurting her people most. **She goes when she's down to 55.**

**The three things worth doing.** Tell the table; they can see it from here.
1. **Hurt Maudrin.** At half, she goes up the hill.
2. **Take the Key.** It ends it.
3. **Break the Wardens' line.** Two down, and the Hushmen falter.

**If they…**
- **[Go for the Key:](play:any-steal-key-chain)** it takes somebody next to her, and she has to have been hit at least once. Sleight of Hand, DC 16. If it fails, it's still on the chain and she's noticed. If it works, they've got it; now they've got to get away on foot.
- **[Have it, and Haldric sees it:](play:s23-haldric-hesitates)** he and his Knights lose their next turns, and the Court's line gives way.

> **SAY:** "Ser Haldric Mose sees the grey case in your hands and stops, sword half raised, like a man who has just heard his own name spoken in a language he never learned."

- **Broke the oath with Kessa:** at the end of round two, [he comes.](play:s23-kessa-intercepts) See the fights. He wants the Heart.

> **SAY:** "The earth shakes. The Ironhorn come over the north ridge, and Kessa is at the head."

- **[Take Maudrin alive:](play:s23-capture-maudrin)** if she's cornered before she can leave, and the last hit's a melee one that chooses it. The column stops.

> **SAY:** "She sits very carefully on the grass, the way an old woman sits."

- **[Kill her:](play:s23-kill-maudrin)** the same, if they choose that instead.

> **SAY:** "The gloves fall open."

  Nobody cheers. The Hushmen nearest her take their gloves off. Either way, the Key's in the case and it's theirs.
- **[Are facing Tristan:](play:s23-tristan-strike)** he gives up when the Key's taken, and puts it in writing.

> **SAY:** "The man in grey speaks softly. 'I regret this.'"

- **[Somebody goes down for good:](play:s23-pc-drops)** their allies get them out. Aurelie's people are behind the wall.

> **SAY:** "Your friend lies in the grass, and the Hushmen's gloves are still raised."

  Revivify inside a minute, or Raise Dead through the mission. The dead pay no Toll and no stairs.
- **Are losing:** play the ally cards they've got left, all at once. If there aren't any, Maudrin stops it herself. "That's enough. Please. That's enough." She takes one unrung Heart, and goes on up the hill.

**Bring it back.** When she's hurt, she says a name under her breath, the way you'd call for a grandmother. *Cassia.* Anyone who's talked to the Sexton has heard it. It's his daughter, who carried the Key home. She's Maudrin's too, a long way back.

**How it ends.** At half Hit Points, she turns her horse.

> **SAY (Maudrin):** "I'll see you at the Shrine."

She goes up. Nobody can catch her: she steps from one place to another, and then she's a grey dot on the second bend. Behind her, the line doesn't know what to do with its hands. **Write her Hit Points down.**

### Scene 5: The baggage

**Where and why.** Straight after. Nine wagons and nobody guarding them. It's their turn to be given something.

> **SAY:** "It's dark now, nearly. The horses are cropping the grass. Some of the Hushmen have sat down. One of them looks up at you and says, 'Do you want us to do anything?'"

**Let them decide about the prisoners.** Most want to go home. They'll give up their gloves if they're asked.

**Then open the trunks.** Search, Wisdom (Perception), DC 12, or just ask the Sergeant. For each Heart the Court had, read it once and pass it over:

> **SAY:** "Among the baggage on the broken line lies a grey case, and in it a Heart wrapped in silence-stone cloth."

[Vaer](play:s23-recover-vaer) · [Ossa](play:s23-recover-ossa) · [Calla](play:s23-recover-calla) · [Hieme](play:s23-recover-hieme) · [Thrane](play:s23-recover-thrane) · [Mirel](play:s23-recover-mirel)

**What's really going on.** Those bells are reachable again. Check the Forecast out loud. If there's time before Maudrin gets down the stairs, there isn't much. A ringing means leaving the hill: three to six days, and she'll be at the Shrine long before that.

**If they…**
- **Find nothing:** because the Court had nothing. There's a trunk of children's shoes, and a ledger saying whose. Jonah's are in it.
- **Want to follow her tonight:** it's dark, they've just fought, and the light's getting brighter. Linnet: "It'll still be there in the morning. Won't it?" Sleep. A long rest's fine.
- **Ask after their allies:** nobody's been killed who had a name, unless a player wants it to have cost something. Ask them.
- **Have captured her:** she'll say one thing. "It's the fourth step that's loose." She doesn't explain.

**Level up.** Everyone's **level 10.** Do it now, or before next time.

**Bring it back.** Whoever was the first to think of home, in the first session, hears a bell in the valley. It's a sheep.

**How it ends.** Ask it exactly:

> **SAY:** "The light at the top of the Tor is the colour of a dream you have almost forgotten. Who goes up first?"

### Running the fights (Session 23)

**The same numbers for all of them:**
- **Maudrin (CR 8; AC 15, HP 110):** three Hush Bolts (+7, 17 Psychic each). Save DC 15. Silence and Hold Person, twice a day. Counterspell and Shield, twice. **Dimension Door to leave, at 55.**
- **A Warden (AC 16, HP 82):** two mauls (+5, 12, pushed 10 feet), or two crossbow bolts (+4, 13). Stone: no spells within 10 feet. AC 12, HP 10; or Sleight of Hand DC 15.
- **Haldric (AC 18, HP 55):** two longswords (+5). Can't be Frightened. The first spell each day that targets only him fails.
- **A Knight (AC 18, HP 52):** two swords (+5, 10 and 4 Radiant each).
- **A Sergeant (AC 15, HP 52):** two blows or shots (+4, about 5, and 3 Necrotic with the glove), or one and a signal: an ally attacks with a d4 extra.
- **A Hushman (AC 12, HP 32):** a glove (+5, 5 and 2 Necrotic), or an arrest (Strength DC 12 or Grappled).

#### The Strike (Haldric still commands)

*Maudrin, Haldric, two Knights, three Wardens, two Sergeants, four Hushmen. Take off whatever the ambush broke. Stock spoiled at the Quiet Quarry: the Wardens' stones reach only 5 feet.*

- **Setup:** the Court at the bottom of the road, in a line sixty feet wide. Maudrin behind it on her horse. The party thirty to sixty feet up the slope. Hushmen mounted at both ends.
- **What happens:** Round one: Silence on the biggest caster; the Wardens walk up the hill. Round two: the Hushmen come round the ends. Haldric calls out the strongest fighter by name and won't be drawn off.
- **Allies:** one company acts at the end of each round. Their player says what it looks like.
- **Who to worry about:** casters in the Silence. Say, "You can walk out of it."
- **When it ends:** Maudrin's Bloodied, captured or killed; or the Key's taken; or they give in.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out a Warden and a Sergeant. For a harder one, add a Warden.
- **Non-lethal:** the Court arrests. Knocking Maudrin out is worth a great deal.

#### The Strike (Rook won the duel)

*Maudrin, four Wardens, three Sergeants, eight Hushmen. No knights on their side. Rook's are on the ridge. Stock spoiled at the Quiet Quarry: the Wardens' stones reach only 5 feet.*

- **Setup:** the same, but the Wardens make a ring round Maudrin, stones showing. There's no casting at her from close in until one's shut.
- **What happens:** the Hushmen go for the casters, two to each. The Sergeants call it.
- **When it ends:** Maudrin's Bloodied; or she's captured or the Key's taken, and then every Hushman on the field gives up.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out a Warden and two Sergeants. For a harder one, add a Warden.

#### The Strike (Tristan's column)

*Only if Maudrin's dead. Tristan (AC 14, HP 38), Haldric, two Knights, six Wardens, three Sergeants, ten Hushmen. Stock spoiled at the Quiet Quarry: the Wardens' stones reach only 5 feet.*

- **Setup:** he's right at the back by the wagons, writing. The Wardens come on in twos.
- **What happens:** he sends them forward in pairs and doesn't come himself. He's counting.
- **When it ends:** he yields, or the Key's taken. Reaching him is most of it: when somebody does, he puts his hands up.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out two Wardens and the Knights. (If Rook won, leave out Haldric and the Knights anyway.)

#### Kessa intercepts

*Only if the oath's broken. Thane Oru Kessa (AC 17, HP 136), an Ironhorn Champion (AC 16, HP 112), five Riders (AC 18, HP 65), five Warhorses. It's on top of the Strike, so bring them in late: round three if the party's doing well, or when Maudrin's already gone.*

- **What happens:** Kessa rides straight at whoever's got the Heart. The Champion wants the best fighter. The Riders make a ring and don't close it.
- **Kessa's turn:** three greataxes. Advantage while he's Bloodied. On a critical hit, 2d6 Lightning to both of them.
- **He's after the Court as well.** Half his Riders go through the Hushmen on the way.
- **When it ends:** he's Bloodied, or somebody gives the Heart back. All he wants is the Heart. He'll say so.
- **Scale on the fly:** leave out three Riders.
- **Non-lethal:** always, with Kessa, unless a player insists.

### If you get stuck (Session 23)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Maudrin asks one player a question** about something kind they did. She's read the file.
2. **An ally does their job,** right now, where it's needed.
3. **The light on the hill gets brighter,** and everyone on both sides looks up.

**If they won't fight and won't give in:** good. Let Maudrin wait. She's patient. Then the Hushmen start taking down the tents behind her, and it's clear she'll walk round them.

**If somebody wants to fight her alone:** she gets down off the horse. She thinks it's only manners.

**Names for Hushmen, knights and riders** (cross one off when you use it): Wray, Ser Cassel, Sergeant Norrey, Lambert, Petra, Beck, Ragna.
