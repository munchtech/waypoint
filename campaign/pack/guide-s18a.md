## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. One room, one man, one enormous idea. Corin Aldeth has waited three generations for people like the party, and he'll tell them everything, including the part that frightens him. It's mostly an argument. The fight's only there if they insist.*

### Before you start (Session 18A)

**Level:** four characters at level 7.

**Check the world state first.** Five facts change tonight:
- **Corin:** unmet. He makes his offer **once in the whole campaign.** If they've met him already, skip the speech and start from where they left it.
- **Which bells have they rung?** He'll ask. He can hear it on them.
- **Kessa:** if they killed him, the Bellwrights have heard, and Corin's much more careful.
- **Did anyone give a memory to the Tithe without a fight?** He'll be furious, briefly.
- **Wren Caddock:** if she's an ally, he'll ask after her. She's been selling his people's names to the Court, and he doesn't know.

**What this session is about.** Somebody is completely honest with them, and it's frightening. Corin has a way to save the world that costs one person their name. He isn't hiding it. He'd like them to tell him he's wrong.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Make the workshop the best room they've ever been in.
- Let them argue. Give Corin good answers, and let him lose one.
- Say the price out loud. Don't ask anyone to pay it tonight, or even to think about who would.
- End on the question: **"Will you help him finish the song?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Corin; five Heart-Hounds.
- **Maps:** a tavern taproom; under it, a long vault with a forge at one end and bells hanging at every height.
- **Props:** *Edda Thorne's Notes*: a few old-looking pages, tied with string. On the last one write, in small neat letters, *the whole name.*
- **A glass and a spoon.** When Corin makes a point, ring it.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Insight,** DC 12: he believes every word he says.
- **Influence** (Persuasion), DC 15, moves him off the Concord, and only if you offer him a better way.
- **Study,** Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation), DC 14, to understand the plaster casting.
- **The Notes:** Dexterity (Sleight of Hand), DC 15, to take them. Or a full day with a Forgery Kit, DC 13, to copy them. Or ask.
- **Counterspell:** when Corin sees someone cast a spell within 60 feet, he can use his Reaction. They make a Constitution save (DC 14) or the spell fails, and they keep the spell slot.
- **Deferred Toll:** once a day he can touch a willing Ringer, and the next Toll they pay comes an hour late. It never goes away.
- **A creature at 0 HP** can be knocked out instead of killed, when a melee attack brings it there.

### Opening recap (Session 18A)

> **SAY:** "You left the Ironhorn camp with *(Thrane's Heart, wrapped in a saddle blanket / empty hands and a plan / a promise)*. It's two days back to the Shafts, and the road goes past exactly one tavern. You can feel it before you can see it. It's in your teeth."

- **Then hand it over:** "What does your character want to hear, after a week of wind?" One answer each. Use them: there'll be a bell down there that makes that sound.

### Scene 1: The Dry Thunder

**Where and why.** A low stone tavern on the Brass Road, late in the day. It's a warm-up, and an audition.

> **SAY:** "It's a low stone room with a fire in it, and it's quiet, the way people are quiet when they're listening for something. Every cup on every shelf is humming, so softly you could miss it. When you come in, the hum goes up by one note. The old woman behind the bar stops wiping a glass."

**What's really going on.** The cups are tuned to the bells downstairs, and the bells can hear a Ringer. Mother Ash knows what they are before they've sat down. She'd like to know who they are.

**Mother Ash.** Slow, floury, deaf in one ear on purpose. *Lines:* "You'll want something hot." · "Put it down soft, love. Soft." · "Which one was it? No, don't tell me. I'll only cry in the soup."

**If they…**
- **Have rung a bell:** the drinks are free. She lifts the hatch in the bar. "He's been waiting since before he was born."
- **Haven't:** they're a Heart's carriers, and that's enough. She asks them one thing: "Did you ever wear the gloves?" Anyone who's worn Hushman gloves gets a cold look, and goes down last.
- **Ask what the hum is:** she puts a spoon against a cup and the whole shelf answers, one after another, like a dog's hackles.
- **Won't go down:** he comes up. He's too excited to wait.

**Bring it back.** Someone puts a cup down hard. Every cup in the room rings, and forty people hold their breath.

**How it ends.** The hatch, a ladder, and heat.

### Scene 2: The workshop

**Where and why.** Under the tavern. Give them time to walk about.

> **SAY (Corin Aldeth):** "Ah, you've been to Brassfall. Good. I was hoping you'd come this way. My name is Corin Aldeth. I'm a Bellwright. My family has tried to finish a song for three generations, and I think you may be the first people in a century who can help me finish it."

**What's really going on.** He's thin, burned, and humming. He's listening to them with one ear turned. He wants to show them everything, and he starts in the middle.

**Corin.** *Lines:* "You've rung one. I can hear it on you. Which bell? Tell me what it sounded like." · "Ask me plainly. Anything about the bells. I'll answer straight; it's the Court that lies." · "My grandmother rang the first handbell at Vael Tor. They called her mad. She was only early." · If somebody gave a memory to the Tithe without a fight: "You handed it over? Without a fight? Then you paid them to forget you."

**Go round the table: "What does your character touch first?"** Anything they touch is a bell with a story:
- **A thimble-sized one** that makes the sound they asked for in the recap.
- **A cracked one on the back wall:** *Wenna Aldeth, Vael Tor.* He doesn't let anyone ring it. He lets them hold it.
- **One with a child's teeth-marks** in the wax. "Odile. She was two. It's the best tone we have."
- **One in a Hushman's helmet,** upside down. "We use what they leave."

**If they…**
- **[Look at the plaster casting on the middle bench:](play:s18a-explore-workshop)** Study, Arcana or Investigation DC 14. It's the shape of the Tuning Key, taken from the Notes, and the lathe is set up for a copy he's never dared finish. A copy wouldn't work. "The Seventh would know."
- **Scratch the big hound:** it rolls over. It rings, very low. That's Peal, and Corin's more pleased than he can say.
- **Are carrying silence-stone:** four hounds stand up at once. "Put that down, please. Gently. It hurts them."
- **[Want to make a bell:](play:any-become-bellwrights)** anyone with Smith's Tools can cast a small ordinary one tonight, with Fen and Odile to help. Ask what they scratch inside it.
- **Say they killed Kessa:** he stops humming. "He came here once. He wanted to know if it would hurt." He'll go on talking. He won't turn his back.

**Bring it back.** He takes the wool out of one bell, lets it ring once, and puts the wool back. Nobody speaks for a moment.

**How it ends.** He clears a bench with his forearm, and unties some string.

### Scene 3: The Notes

**Where and why.** The bench. Put the prop on the table.

> **SAY:** "The pages are brittle and close-written in a careful hand. 'Edda Thorne,' Corin says. 'Warden of the Bellwright Tower, the night it went quiet. She wrote down everything the High Sexton told her, including the things he shouldn't have. We have kept these for three generations, and we have never used them. None of us trusted the others enough.'"

**What's really going on.** The Notes describe the **Concord,** the one safe way to deal with the Seventh. Let Corin explain it in four plain sentences. This is the plan the rest of the campaign may follow, so make sure they get it:

> **SAY (Corin):** "Six bells, rung, each in its own voice. Together they hold the lullaby, the way six people can hold up a roof. While they hold it, the Key is seated in the Seventh. Seated. Never struck."

**If they ask…**
- **What happens if it's struck?** "Then you wake what's under it, or you crack what holds it. She says both. She underlines both."
- **Who has the Key?** "The Court. Maudrin Vesk. She keeps it as if it were a *lamp*."
- **What does it fix?** "The dusk. The winters. The forgetting. The world is a song that stopped in the middle. I want to hear the end."
- **What's under it?** "Oum. The Dreamer. We are what it's dreaming. Don't look like that. You've always known you were somebody's."
- **What's the leaf pinned to the back?** The Quiet Night leaf. It's in Edda's own shorthand and it'll take time to read. It belongs to [Session 22's truth](play:s22-read-notes). If they've got somebody very clever and you'd rather have it now, read it now.
- **How did your grandmother die?** "She rang one note of it, by herself, to see. It was beautiful. She was a week dying, and she couldn't tell us who we were."

**[Copying or stealing them:](play:s18a-copy-notes)** Sleight of Hand DC 15, or a day with a Forgery Kit, DC 13. If it fails, he catches them at it. He's hurt, not angry. "You could have *asked.*" He reads them the leaf's first line and keeps the rest.

**Bring it back.** Odile reads over somebody's shoulder, out loud, and gets a word right that Corin's had wrong for years.

**How it ends.** Somebody turns over the last page, and there it is in small neat letters: *the whole name.*

### Scene 4: The price

**Where and why.** The same bench, quieter. He sends the apprentices upstairs.

> **SAY (Corin):** "Yes. There's a cost. There's always a cost. The Notes ask the Ringer for 'the whole name'. For three generations my family read that as a bell-name. A title, you understand. An honour." *(He stops humming.)* "I think it means yours. I am sorry."

**What's really going on.** [He's honest about it.](play:s18a-argue-with-corin) He doesn't know whether it means the name in everybody's memory, or on the page, or both. (It's everyone's memory. You know that; he doesn't.) He isn't asking for a volunteer. **You aren't either.** The Great Toll is asked in private, of one player, much later, and they can always say no.

> **SAY (Corin, if they push):** "You think I do not know how it sounds? My grandmother rang a bell once. She forgot her sister's face for a week and called that the price. She was wrong. It was only the first line."

**If they argue, here's what he says:**
- **"That's monstrous."** "Yes. The Court takes it from everybody, in small pieces, for ever. I want to take it from one person, once, who said yes."
- **"What if you're wrong about the Dreamer?"** "Then I've cracked the only thing that's holding. I think about it every night. I'm asking for six bells first *because* I think about it."
- **"Would you pay it?"** He doesn't hesitate. "If it'd take me. I've never rung one of the Seven. I don't know that it knows me." (Let that sit. It matters in Session 27.)
- **"Is there another way?"** "Find me one. Please."
- **"Can you make it cheaper?"** Deferred Toll: an hour late, never less. He's used it once, for somebody who wanted to say goodbye properly.

Influence, Persuasion DC 15, and only with a real alternative: he'll agree not to act until they've tried it.

**Bring it back.** Put down the spoon. Ask everyone, in character, to say their own whole name out loud. Corin listens to each one with his head on one side, as if it were a bell.

**How it ends.** "I don't need an answer about *that*. I need to know whether you'll help me get as far as the door."

### Scene 5: The answer

**Where and why.** Wherever they're standing.

**If they…**
- **[Help him:](play:s18a-meet-corin)** he gives them the Notes. He just hands them over, and then doesn't know what to do with his hands. He's an ally; so are his people.
- **[Refuse:](play:s18a-refuse-corin)** he lets them go up the ladder. Then:

> **SAY:** "Corin looks disappointed rather than angry. Somewhere under the workshop, something bronze begins to pace."

- **A hound follows them** from here on. It doesn't hide well: it rings. It won't attack. It'll be sitting outside the tent in the morning.
- **Say "not yet":** that's a fair answer. He's unmet still for the purposes of the record; leave the fact as it is. He'll find them at the Tor.
- **[Attack:](play:s18a-fight-corin)** see the fights. He stops the moment anyone says they've rung a bell.
- **[Kill him:](play:s18a-kill-corin)** ask first. "A Bloodied Corin puts his hammer down. Are you sure?"

> **SAY:** "The tuning hammer rings once on the stone floor, a clean note, and nobody picks it up."

- **Tell him about Wren:** he sits down on an upturned bell. He knew there was somebody. He asks what she was promised. He forgives her out loud and it takes him three tries.

**Bring it back.** One bell, on the top shelf, rings by itself. It's the one that makes the sound the player asked for.

**How it ends.** Ask: **"Will you help him finish the song?"** Let every player answer, even if the party's already agreed. If they don't all agree, that's how Session 19 starts.

### Running the fights (Session 18A)

#### The Bellwright ambush

*Five Heart-Hounds (AC 15, 58 HP each), and Corin (AC 14, 81 HP) if he's forced. Optional, and easy to stop.*

- **Setup:** the workshop. Bells hang everywhere at head height; the ladder's at one end and the forge at the other.
- **What happens:** the hounds bite and ring. Whoever's bitten makes it impossible to hide for anyone near them. They herd intruders toward the forge, away from the bells. Corin shouts at everybody to stop.
- **A hound's turn:** two bites (+5; 7 plus 3 Thunder each), or a peal, if it has one: a 15-foot cone, Constitution DC 12, 17 Thunder or half. It gets that back on a 5 or 6. They have Advantage when a packmate's next to their target.
- **Corin's turn, if it comes to it:** three struck notes, up to 120 feet (+6, 16 Thunder each), or Shatter (DC 14). He Counterspells. **He never strikes first at anyone who's rung a bell.**
- **Who to worry about:** anyone who's alone with three hounds. Corin's three notes are 48 damage if they all land; aim them at different people.
- **The room:** anything that swings wide hits a bell. Every time, everyone hears it, all the way up into the tavern.
- **When it ends:** somebody says they've rung a bell; or Corin's Bloodied (40 HP or fewer), and calls them off; or they're up the ladder.
- **Scale on the fly:** without Corin it's lighter. Three hounds and Corin is lighter still.
- **Non-lethal:** hounds at 0 HP go still and can be re-tuned. Corin can be knocked out. He'll wake up apologising.

- **Where to break:** when he unties the string.
- **Running long:** cut the tavern to its read-aloud and the hatch.
- **Running short:** cast a bell together. Ask what it'll be used for.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop at "the whole name." Put the spoon down.

### If you get stuck (Session 18A)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **A bell rings** that nobody's touched, and Corin writes down the time.
2. **Peal brings somebody a file,** like a stick.
3. **Boots in the tavern above.** Mother Ash drops a tray: that's the signal for Hushmen. Everyone has to be quiet, in a room full of bells, for one minute. Time it.

**If the argument's going in circles:** Fen comes back down for a forgotten lamp and asks, "Who are they going to ask to do it?" Nobody has to answer.

**Names for Bellwrights** (cross one off when you use it): Old Dunstan, Merrily Roe, the cooper Hadd, Sister Tin, Jory Link, Aunt Verity, Two-Fingers Pell.

### End of session 18A

**The closing question:** "Will you help him finish the song?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the workshop, the Notes, the argument, the answer.
- **World-state facts to check:** Corin (ally, refused, enemy, dead), the Notes (party or Bellwrights), the Bellwrights' Clock.
- **Bargain log:** anything they promised him, and anything he promised back.
- **A private note for you:** how each player reacted to the price. You'll ask one of them, in Act V, and you'll want to know.

**Rewards:**
- **Edda Thorne's Notes,** if given, copied or taken.
- **The Bellwrights,** if he's an ally: safe cellars, and Deferred Toll once a day.
- **A small ordinary bell,** for anyone who cast one.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to whoever argued best, on either side.
