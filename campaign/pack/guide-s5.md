## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. Tonight is a heist with a library in front of it. The clues are free: the diary is always found once they're in the vault, and the roll only decides how fast. The new rule is silence-stone at full strength: inside the vault no spell works at all.*

### Before you start (Session 5)

**Level:** four characters at level 3.

**Check the world state first.** Four facts in Foresight:
- **Courtesy memories:** who gave one at the gate. Each has a jar on the vault's newest shelf, near their own oldest jar.
- **Ossa's Heart:** if it's in the Court vault, it's on a shelf tonight ([taking it](play:s05-take-ossa-from-vault) sets off the alarm). If it's in the Dawn Order armory, that's Session 6's business.
- **What the Court knows of the party:** "Named enemies" means the keepers are watching Lysse already; Ansel is more frightened and more expensive.
- **The Tithe Clock:** +1 for each night so far in the city. At 6, the sweep begins (see Session 9).

**What this session is about.** A nervous scholar in the Bone Archive has spent years looking for Edda Thorne's diary. An ancient mind in a wordless book knows where it went and what it was labelled. The party breaks into the Court's vault, a room of memories in glass jars, sealed against every sound. They find the truth about the Quiet Night, a bell's Heart, the Court's master ledger, and a jar with their own name on it.

**Your goals tonight:**
- The players find the diary (always) and read the Quiet Night entry aloud.
- Each player who wants to finds their own jar, and decides whether to open it.
- They choose what to carry out, knowing that some things set off the alarm.
- End on: "What did the Court take from the diary?"

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Lysse; Ansel Quay; a wordless book for the Librarian; four Hushmen and a Hushman Sergeant (the alarm).
- **Maps:** the Archive is a tall nave of shelves, a reading room and a stair to the Sealed Stacks. The vault is one long room lined with silence-stone: shelves of jars down both walls, a reading stand at the far end, one door, and a service passage to the kitchens.
- **Props:**
  - *A jar label* for each character: their name in the Court's hand, and "earliest memory, sealed." Hand it over when they find their shelf.
  - *Edda's diary entry:* the box is in the plan above. Read it slowly. Don't summarise it.
  - *A small cloth-wrapped book* labelled MIREL, LOST.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Silence-stone, the whole room.** No spell can be cast in the vault or reach into it. A fixed stone (AC 12, 10 HP) can be broken, which is loud. Casters inside fight with what's in their hands.
- **Jars.** Opening your own jar restores the memory: it was a Tithe, so it comes back. Opening someone else's only lets you witness it, and it fades; after three strangers it's spent. Smashing a jar scatters a memory; it doesn't restore it.
- **The alarm.** Taking Calla's Heart or the original Ledger rings a bell-pull. Hushmen arrive in three rounds.
- **Librarian trades** are their own kind in the Memory Ledger: not a Tithe, not a Toll. They don't come back.
- **Study:** an Intelligence check to find something fast. A failure costs time, never the clue.

**Places at a glance:**
- **The Bone Archive:** a cathedral of bone-bound books. Some still have a faint pulse.
- **The Sealed Stacks:** uncatalogued and not silence-stone. Spells work there, but they hum loudly, and the Librarian hears them.
- **The Tithe-Ledger Room (the vault):** silence-stone throughout. Jars, the Ledger on its stand, one door, a service passage.
- **Bet's bakery:** warm, safe, two streets from the Archive. A good place to read the diary afterwards.

**Rumours (d6), from the Archive's keepers, clerks and copyists:**
1. "Lysse Carrow asks about Edda Thorne too loudly. The keepers have noticed." (True.)
2. "The steward Ansel counts the vault every morning, and he's started counting twice." (True.)
3. "Some of the jars are labelled with names nobody in the city has." (True: the export column.)
4. "If you read the wordless book, it shows you your own face." (True.)
5. "Hushmen never go up into the Sealed Stacks. They say it's beneath them." (Half true: they're afraid of what reads up there.)

### Opening recap (Session 5)

> **SAY:** "Last time, you gave the Gate Office a memory each *(or: refused it, and say what that cost)*, and at the masque the Chancellor spoke to every one of you by name. *(Say how they answered her bargain, in one line.)* You walked back through the lamp-lit lanes knowing you were followed. This morning, a note in a nervous hand: the Bone Archive, the reading room, come alone if you can, together if you must."

- **Name anything given at the gate,** in the player's own words. It can come back tonight.
- **Then hand it over:** "The Archive's doors are open. Who goes in first?"

### Scene 1: Lysse Carrow

**Where and why.** The Archive's reading room, morning. Read the arrival box from the plan above first.

> **SAY:** "A woman with a bone pin in her hair and a satchel of books slightly too heavy for her waves you over, then looks round to see who saw. 'You're from the Mere. You've seen a bell. I've read every book in this building, and I've found one line about Edda Thorne's notes. It says they're kept where no bell can hear them. There's only one place in Hallowmere sealed against sound.'"

**What's really going on.** Lysse is right: the diary is in the vault. She doesn't know it's been mislabelled, or that the Librarian upstairs knows the label. She's also a Tithe victim herself, and doesn't know where her own memories went.

**Lysse.** Excitable, precise, cites sources, trails off when frightened. *Wants:* to read the diary. *Lines:* "According to the Second Index, no, sorry, the Third." · "Hushmen don't come in here. Usually." · "Can you bring me back a copy? Just a copy."

**What they can learn here:**
- **Where the diary is:** the vault. Free.
- **How to get in:** Lysse knows that Ansel Quay holds the key and can be bought, and that the clerks wear grey smocks. [Posing as clerks](play:s05-clerks-and-lysse): Deception DC 15 each with a Disguise Kit; Lysse gives Advantage.
- **The Sealed Stacks:** she's never been allowed up. "Something up there reads to itself."

**Bring it back.** A keeper in a bone mask walks past the end of the table, slowly. Lysse stops talking until he's gone.

**How it ends.** They go up to the Stacks, or straight to Ansel.

### Scene 2: The Librarian of the Sealed Book

**Where and why.** The top of the Archive, in the dark, where a book lies open on a stand. Its pages are blank. Each reader sees their own face in them.

> **SAY:** "Words write themselves across the empty page in a hand like frost on glass. *You have come with a question that has a memory behind it. Ask, and I will answer. The diary is not on any shelf of mine. It went into the sealed room, and it was labelled as something else. Ask me the label, and I will tell you, for one true memory.*"

**What's really going on.** The Librarian answers one question for each true memory a player gives. It also holds Mirel's Heart, and will trade it for a dream the giver has never told anyone.

**The Librarian.** Writes, never speaks. Patient, exact, a little sad. *Lines:* "*Every book is a memory someone agreed to lose.*" · "*That is a true memory. Thank you.*" · "*You may take it. You may not keep it.*"

**What they can learn here:**
- **[The label:](play:s05-ask-the-label)** MIREL, LOST, for one true memory. Or Lysse finds it with a Study check (Intelligence (Investigation) DC 15) in an hour, or by morning on a failure. The clue is never lost.
- **[The Mirel trade:](play:s05-mirel-bargain)** the Heart, for a dream the player has never told anyone. Let the player describe it, then it's gone. A Gift will do instead. Neither is Mirel's Toll: that's still owed when the bell rings.
- **[Stealing it:](play:s05-steal-mirel)** Stealth or Thieves' Tools DC 16. The book wakes; see the Librarian below.

**Bring it back.** A page turns by itself, slowly, and the face in it is whoever hasn't spoken yet.

**How it ends.** They have the label, and perhaps a Heart. Ask: "Tonight, or tomorrow?"

### Scene 3: Getting into the vault

**Where and why.** The vault door, deep in the palace's service wing. One door, one lock, one steward.

**If they…**
- **[Bribe Ansel:](play:s05-bribe-ansel)** Influence DC 15; a Gift or a promise of safety makes him Friendly. He lets them in without a roll and may sell his copy of the Ledger. Intimidation works, and costs Wren's trust. [Killing him](play:s05-kill-ansel) closes the vault for good.
- **[Break in:](play:s05-break-into-vault)** Thieves' Tools DC 17 on the lock, Stealth DC 15 past the guardroom.
- **Go in as clerks:** see Scene 1.

> **SAY:** "The door closes behind you and the world goes out like a candle. No echo. No breath. Your own footsteps are something you see, not hear. The jars are cool, and they hum faintly as you pass, as if they're trying to remember the tune they used to belong to."

**Bring it back.** Someone's lantern gutters in a draft from nowhere. The silence feels closer.

**How it ends.** They're in. Go straight to the jars.

### Scene 4: The jars

**What's in the vault** (let them search, and give them each thing they look for):
- **[Edda Thorne's diary:](play:s05-take-diary)** the cloth-wrapped book labelled MIREL, LOST. Free if they know the label; Wisdom (Perception) DC 12 if not, and a failure only costs time. Read the entry aloud from the plan above. [Reading it and leaving it](play:s05-read-diary-and-leave) means the Court never knows.
- **[Their own jar:](play:s05-open-own-jar)** labelled with their name, holding their earliest memory, sealed and warm. Opening it restores it. Ask the player to describe it, and give them the time. The courtesy jars are on the newest shelf.
- **[Calla's Heart:](play:s05-take-calla)** on the HARVEST, WITHHELD shelf, warm in its cloth. Taking it sets off the alarm. A weight-swap (Sleight of Hand DC 15) only delays it.
- **[The Tithe-Ledger:](play:s05-take-ledger)** on the stand. Study DC 15 finds Haldric's armory payments in a minute, or ten minutes with no roll. Taking the original sets off the alarm. Copying the page doesn't.
- **[The export column:](play:s05-export-column)** a column of jars bound for somewhere that isn't Hallowmere. Anyone who reads the ledger notices. Someone is selling memories abroad.
- **[Other people's jars:](play:s05-free-other-jars)** they can carry them out, but only the owner can restore one. Lysse's jars are under other names (Study DC 17).

> **SAY (a character opens their own jar):** "The glass is warm. When the seal breaks there's no sound, of course, but there's a smell, and then you're somewhere else, and you're very small, and someone is…" *(Stop. Let the player finish it.)*

**This is a heavy moment if a player wants it to be.** Let them speak. No joke from you. Mark it in Waypoint if it lands.

**[If the alarm goes:](play:s05-alarm-no-take)** two Hushmen at the door in round 3, more in round 4. Run the vault alarm below.

**Bring it back.** A jar on the shelf nearest the door starts to hum louder. Footsteps in the corridor, seen through the gap under the door as moving shadow.

**How it ends.** They're out through the door or the service passage, carrying what they chose. Ask the closing question.

### Running the fights (Session 5)

#### The vault alarm

*Four Hushmen and a Hushman Sergeant. Only if the alarm sounds.*
- **Setup:** two Hushmen reach the vault door in round 3; two more and the Sergeant in round 4.
- **Each round:** they block the doors and grapple rather than swing among the jars. No spells work inside, so casters must reach the corridor first.
- **When it ends:** the service passage is the way out, and the Hushmen won't follow past the kitchens.
- **Scale on the fly:** escape only: remove the Sergeant. At level 4: add two Hushmen.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** surrender means a cell, not a Tithe. They never break a jar to stop a thief.

#### The Librarian

*Only if they try to steal Mirel's Heart.*
- **Setup:** the book doesn't move. Its pages cut like blades (the Knight's Greatsword, as radiant damage: written light). It uses Parry against hands reaching for the Heart.
- **Each round:** it targets only whoever is holding the Heart or the book.
- **When it ends:** the moment no one is touching either. Closing the book (Strength DC 15, an action) ends it. It never pursues past the Stacks' door.
- **Scale on the fly:** don't add creatures. The keepers arrive in ten minutes if the fight is loud.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** it stops at 0 HP and writes, *"You may come back."* It's cold to them afterwards.

### Pacing (Session 5)

| Time | What's happening |
|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:15 | Recap; check the four facts |
| 0:15 to 0:45 | Scene 1: Lysse in the reading room |
| 0:45 to 1:30 | Scene 2: the Librarian and the Mirel trade |
| 1:30 to 2:00 | Scene 3: Ansel, or the lock |
| 2:00 to 3:10 | Scene 4: the jars, the diary, the choice of what to carry |
| 3:10 to 3:30 | Out, or the alarm; the closing question |

- **Where to break:** after the Librarian, before they decide how to get into the vault. Planning the heist over a break is half the fun.
- **Running long:** let Lysse find the label by morning and skip the Librarian; Mirel's Heart waits for a second visit (Session 9).
- **Running short:** let them read the diary to Lysse at Bet's bakery afterwards, and play her face as she hears it.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop at the vault door, as it closes. Next session opens inside.

### If you get stuck (Session 5)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Lysse cites a source.** "The Third Index says the steward's a coward. I think it means kind." Points them at Ansel.
2. **A jar hums their name.** In the vault, a jar near them is louder than the rest: theirs.
3. **The keepers come by.** A bone-masked keeper with a lamp. "The Archive closes at the second bell. There isn't one. Close anyway."

**Names for the Archive** (cross one off when you use it):
- **Keepers:** Keeper Mabyn, Keeper Lowe, Keeper Ashgrave.
- **Clerks:** Pell Tanner, Dora Quist, little Simeon Vey.
- **Names on jars:** Hollis Brant, Mae Cotter, Joss Fairweather, Ruth Ainsley, a jar marked only "the Pellmoor ferry, 410."

### End of session 5

**The closing question:** "What did the Court take from the diary?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember from tonight."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the Librarian play (label, trade or theft), the entry play (Ansel, the lock, or clerks), and each thing they took or opened.
- **World-state facts to check:** Edda Thorne's diary, Calla's Heart, Mirel's Heart, Ossa's Heart, Ansel Quay, what the Court knows of the party, the Tithe jars, and the Tithe Clock.
- **Memory ledger:** restored courtesy memories and earliest memories (mark the Tithe as returned); any Librarian trade, as its own kind.

**Rewards:**
- **Level:** none yet.
- **The diary:** the true account of the Quiet Night, and the first hint of the Concord.
- **The Ledger, or Ansel's copy:** the evidence that can break Haldric in Session 6.
- **Coin:** the vault holds none. Ansel's copy costs them; a promise of safety is cheaper than gold, and binding.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to each player who opened their own jar and played the memory, and to whoever gave the Librarian a true dream.
