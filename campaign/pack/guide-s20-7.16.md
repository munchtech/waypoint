## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. It's the other half of the story, told fast: the place they didn't go first. One arrival, one bargain, one Heart. Everything here was a whole session on the other road, so pick the best scene of each and let the rest happen on the way.*

### Before you start (Session 20)

**Level:** four characters at level 8.

**Check the world state first.** Five facts decide the night:
- **Act IV road:** if they went to **Brassfall first (A)**, tonight's **Vael Tor**. If they went to **Vael Tor first (B)**, tonight's **Brassfall**. Cross out the half of each scene you don't need. That's most of your preparation.
- **The Heart they're here for:** Thrane's (Brassfall) or Mirel's (the Tor). If they've already got it, Scene 3 is a conversation instead of a bargain.
- **The lamp** (Brassfall only): nobody's, carried, or already with Yenna.
- **Corin:** if he's still **unmet**, he finds them tonight, in Scene 4. If he's an ally or refused, skip him.
- **Mirel** (the Tor only): if she's rung, nothing walks at night.

**What this session is about.** They've done this once. They know what a Heart costs now. What'll they pay when they can see the bill coming?

**Your goals tonight:**
- Get them across the plain, and show them what the first ringing changed.
- Run one real bargain, with a real cost, and let them choose it.
- Have them leave with the Heart, or with a clear reason why not.
- End on the question: **"What did the second Heart cost you?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **The other road's guide.** For Brassfall, skim Sessions 16A and 17A. For the Tor, skim 16B. Put a bookmark in the one scene you liked best, and run that one properly.
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters. **Brassfall:** two Champions, five Riders, five Warhorses. **The Tor:** four Sleepless.
- **Maps:** the Heart-Hall and the fires round it; or the hillside and the ring of stones.
- **For Brassfall:** something metal you can put down as a stake. **For the Tor:** a notebook with no words in it.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Influence:** they say what they want; you decide if the person's Willing, Hesitant or Unwilling. Only Hesitant needs a roll.
- **Help:** anyone proficient can give a friend Advantage on one check.
- **An oath is a rule at this table.** If Kessa's loan is sworn, write the exact words down. It's checked next session.
- **A traded dream's gone.** The player makes it up, says it once, and the character doesn't have it any more.
- **Sleeping on Vael Tor (if Mirel's unrung):** everyone who sleeps makes a Wisdom save, DC 13. A failure is a nightmare; failing by 5 or more brings one Sleepless (one a night at most). Whoever stays on watch doesn't roll.
- **Passing unseen through a camp:** Stealth, DC 15 each. One careful plan is worth Advantage.

### Opening recap (Session 20)

> **SAY:** "Six bells can be rung in Corvane, and you've stood under *(number)* of them. The last one was *(Thrane's, in the Lightning Shafts / Mirel's, in the Sleeping Hall)*. You paid *(the Toll, in one line)*, or you walked away. The Court's grey riders came for you afterwards, and they know your faces now. There's one more bell to ring before the Seventh. It's three days across the plain, and it's getting dark earlier."

- **Then hand it over:** "What's the one thing your character's doing differently, this time?" One line each. Hold them to it.

### Scene 1: Across the plain

**Where and why.** Three days on the road. Fifteen minutes. It's here so the players can see what they did.

> **SAY:** "You can tell where the bell reached. There's a line across the country, as if somebody'd drawn it. On one side of it people look the way they've always looked. On the other side they keep stopping, in the middle of things, with their heads up."

**Show them one thing the first ringing changed,** from your notes:
- **Thrane rang:** it's raining, properly, for the first time in a hundred years. Children have been let out in it. Somebody's mending a lightning rod and swearing happily.
- **Mirel rang:** people have slept. They're sitting on walls in the sun telling each other what they dreamed. Somebody asks the party, shyly, if they'd like to hear one.
- **Neither rang:** it's quiet, and there's less of everything than there should be.

**If they…**
- **Hurry:** they arrive a day ahead of the Court's news. Give them Advantage on the first Influence check tonight.
- **Stop and help somebody:** they arrive at dusk, with one more friend. Any name from the list.
- **Look behind:** Wisdom (Perception) DC 14. Two grey riders on the skyline, not closing. **They've been seen.** That's the start of Session 21, not tonight.
- **Want a fight:** there isn't one here. Say so kindly and move on.

**Bring it back.** Somebody on the road recognises the Ringer, and gives them an apple, and won't take money.

**How it ends.** The road goes up. Read the arrival, below.

### Scene 2: The other gate

**Where and why.** The place itself. One arrival, and the first person they meet.

#### If tonight's Brassfall

> **SAY:** "The mountains are veined with bronze, and the sky flickers without clouds. At the mouth of the Old Works, a blue lamp is waiting."

**What's really going on.** [They've come to Brassfall second.](play:s20-brassfall-second) Garrow's been holding that lamp up since he died. He only wants one thing, and it's on the way.

- **Old Garrow.** Gruff, impatient, funny. He calls everyone lads and lasses. *Lines:* "Lads and lasses! You took your time. Did you come the long way round, by the sea?" · "It's not heavy. It's only a lamp. It only wants carrying to somebody." · "Tell her I'm sorry about the collapse. Tell her I'm proud. In that order, and don't drop it."
- **[Run the lamp and Yenna in one scene.](play:s20-lamp-delivered-quick)** He asks, they say yes, and it's dusk at the red horse-skull:

> **SAY:** "Garrow's lamp is waiting at the mouth of the works, and over the next hill a red horse-skull."

- Let the player who carried it say the words. Yenna hangs it up inside, comes out, and offers one thing: a horse, a bow, or a word to her clan.
- **[Refuse him:](play:s16a-refuse-lamp)** he nods. He's been refused before. Yenna's still here, and she's hurt, not angry.

#### If tonight's Vael Tor

> **SAY:** "The hill rises alone above the plain. From the summit, a sound like someone breathing in their sleep drifts down toward you."

**What's really going on.** [They've come to the Tor second.](play:s20-vael-tor-second) The Hall's doors are never locked. Who'd leave?

- **Brother Lull,** if you like a guide: round, whispering, always folding something. *Lines:* "Oh, *shoes*, please. Thank you." · "You can talk. They like it. Just don't sing."
- **Let them walk the rows.** Ask each player for one sleeper they notice. One of them's somebody the party's heard of. [They can try to wake one:](play:s16b-wake-a-sleeper) it's a minute of somebody looking up at them, and then it's over.
- **If Mirel's already rung,** they've all turned over, and some of them are smiling.

**Bring it back.** Brassfall: the lamp leans toward whoever just told a lie. The Tor: everyone in one row breathes out together.

**How it ends.** Brassfall: "He'll see you now. He's decided to like you." The Tor: somebody's standing by the empty bed, with a book.

### Scene 3: One bargain

**Where and why.** This is the session. Give it an hour, and make the price real.

#### If tonight's Brassfall: the thane

**Thane Oru Kessa.** Laughs with his whole chest; tucks his gloved hands in his belt. Skip the contest unless they ask for it. He's heard about them.

> **SAY (Kessa, in private):** "I'm dying, rider. Ring it, and return it after."

**What's really going on.** The Heart's been eating his hands. He believes the Key can cure him, because Isolde sold him a memory that said so. It can't. He can't ring Thrane himself, because the Shafts mark whoever rings, and he wouldn't live through the first stroke.

**If they…**
- **[Take the loan:](play:s20-accept-kessa-quick)** say the oath aloud. It comes back to the Heart-Hall. Ask each player whether they're swearing it. Write it in the Bargain log.
- **[Take it by night:](play:s20-take-thrane-quick)** one plan, one night. If Yenna's a friend she does the carrying, and all they need is a distraction. Otherwise Stealth, DC 15 each.

> **SAY:** "Drums at midnight. A rider yawns, sees nothing, looks away."

  He'll know by morning. Tell them that before they go in.
- **[Duel him for it:](play:s17a-duel-kessa)** "Finally." He fights fair and stops at Bloodied.
- **Try to take it openly:** see the fights. He'll stop it for a fair wager or a yielded blade.
- **Tell him the Key won't cure him:** he goes quiet, and then he asks who else knows. He still wants it rung.
- **Offer him something else:** what have they got that he'd believe? A Gift from Isolde's vial, or a way to heal the mark, or a promise about Maudrin. Let a good offer work.

#### If tonight's Vael Tor: the book

> **SAY:** "A figure in archivist's grey stands by the central bed, holding a bone-bound book that has no words. 'A dream you have never told, and the Heart is yours.'"

**What's really going on.** [It's the same offer it always makes.](play:s20-courier-bargain-quick) It wants to be read, and to read. It doesn't hurry.

**If they…**
- **Trade a dream:** the player makes it up and says it, once. Then it's gone. Put it in the ledger as a Trade.
- **Trade a Gift instead:** it'll take one.
- **[Say no to all of it:](play:s16b-refuse-courier)** it closes the book, and stays. Mirel won't ring, and that's allowed.
- **[Take it:](play:s16b-take-from-courier)** it opens its hand. Say first that the Librarian will shut every door it has.
- **Ask it things:** one true memory, one true answer. It knows where Maudrin is. ("Packing a small bag. She has taken the Key out of its case to look at it, twice.")

**Bring it back.** Brassfall: he misses the cup with his hand, and waits to see who'll notice. The Tor: the book turns a page, and the page has tonight on it.

**How it ends.** They've got it, or they haven't, and somebody's a little poorer. **Say the cost out loud:** "So that's what it cost."

### Scene 4: The night before

**Where and why.** A fire. This is either Corin's scene, or a quiet one. Don't run both.

#### If Corin's unmet

He comes into the firelight with his hands where they can see them.

> **SAY (Corin):** "Ah, you have been to the mountains. Good."

*(At the Tor, "to the hill".)* Thin, in a scorched apron, with his head on one side and a tuning hammer through his belt. He hums until he's upset.

**What's really going on.** He's been a day behind them for a week. He's got Edda Thorne's Notes, which his family has kept for three generations, and they describe a way to finish the song: six bells rung, the Key *seated* in the Seventh and never struck. It asks the Ringer for "the whole name". He thinks that means theirs. He'll say so without being asked.

- **[Say yes:](play:s20-corin-accept-here)** he hands over the Notes, all of them, and doesn't know what to do with his arms.
- **[Say no:](play:s20-corin-refuse-here)** he's disappointed, not angry. In the morning there's a bronze hound sitting a hundred feet off. It rings a little when it scratches.
- **[Argue with him:](play:s18a-argue-with-corin)** he'd love that. He's had nobody to argue with.
- **Say "ask us again":** that's fair. Leave the fact as unmet. He'll come to the Tor.

#### If he isn't

- **Brassfall:** the clan's dancing, and somebody has to dance. Or Yenna comes and sits by the fire and doesn't say anything for a while, and then tells one story about her father.
- **The Tor, if Mirel's unrung:** they've got to decide where to sleep. [On the hill,](play:s16b-rest-on-tor) with the saves; or in [the Cult's circle,](play:s17b-accept-rest) at the Cult's price; or down on the road, cold and safe. If something walks, see the fights.
- **The Tor, if she's rung:** everyone dreams. Go round: one image each.

**Bring it back.** One ember in the fire is exactly the colour of the Heart. It takes a long time to go out.

**How it ends.** Whoever's on last watch hears the bell hum. Once. It's heard them.

### Scene 5: The bell you haven't met

**Where and why.** Morning. Five minutes, walking toward it. **Don't ring it tonight.**

> **SAY:** "You don't have to look for the way. The Heart's pulling, like a child that's seen somebody it knows. And then you're over the last rise, and there it is, hanging in its own quiet, and it's leaning toward you. It's been waiting longer than there've been maps."

**If they…**
- **Rush at it:** Brassfall's bell is down the Lightning Shafts; the Tor's is over the empty bed. Either way, stop at the threshold. "You can ring it. You'll have to say who." That's next week.
- **Settle who:** let them start that talk, and leave it unfinished if they can't agree.
- **Look back:** Wisdom (Perception) DC 13. Dust. A dozen grey coats, and they've stopped being careful.
- **Have no Heart:** they stand in front of a bell they can't ring. "It's all right," it seems to say. "I'm used to it." They can still get it. Ask them how.

**Bring it back.** The apple from Scene 1. Is there any left?

**How it ends.** Ask: **"What did the second Heart cost you?"** Go round. If nobody paid anything, ask what it cost somebody else.

### Running the fights (Session 20)

You may not need either. They're here if somebody reaches for a weapon.

#### Kessa's guard at the Heart-Hall

*Two Ironhorn Champions (CR 5), five Ironhorn Riders (CR 3), five Warhorses. Only if the party tries to take the Heart by force.*

- **Setup:** a big tent with four ways out, forty feet across. The Riders are outside, mounted. The Champions are on the threshold, side by side.
- **What happens:** each Champion picks one person, out loud, and fights only that person. Riders go round and block the ways out, and catch whoever runs. Nobody attacks a person who's put their weapon down.
- **What they fight for:** the Heart. They don't chase anyone who hasn't got it.
- **Who to worry about:** whoever's outside when the horses arrive. A charge is the biggest hit here.
- **When it ends:** Kessa walks out and shouts. He'll stop it for a fair wager, or a blade laid down, any time after the first round.
- **Scale on the fly:** drop one Champion for a lighter fight. Keep the second for round two for a harder one.
- **Non-lethal:** all of it. They're guests who've been rude.

#### The Sleepless of the Tor

*Four Sleepless (CR 5; AC 13, 67 HP). Only if Mirel's unrung and somebody's save went badly.*

- **Setup:** night, on the hill, between the stones. They come up through the ground mist, one at a time, wearing the sleeper's fear.
- **What happens:** they go for whoever made them, one at a time. They talk while they do it. Let the player answer back.
- **Who to worry about:** that player. Ask before you start: "All right with this?" If not, use a faceless child.
- **When it ends:** at dawn; if Mirel rings; or the moment they're inside the Cult's circle. Say all three aloud in round one, as a thing Lull shouts from the Hall.
- **Scale on the fly:** three for a lighter fight, five for a harder one.
- **Non-lethal:** they can be walked away from. They stop at the stones, and at the light.

- **Where to break:** after they've agreed the price, before they've paid it.
- **Running long:** cut Scene 1 to its read-aloud, and give Garrow's lamp one line each way.
- **Running short:** run the [steppe contest](play:s17a-contest) at Brassfall, or the watch on the Tor.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop with the Heart in somebody's hands. Corin can come in the morning.

### If you get stuck (Session 20)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **The person they're bargaining with gets tired,** and says the true thing instead.
2. **The Heart hums the note of a bell they've already rung.** Everyone in earshot looks up.
3. **A grey rider turns up alone,** lost, thirsty, and very young, and asks the way.

**If they remember every beat from the other road and want to skip to the end:** let them. One sentence of summary, and straight to the bargain.

**If nobody will pay:** that's an answer. The bell isn't rung, and the finale's harder. Say so, plainly, and let them keep it.

**Names for riders, sleepers and travellers** (cross one off when you use it): Brill, Perrin, Gatt, Corwen, Tansy, Marl, old Anse.

### End of session 20

**The closing question:** "What did the second Heart cost you?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the arrival, the lamp or the Hall, the bargain, Corin.
- **World-state facts to check:** Thrane's or Mirel's Heart, the lamp, the oath to Kessa, Corin, the Notes, the Ironhorn, Bellwright and Court Clocks.
- **The Bargain log:** the oath, in its exact words; or the dream, as a Trade.
- **Who's been seen,** and by whom.

**Rewards:**
- **The second Heart,** or a reason.
- **A friend on this side of the plain:** Yenna, or Lull, or page two hundred and six.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to whoever paid.
