## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. Tonight is short and quiet: a letter, a courier the Court wants, and a message from the Chancellor. It's the session where loose ends get tied, so give every character a goodbye. If it runs short, run it together with Session 9. Its big dungeon is [the Tithe-works](play:s08-tithe-works), under the palace, for the jars from Session 5.*

### Before you start (Session 8)

**Level:** four characters at level 4.

**Check the world state first.** Four facts in Foresight:
- **Lady Isolde:** still in Hallowmere, with orders to follow them if they leave. If they've already turned or captured her, Scene 2 changes.
- **Edda Thorne's diary:** if they have it, the Librarian can tell them more about I.V. (Scene 1).
- **The Tithe Clock:** every night they stay adds 1. At 6, the sweep; Session 9's gate is harder.
- **Who they owe:** Rook, Wren, Bet, Lysse, the Bray family, Aurelie. Each gets a goodbye in Scene 3.

**What this session is about.** A breathless courier brings a one-line letter in blue wax from the north: *I am still here. Come to Glassreach before the winter takes the last of me. I.V.* The Court wants the courier and the letter. Then Maudrin, through Isolde, sends them a polite farewell that is also a warning. They leave knowing who I.V. is, or wanting to.

**Your goals tonight:**
- The letter needs no solving. They can go just because it asks.
- Isolde is a real person, with a son. Give her a chance to be more than the Court's eyes.
- Every character says goodbye to someone in Hallowmere.
- End on: "What does I.V. want from you?"

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Edrin Moss (the courier); a Court agent and three Hushmen; Lady Isolde. For the Tithe-works: Nell Quell, a Hushman Sergeant and three Hushmen, six memory-eels, the First Tallyman (a Ghost) and two Hollowed (Specters).
- **Maps:** the inn steps and an alley beside them, for the snatch. For the Tithe-works, sketch the nine areas from the wiki (The Tithe-works, area by area).
- **Props:**
  - *The letter:* one line, in a shaking hand, sealed in blue wax (a blue crayon blob will do). Hand it over.
  - *Maudrin's message:* the box is in the plan above. Isolde reads it from a grey card.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Tracing the courier.** Investigation DC 15, or Influence DC 15 on Edrin (Indifferent; Friendly if paid or warmed up).
- **Reading Isolde.** Insight DC 15. Influence DC 15 to learn when she leaves.
- **Turning Isolde.** Influence DC 15, but only after a real offer or real leverage. The leverage is her son, or her secret trade.
- **Leaving tonight:** Group Stealth DC 15 at the north gate, with Advantage at night.
- **Every spell hums** within 30 feet outside a silence-stone field. Court agents listen for it.

### Opening recap (Session 8)

### Scene 1: The letter

**Where and why.** The inn steps, morning. Read the letter box from the plan above, and hand over the prop.

**What's really going on.** I.V. is Imber Vesk, the Glass Sexton, once a Vesk of the Court, now frozen in the glass to the north. Edrin carried the letter out for the Stilled. The Court's agents followed him in, and they want both.

**Edrin.** Weather-beaten, out of breath, wants his money and a fire. *Lines:* "Don't ask me to read it. I don't read." · "An old woman paid me. She talks to the glass." · "There were grey coats on the road behind me. Is that bad?"

**If they…**
- **[Trace the courier:](play:s08-trace-courier)** Investigation DC 15, or Influence DC 15 on Edrin. They learn where to start in Glassreach: Wyn Talla's hut, beside Yara's camp. Her family calls the woman in the glass "the sister", and a voice that called itself I.V. promised the sister would move again. On a failure, Edrin leaves at dawn and they can follow his tracks north.
- **[Read his mind](play:s08-read-edrin)** instead: the same facts, and Edrin's goodwill is gone.
- **[Hire him as a guide:](play:s08-hire-edrin-guide)** no check. On the road, Perception DC 14 notices a carriage keeping its distance.
- **[Ask the Librarian](play:s08-ask-the-librarian)** for one true memory: he names Imber Vesk, in the glass since 32 SS, and adds nothing more. Never take the memory from an absent player's character. Or take Lysse's shortcut through the Court receipts: a Study check, Intelligence (Investigation) DC 16, in an hour.
- **Protect Edrin:** later that day, Isolde's man and three Hushmen try to take him off the street (the courier snatch, below). Isolde wants to read the letter too.

**Bring it back.** A grey coat in the alley mouth steps back out of sight, too late. Edrin sees it too.

**How it ends.** They hold the letter, and know the Court wants it.

### Scene 2: Maudrin's last word

**Where and why.** That afternoon, a carriage outside the inn. Isolde, in grey lace, doesn't get out. She opens the door and waits.

Read Maudrin's message from the plan above. Isolde reads it from a grey card, then tears the card in half.

**What's really going on.** Isolde has orders to follow them north. She hates it. Her son is in the Court's keeping, which keeps her loyal. And she's been selling memories to the Ironhorn Compact; if they found the export column, they can work that out.

**Isolde.** Flirtatious and cutting, tired underneath it today. *Lines:* "She sends her regards. I send nothing; I'm only the post." · "You're going north. So am I, it seems." · "Don't threaten me. Offer me something."

**If they…**
- **[Question her:](play:s08-question-isolde)** Insight DC 15: she's going north herself and isn't pleased. Influence DC 15 to learn when: within a day of them.
- **[Turn her:](play:s08-turn-isolde)** only with a real offer (her son's safety) or real leverage (the export trade). Either moves her to Friendly; then Influence DC 15 seals it. She's still sent north (refusing would expose her), but brings only two Hushmen and warns them what the Court wants. Bargain Log: her son's safety (Pellmoor under Brannagh Wick's pilots, or Rook's protection).
- **[Intercept her post:](play:s08-intercept-isolde-post)** Stealth DC 15 to reach her dispatch case; a Forgery Kit DC 16 to write the reply. The case holds her orders: herself, two Hushmen, and a Sergeant at the waystation. Her escort fights to keep it. A forged reply sends her the wrong way for two days.
- **[Ignore the letter:](play:s08-ignore-letter)** let them. It comes again a week later, and two travellers at Kell's Crossing stop speaking. Tell the players plainly that the winter bell is in Glassreach. The Tithe Clock resets after a week out of Court cities.

**Bring it back.** The carriage horse stamps and blows. Isolde glances at the sky. "I have somewhere to be. So do you."

**How it ends.** The carriage leaves. They have a night to say their goodbyes, and at dusk Ilam's key opens a door under the palace: [the Tithe-works](play:s08-tithe-works), if they want the jars before they go.

### Scene 3: Goodbyes

**Where and why.** Their last evening in Hallowmere: Bet's bakery, Rook's hall, the Archive, a cellar.

**Run it as a round.** Go round the table. Each player picks one person in the city their character wants to see before they go, and you play that person for a minute or two. Start with whoever has had the least spotlight tonight. Suggestions:
- **Bet:** bread for the road, and she cries when they leave, and doesn't know why. If they [bring her jar out](play:s08-bring-the-jars-home), she opens it, and knows.
- **Rook** (if she won): "We'll keep the dawn as long as we can. Ring it if you can."
- **Lysse:** a copy of the diary entry, in her own hand, "in case."
- **Wren** (if she's out): Bellwright names in the north who'll take them in.
- **Jonah:** a question. "Will you come back?" If Ilam's jar came out of the Tithe-works, he opens it here and says his son's name.
- **Aurelie:** a blessing for the road. "When the sun comes back."

**If they…** [leave tonight](play:s08-leave-tonight): Group Stealth DC 15 at the north gate, with Advantage. Then Session 9 begins on the road, and Scene 1 of it (Vaer) only runs if they came back for it.

**Bring it back.** A lamplighter comes down the street, putting out lamps one by one, though it isn't morning.

**How it ends.** Ask the closing question.

### Running the fights (Session 8)

#### The courier snatch

*Isolde's man (a Court agent) and three Hushmen.*
- **Setup:** two Hushmen grab Edrin while the third blocks the alley. The agent goes for the letter.
- **Each round:** the agent uses Cunning Action to Disengage and run with the letter.
- **When it ends:** once the party intervenes hard, the Hushmen let go of Edrin and fall back. The agent drops the letter if Bloodied.
- **Scale on the fly:** it's a scuffle, under Low. For a real fight, add a Hushman Sergeant. Three characters: the agent and two Hushmen.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** nobody here wants a death in the street. A downed character is left; the agent wants paper, not prisoners.

| 2:45 to 3:30 | Scene 3: goodbyes, and the jars handed over |

- **Where to break:** after Isolde's carriage leaves, before the goodbyes, or in the Tithe-works' porters' bothy (area 4).
- **Running long:** take the Tithe-works' short way (areas 1, 2, the porters' lift, 8 and 9), cut the goodbyes to three people, and open Session 9 with the rest.
- **Running short:** take the long way through the Tithe-works, then go straight on into Session 9.
- **If you need to stop early:** stop when the carriage leaves, on the closing question.

### If you get stuck (Session 8)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Edrin asks for his money.** Somebody pays, or doesn't.
2. **The Tithe Clock ticks out loud.** A Hushman knocks on the inn door: "Routine." Then ask, "How long are you staying?"
3. **Someone they helped comes to them.** Bet with bread. Ilam with a warning. Lysse with a book.

**Names on the North Road** (cross one off when you use it): the carter Dob Sallis, the toll-keeper Mag Hurst, a pedlar called Finch, the trapper Wyn Talla (by name only, tonight).

### End of session 8

**The closing question:** "What does I.V. want from you?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the courier play, the Isolde play, the Tithe-works and whose jars came out, and whether they left tonight.
- **World-state facts to check:** Lady Isolde, the Glass Sexton (still frozen), the Tithe Clock, the Court Clock, and Calla's Heart if they took it from the back shelves.
- **Bargain log:** any deal with Isolde, and what it protects.

**Rewards:**
- **Level:** none. They stay level 4.
- **The letter**, and a starting point in Glassreach. From the Tithe-works: Gloves of Thievery, 50 gp, two Potions of Healing, and the jars.
- **Gifts from the goodbyes:** Bet's bread for the road, Lysse's copy of the entry, and Wren's names in the north.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to each player whose goodbye made the table go quiet.
