### Places at a glance (Session 1)

- **The old dock** (Scene 1). *Light:* bright, the fog lifting. *Footing:* wet boards; the far end is narrow. *Sound:* the Quays shouting behind; no gulls at all. *Exits:* back along the dock, or into the water. *Cover:* net-racks and upturned boats.
- **Mother Sallow's hut** (Scene 2). *Light:* dim, the stove's glow. *Footing:* a reed floor, one round room. *Sound:* the kettle; reeds rattling outside. *Exits:* one door. *Cover:* the table and the stove.
- **The Quays, the Tally and Strangers' Row** (Scene 3). *Light:* daylight; lamps come on at dusk. *Footing:* piers driven into drowned roofs. *Sound:* a dozen languages. *Exits:* everywhere, but six Hushmen sit in the Landing House. *Cover:* crowds, stalls and warehouses.
- **The Old Shore lane** (Scene 4). *Light:* Quill's lantern, then doorways. *Footing:* a narrow lane between houses, net-racks to either side; the handcart waits 60 feet away. *Sound:* two rounds of noise bring the ferry-folk out of their doors. *Exits:* the lane both ways, and the water. *Cover:* the net-racks and the houses' corners.
- **The Harrow house** (Scene 5). *Light:* one lamp. *Footing:* one room, one door, a shuttered window over the water. *Sound:* oars dipping without a splash. *Exits:* the door, or the window into the Mere (Hushmen hate the water). *Cover:* the table, a bed and the net-racks outside.

### Rumours on the Quays (Session 1, d6)

Roll or pick one when a character asks around, buys a drink or waits in the Landing House queue. Each one is what people say, not the truth.

1. A Calishite pasha paid a thousand gold for one keep: a Corvanese child's first snowfall.
2. The Seam moved a mile north last spring, and two ships that didn't know it are still in the Wall.
3. Master Venn wants something from the old ferry-folk and will pay "anything, friend."
4. Hushmen are checking clocks again. Someone sold one that still chimed.
5. A Lantanese gnome claims that if you strike the right note on the Mere, the drowned bell answers.
6. The *Gull's Wager*'s first mate still lives on Strangers' Row, and drinks to forget what he saw in the Wall.
