A **Toll** is the price of ringing a bell. The Ringer pays it, it is always personal, and it lasts. *Revision 7.5 replaces the older Toll rules: wherever the book, a session plan or a play names a bell's Toll ("a Sense, for one day"), read it as that bell's **kind**; its weight and how long it lasts come from this page.*

### The warning (always before a ringing)

Before anyone strikes a bell, warn the Ringer of two things, the **kind** and the **weight**, and nothing more:

> **SAY:** "Ringing [bell] will take [its kind] from you, and it will be [heavy / grievous / ruinous]. You won't know exactly what until it's paid, and it can never be undone or bought back. Do you ring?"

- **Never say exactly what will be lost.** The not-knowing is the point.
- **Yes or no, nothing in between.** If the player says no, the bell does not ring and nothing is taken.
- **You choose the exact loss, after the bell rings,** from what the table already knows of the character: their session-zero answers (what they lost, who they know), their backstory and what has happened in play. Never invent a new backstory loss, and never touch anything on the Off-limits list. The player then plays the loss and writes one line about it on the Toll Tracker.
- **Who may ring:** any willing adult character who is present. Children never ring (Ila may stand beside the Ringer). An absent player's character never pays. The dead pay nothing.

### The weight rises with every bell

Count the regional bells the party has already rung for the first time before this ringing (Waypoint shows it on any play that rings a bell):

| Bells already rung | Weight |
|---|---|
| 0 or 1 | **Heavy** |
| 2 or 3 | **Grievous** |
| 4 or 5 | **Ruinous** |

Ringing a bell again (to renew Vaer or Calla, or after the Court silences one) costs a Toll at the current weight, but only a bell's first ringing raises the count. Striking Ysh, the Seventh, costs all six kinds at **Ruinous**.

### The kinds

| Kind | Heavy | Grievous | Ruinous |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A Memory** (Vaer: a beginning; Ossa: someone who has died; Hieme: any) | One defining memory, gone for good. The player rewrites the belief, bond or fear it held up. | One person who matters to the Ringer, gone from memory: they know of them, not them. | A whole year of the Ringer's life, gone, along with everyone they met in it. |
| **A Sense** (Thrane) | One sense dulled for a year: Disadvantage on every check that relies on it. | One sense dulled for good. | One sense gone for a year (sight means Blinded, hearing Deafened), then dulled for good. |
| **A Fear** (Mirel) | The bell gives the Ringer a new fear, for a year: in sight of it, a DC 13 Wisdom save at the start of each turn or Frightened until the turn ends. | That fear, for good. | A fear of something the Ringer cannot avoid (the dark, deep water, being alone, sleep), for good. |
| **A Gift** (Calla) | The Ringer's most valued possession (a magic item, if they have one), given to a stranger in need, never returned. | That, and a heartkeep given as a keep to someone who needs it. | A skill or tool proficiency, given to someone in need; the Ringer loses it for good and may learn a different one at their next level. |
| **A Name** (Ysh only) | An ally forgets the Ringer's name and everything they shared. | Someone who loves the Ringer forgets them entirely. | Everyone in one town or city forgets them. |
| **A Year** (Ysh only) | Five years older. | Ten years older. | Twenty years older, and 5 feet less Speed, for good. |

**Sight and hearing:** at Ruinous, take sight or hearing only if the player said at session zero they would play it; otherwise choose taste, smell or touch.

### What a ringing gives back

Every bell's **first ringing** is permanent resonance: it counts toward the Concord and the Second Dawn forever, even if the blessing fades or the Heart is taken away later.

| Bell | Blessing | How long |
|---|---|---|
| **Ossa** | The Mere's dead rest; the Choir stops hunting. | For good |
| **Vaer** | A true dawn over Hallowmere. | Every morning for a year, then ring again |
| **Calla** | The ash fields bear: bread for everyone at Kell's Crossing. | One season, then ring again |
| **Hieme** | The thaw; the Stilled wake over the following weeks. | For good |
| **Thrane** | The storms come home; the Stormwall loosens. | For good |
| **Mirel** | The Sleepless dissolve; the sleepers dream gently. | For good |

### Rules that do not change

- Tolls can never be bought back, and no magic ends one early: not Greater Restoration, Wish, Modify Memory or Remove Curse. The bell holds it.
- If the Ringer drops before the bell is struck, the ringing is cancelled and nothing is taken. After the strike, the Toll is owed.
- Corin can defer a Toll for an hour, never cancel it.
- Record every Toll on the Toll Tracker (Appendix B.1) and in Waypoint's Memory ledger (kind: Toll).
- Losses on the Stairs of the Hush, Mere Memories, eel bites and Tithes are not Tolls; they follow their own rules.
- The Great Toll (Part 10.3) is separate: asked in private, always refusable, paid once in the Concord.
