## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. Act III starts here: a forest of glass, a cold that waits, and people standing in it who have not moved for four hundred years. Tonight is mostly wonder and talk. There is one fight, and it only happens if they leave the path.*

### Before you start (Session 10)

**Level:** four characters at level 5.

**Check the world state first.** Five facts in Foresight change tonight:
- **Isolde:** is she following them, working for them, captured, or never sent? If she's following, she's days behind and stays offstage tonight.
- **Edrin Moss:** did they protect the courier in Session 8? If so, he's at Yara's fire and speaks up for them.
- **The letter:** did they learn that Wyn Talla sent it? If so, they arrive asking for her by name.
- **Who comes from the Sword Coast?** Scene 6 belongs to that character.
- **Child peril:** check your session-zero list. If it's Off-limits, the Stilled child is an old woman who was a child when the winter came. Use that everywhere below.

**What this session is about.** The trees are glass. The people in them are alive. Whoever sent the blue-wax letter is in here somewhere, and the party needs a guide, warm clothes, and some idea of what the glass does when it breaks. They meet Yara, who sells all three, and Wyn, whose family has kept watch over one frozen woman for four hundred years.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Make the forest beautiful first and frightening second.
- Teach one rule clearly: **warmth wakes people gently; breaking the glass hurts them.** The whole act runs on it.
- Let them befriend Yara. She has the map.
- One Stilled person wakes and says one true thing.
- End on: "What is the glass hiding?"

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; Yara, Wyn, Edrin; six Glass-Wolves; two Stilled; the child, the deer, the fox; Corran Vell.
- **Maps:** a forest path with trees on both sides (for the fight). The camp and the clearing need no map.
- **Props:**
  - *A price list* for Yara's camp, on a card: warm cloak 10 gp or a keep; glass-cutting tools 5 gp; a bed 5 gp or a keep; the map, not for sale.
  - *A cold tracker:* write each character's name and tick who has a cloak. Anyone without one saves every hour.

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **Extreme Cold:** at the end of every hour in the glass, each character makes a Constitution saving throw, DC 10. Fail: 1 Exhaustion. A warm cloak, cold-weather gear, or Resistance or Immunity to Cold succeeds automatically.
- **Exhaustion:** each level is −2 to every d20 roll (attacks, checks, saves) and −5 feet of Speed. A long rest removes one level. Six levels kills, so never let it get past three by accident.
- **Influence:** they ask Yara for something, you name the DC (15), they roll Charisma (Persuasion) or whatever fits. If they've been honest with her, they roll with advantage (two d20s, keep the higher).
- **Keeping to a path without a map:** Wisdom (Survival) DC 15. With Yara's map: advantage, and no wolves on the marked path.
- **Waking a Stilled:** an hour of warmth, or 20 Fire or Lightning damage over the scene, or glass-cutting tools (Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 14), all wake someone without harm. Smashing (Strength DC 17), Thunder damage, or more than 20 Fire hurts them.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** a token that lets a player reroll one die. You'll give one tonight for a kindness.
- **New at level 5:** Fireball, Speak with Dead, the Tongues spell, Water Breathing. Someone will want Fireball. Say what fire does here *before* they cast.

**Places at a glance:**
- **The edge:** snow, then grey trees, then glass ones. The change takes about ten steps.
- **Yara's camp:** a ring of lashed tents round a banked fire, a workbench of saws and files, a string of glass petals turning in the smoke.

- **The Stilled Clearing:** an hour in. A deer, a fox and a child, mid-step.
- **The Chapel of Winter:** a long way off through the trees. Tonight they only hear it.

**Rumours (d6), round Yara's fire:**

2. "There's a man in the chapel who preaches to nobody." (True: the Glass Sexton.)
3. "Nobody's climbed the Spire." (True. Yara wants a map of it.)

### Opening recap (Session 10)

> **SAY:** "Last time, you left Hallowmere. *(Say how: by the gate, by the drains, under a sun nobody had ever seen.)* You answered the Ledger, and you carried one promise north. *(Ask its owner to say it.)* You've walked for days, and every morning has been colder. The letter in blue wax said: *I am still here. Come to Glassreach.* This morning the road ran out."

- **Then hand it over:** "What are you wearing?" Let each player answer. Mark who has a warm cloak.

### Scene 1: The edge of the glass

**Where and why.** The end of the road, mid-morning. They see the forest for the first time.

> **SAY:** "The trees appear first, like pale columns in the mist. Then you see they are not trees. They are glass. Every branch is frozen. Every leaf hangs motionless in the air, reflecting the light of a sun that does not move. Somewhere in the forest a bird is singing one note over and over. It has been singing for four hundred years."

**What's really going on.** When Hieme's bell fell silent on the Quiet Night, winter stopped in the middle. Everything in it stopped too. The trees aren't dead and neither are the people.

**If they…**
- **[Walk straight in:](play:s10-extreme-cold)** read the box below, then call for the first save at the end of the hour. If a player is sliding toward a third Exhaustion level, Yara throws a cloak at them and puts it on their tab.

> **SAY:** "The cold here doesn't bite. It waits. After an hour your fingers stop telling you where they are, and your breath falls from your mouth as tiny stars that ring when they hit the ground."

- **Touch a leaf:** it's warm for a heartbeat, the way a hand would be. Then cold.
- **Look for the bird:** it's on a low branch, beak open, perfectly still. The note comes out anyway.
- **Use magic to look:** Detect Magic shows nothing. This isn't a spell. It's a silence.

**Bring it back.** A leaf turns in the air beside someone's face, very slowly, as if it had been waiting for them to look. Ask them: "Do you touch it?"

**How it ends.** A thread of smoke above the trees, and the sound of a saw.

### Scene 2: Yara's camp

**Where and why.** Just inside the edge. It's the only shop, the only fire and the only map for fifty miles.

> **SAY:** "A ring of tents, lashed together against the wind. In the middle, a woman with a shaved head and fingerless gloves is crouched over a flower made of ice, working at its stem with a fine-toothed saw. Inside each glass petal, something tiny is moving. She doesn't look up. 'Mind your feet,' she says. 'That patch is worth more than your boots.'"

**What's really going on.** Yara knows every frozen path. She has sold tools to a dozen people who went to see the Sexton, and none of them came back. She wants enough coin to leave the north, and she wants, more than she'll admit, a map of the Frost Spire.

**Yara.** Dry, quick, always talking about money so nobody asks about anything else. *Lines:* "Coin's nice. Coin doesn't cut ice. Trade me something useful and I'll throw in a route." · "You're underdressed. That's not an insult, it's an invoice." · "I don't do the chapel. I do *near* the chapel." · If they're rude: "Double. And now I'm thinking of triple."

**Her prices:** a warm cloak, 10 gp or one keep (or a good tool); glass-cutting tools, 5 gp; a bed for the night, 5 gp each or a keep. The map isn't for sale.

**If they…**
- **[Befriend her:](play:s10-befriend-yara)** Influence DC 15, with advantage if they tell her the truth about why they're here. Success: she hands over the map (advantage on Survival, no wolves on marked paths) and comes with them as far as the clearing. Read:

> **SAY:** "'I'll tell you where the safe paths are. You tell me why you're going to the Sexton. Nobody goes to the Sexton for a pleasant chat. And if you're going to die, I'd like to know what for, so I can sell the story.'"

- **Fail, or insult her:** she still sells cloaks, at double. The map stays in her pocket until they do her a favour: hold the ladder while she cuts a high flower, or sharpen every saw on the bench.
- **[Ask about the flower she won't sell:](play:s10-glass-flower)** read the box. It can't be bought, and if anyone tries to steal it she closes the camp to them. If someone tells her, freely, a true memory of their own grief, she tells them about Teo and the winter that took him. That player earns Heroic Inspiration. A Bell-Touched character can tell her it won't shatter. It's true, and she doesn't believe it yet.

> **SAY:** "It sits in a felt-lined box: a single glass rose, and inside it something moves like a laugh caught in a jar. 'That's not for sale,' Yara says, and shuts the box before you can see the colour."

- **Ask about the Sexton:** "He preaches. All day, all night. If you argue, the trees crack. So nobody argues."
- **Ask what happens if you break the glass:** "Depends what's in it. Warm it slow and they wake up confused. Crack it and they wake up screaming. Please don't find out which near my tent."
- **[Rest here:](play:s10-rest-at-camp)** a long rest is safe and warm at 5 gp each or a keep. Outside, with no gear and no fire, a long rest gives nothing and costs a DC 10 Constitution save.

**Bring it back.** One of the glass petals on the string spins, catches the fire, and for a second everyone hears a wedding.

**How it ends.** They have cloaks, or they've decided they don't need them. An old woman at the fire has been watching them the whole time.

### Scene 3: Wyn and the sister

**Where and why.** The same fire. If they asked for Wyn by name, run this first.

> **SAY:** "The old woman by the fire has a trapper's hands and a weaver's patience. 'My family has sat with her four hundred years,' she says. 'We call her the sister. The voice in the chapel is the first thing that ever answered us.'"

**What's really going on.** [Wyn Talla](play:s10-meet-wyn) is the last of her family to keep the vigil. The woman in the glass is Maren Talla, frozen on the first night of the winter. The voice that called itself I.V. promised Wyn that Maren would move again, and told her what to write. Wyn paid Edrin to carry it.

**Wyn.** Slow, plain, proud, very frightened of hammers. *Lines:* "You came. I told her you'd come. I tell her everything." · "My mother sat with her, and hers. I haven't got a daughter. So it's me, and then it's nobody." · "Don't use a hammer. Whatever you do. Please." · "He said she'd move again. He didn't say how."

**If they…**
- **Ask who I.V. is:** she doesn't know. "It came out of the glass. A man's voice, like a church. It said two letters."
- **Ask about the song:** every Talla learns it. She hums it: four notes falling, one rising. She has sung it to the glass every evening of her life. Once, when she was young, the glass hummed back.
- **Promise her they'll be gentle:** she gives them her spare cloak. It's the only one she has.
- **Ask to be taken to Maren:** tomorrow, she says. "Past the clearing. You'll want to see the clearing first. You'll want to know it can be done."
- **If nobody goes to her,** she comes to them, sits down, and starts talking.

**Bring it back.** Wyn pours a second cup of tea and sets it down beside her, for nobody. She does it every night.

**How it ends.** She asks them one thing: wake the little one in the clearing, kindly, so she can see it done.

### Scene 4: The path in

**Where and why.** Yara's red wool strips, an hour through the trees. Skip the fight if they stay on the path; it's a reward for befriending her.

> **SAY:** "The path is a line of red wool, knee-high, tied to glass trunks. On either side the forest goes back and back, every tree holding a smaller reflection of the one before. You can see yourselves in all of them, very small, walking the other way."

**What's really going on.** On the marked path the glass is thick and the wolves don't come. Off it, the glass is thin and they hunt by sound.

**If they…**
- **Keep to the path with Yara's map:** no check. Nothing happens except beauty. Ask each player what they see in the glass.
- **Keep to it without the map:** Survival DC 15. On a failure they drift off it.
- **[Leave the path or split up:](play:s10-off-the-path)** warn them first with the box, then run the fight. If they split, cut back and forth between the groups and bring them together at the clearing when the howling starts.

> **SAY:** "A crack like a whip, somewhere to your left. Then a howl that rings, high and pure, the way a wineglass does when you run a wet finger round its rim."

- **Go back the moment they hear it:** let them. The wolves stop at the first strip of red wool and watch.

**Bring it back.** One strip of wool is missing. Further on, it's tied round the neck of a glass fox.

**How it ends.** The trees open out.

### Scene 5: The Stilled Clearing

**Where and why.** An hour in. Their first Stilled.

> **SAY:** "A clearing, very quiet. A deer with one hoof lifted. A fox, turning to look back over its shoulder. And between them a small girl in a wool hat, running, both feet off the ground, with her arms out in front of her. Her mouth is open. She might be laughing. She has been about to land for four hundred years."

**What's really going on.** Every one of them is alive. The girl heard the Sexton sing, on the first night, and heard him stop.

**If they…**
- **[Free her:](play:s10-wake-the-child)** glass-cutting tools, Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 14, harms nothing. Or 20 Fire or Lightning damage in total, a little at a time, melts it safely. Smashing it is Strength DC 17, and the cracks run into the deer and the fox, which shatter and die. When she wakes, read the box. She says it, shivers, and freezes again within a minute. She never attacks.

> **SAY:** "She lands. She looks at her hands, then at you. 'The man in the tower was sad. He sang to us so we wouldn't be afraid. Then he stopped singing, and the winter kept going, and we couldn't move anymore. Is it still winter out there?'"

- **Answer her:** let a player answer. Whatever they say, she nods as if it makes sense. Then the frost climbs her coat again. "Come back," she says, if there's time.
- **Try to keep her awake:** a cloak, a fire and arms around her buy a minute more, no longer. Only Hieme's bell can end the winter for good. Say so if they ask.
- **[Use fire or thunder:](play:s10-fire-on-the-glass)** Yara (or Wyn) grabs the caster's wrist first. Tell the player plainly: fire and lightning melt, thunder shatters, and whoever is inside takes the damage. A Fireball here kills the deer and the fox, wakes every adult near it in pain, and the child wakes and runs. If they cast anyway, the adults run before they fight; give the party a round to choose.

> **SAY:** "Yara grabs your wrist before the last word. 'Not here. Not near them. You don't know what's inside the glass.'"

- **Won't touch her:** Wyn asks them to. The woman she keeps watch over is three trees away, and she needs to know it can be done.

**Bring it back.** After the child freezes again, her arms are in a different place. She's reaching the other way now: towards them.

**How it ends.** They know two things. The man in the tower can be sad. And the winter kept going after he stopped.

### Scene 6: The sailor in the sea-coat

**Where and why.** The far edge of the clearing. Run it if a character comes from the Sword Coast; otherwise it's optional.

> **SAY:** "Among the Stilled at the clearing's edge stands a man in a sea-coat cut four hundred years out of fashion, a brass badge on his breast: a ship under three stars, the old arms of a Tethyrian merchant house."

**What's really going on.** [He's Corran Vell](play:s10-stormborn-sailor), bosun of the carrack *Brightwater*, out of Zazesspur. His captain sailed for the Ringing Land and walked into the first night of the winter. The whole crew is in these trees.

**Corran Vell.** A deep voice, old-fashioned words, a sailor's habit of checking the sky. *Lines:* "What year is it? Is it still ten hundred and eighty-two?" · "We only came ashore for water." · "There were thirty-one of us. I counted. A bosun always counts." · "Who won? There was a war on, at home. There's always a war on."

**If they…**
- **Recognise the badge:** Intelligence (History) DC 10. A sailor, or anyone from Tethyr, knows it outright.
- **Melt him gently:** tools DC 14, or fire a little at a time. He wakes swinging from shock for one breath; Influence DC 15, in Common, calms him. He stays awake an hour at most, then the frost takes him again, standing to attention.
- **Tell him the year:** let the player say it. Then: "That's four hundred and—" He stops counting. "My boy would be... no. No, he wouldn't."
- **Leave him:** that's fine. He'll be one of the Stilled in tomorrow's ambush.

**Bring it back.** He takes off the brass badge and gives it to the character from the Sword Coast. "For the harbourmaster. So they can cross us off the late list."

**How it ends.** Someone from their own world came here four centuries ago. He'd like to go home.

### Scene 7: The sermon at dusk

**Where and why.** Back at camp, or wherever they've stopped.

> **SAY:** "As the light goes, a voice comes through the trees. It's a long way off, and it doesn't hurry. You can't make out the words, only the shape of them: rising, and turning, and settling, the way a man speaks who has had a great deal of time to find the right one. Every glass leaf in the forest hums along."

- **Yara,** without looking up from her saw: "That's him. Goes till dawn. You stop hearing it after a year or so."
- **Wyn** stands up, facing the dark, and hums her family's four notes back at it. For one breath, the voice stops. Then it goes on.

**How it ends.** Ask the question: **"What is the glass hiding?"** Let each player answer in a sentence.

### Running the fights (Session 10)

#### Off the path

*Six Glass-Wolves; two Stilled who wake as the glass cracks.*
- **Setup:** the wolves look like sculptures until they move. They circle first. The two Stilled stand among the trees, thirty feet off: mark them on the map so players can see them.
- **Each round:** the wolves bite and knock people Prone, and get advantage on anyone standing next to one of their pack-mates (Pack Tactics). When a wolf dies it bursts: anyone within 5 feet makes a Dexterity save, DC 12, or takes 1d6. Each burst cracks the nearest winter-moment. One Stilled wakes in round 2, the other in round 3, and they attack whoever is closest.
- **When it ends:** the wolves flee from fire, and from a high, steady note. Yara's whistle will do it, and so will a fiddle. Back on the red wool, they stop. A Stilled can be calmed with an action: Influence DC 15.
- **Cold:** if the fight runs past the hour, call for the save after it.
- **Scale on the fly:** three characters, three wolves and one Stilled. At level 6, add two wolves.
- **Non-lethal outcomes:** nobody is eaten. A character who drops is dragged off ten feet and left when the whistle sounds. The real cost is a Stilled who wakes in pain.

### If you get stuck (Session 10)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **Yara names a price.** For whatever they're stuck on. It's always a favour.
2. **The cold counts.** "That's an hour." Whoever has no cloak rolls.
3. **Something in the glass has moved.** Just a little. Since they last looked.

**Names in the glass** (cross one off when you use it): a trapper called Holl Dray; Yara's supplier, Nan Orrick; a Stilled woman with a basket, Sefa Lorne; the Brightwater's cook, Biddo Marr.
