## Running this session (new DM guide)

*Read this once the day before. It's a lullaby, then a fight to get off the hill. A bell that gives instead of taking, hundreds of people turning over in their sleep, and the first hope anyone's felt in months. Let them have it before the grey riders come.*

### Before you start (Session 19B)

**Level:** four characters at level 7.

**Check the world state first.** Five facts change tonight:
- **Mirel's Heart:** do they have it? If it's still with the courier, the first scene is [the bargain](play:s16b-courier-bargain), again.
- **Mirel:** if she rang already, in 16B, go straight to Scene 3.
- **How many bells have they already rung?** That sets the Toll's weight: none or one, **Heavy**; two or three, **Grievous**; four or five, **Ruinous.**
- **The Cult:** ceased or feeding? It decides who's crying, and where the party can run to.
- **Corin:** an ally? He can put the Toll off an hour.

**What this session is about.** Hope has a price, and it's a strange one: to make the fear stop for everyone else, somebody has to take a new one home.

**Your goals tonight:**
- Give the warning, honestly, before anybody lifts a hand.
- Make the ringing beautiful. Read it quietly.
- Give everyone a good half hour. Then the riders.
- End on the question: **"What did it feel like to hope?"** Then stop.

**Prepare:**
- **Tokens or minis:** the four characters; three Wardens; three Sergeants; six Hushmen.
- **Maps:** the Hall, with its rows; the hillside outside, with the ring of stones and the Cult's little circle on one side.
- **A lullaby,** any one, hummed or played very softly. Stop it when you read the ringing.
- **The Toll Deck,** and the fear cards you made for Session 16B. **Don't use one of those for the Toll. The bell gives a *new* fear.**

**Rules you'll use tonight, in plain words (D&D 2024):**
- **The Fear Toll (the bell gives one):** when the Ringer's in sight of the thing, at the start of each turn they make a Wisdom save, DC 13, or they're Frightened until that turn ends. **Heavy:** for a year. **Grievous:** for good. **Ruinous:** for good, and it's something they can't avoid, like the dark, or sleep.
- **It's a feeling.** It never decides what the character does. Magic can't end it early; Calm Emotions helps for a minute, and Heroes' Feast for a day.
- **Use only fears the player agreed to** at session zero.
- **Frightened:** Disadvantage on checks and attacks while they can see it, and they can't choose to move closer.
- **Warden's Stone:** while it's out, nobody can cast a spell within 10 feet of the Warden. **It's a thing:** AC 12, 10 HP. Or knock it off its chain: a Utilize action and Dexterity (Sleight of Hand), DC 15.
- **Arrest:** Strength save, DC 12, or they're Grappled and can't cast anything with a Verbal part.

**Places at a glance:**

- **The rows:** hundreds of sleepers, climbing away into the grey.
- **The hillside:** stones, long grass, the south road, the Cult's circle to the west.

**Rumours (d6)** among the Cult this morning:
1. "It doesn't take. Mirel *gives*." (True.)
2. "The last one who rang her was afraid of birds till he died." (True.)
3. "Grey coats on the south road. A dozen." (True.)
4. "If she rings, they'll all wake up." (False. They'll dream, and it'll be a good one.)
5. "There was never anyone in that bed." (True. It was made for the Ringer, to lie down after.)
6. "The stair's gone quiet." (True.)

### Opening recap (Session 19B)

> **SAY:** "You've been as far as step *(nineteen / twenty / a hundred)*. Something spoke to each of you in your own voice. It asked you *(read out one or two)*. Now you're back in the Hall, in the grey light, with *(a warm bronze Heart the size of a loaf / a man with a hammer / a child holding a pair of shoes)*. Hundreds of people are asleep all round you. And over the empty bed there's a bell, the colour of old honey, leaning toward you."

- **Then hand it over:** "What's the best night's sleep your character ever had?" One answer each. Don't rush. It's the shape of what's coming.

### Scene 1: The Heart and the bed

**Where and why.** The Hall. Settle who's doing it, and say the price.

> **SAY:** "The bed under the bell is made up, with one corner turned down. It's the only empty one that's got dust on it. Standing on it, you could just reach."

**What's really going on.** Mirel wants her Heart. She's the gentlest of the seven, and the only one whose Toll comes toward you.

**If they…**
- **Haven't got the Heart:** the courier's here. [A dream you've never told anyone,](play:s16b-courier-bargain) or a Gift. It'd very much like to hear it, so it'll take the dream *after*, if they give their word.
- **Robbed the courier:** it isn't here. Its book is, face down.
- **Have Corin as an ally:** he's walking up and down, whispering. "Once. Let her ring *out*. Don't damp it. And lie down, after. That's what it's for."
- **[Ask Corin to put the Toll off:](play:s19b-corin-defers-toll)** he can, for an hour.

> **SAY:** "Corin lays a hand on your shoulder. 'An hour,' he says. 'Ring first. Be afraid after.'"

- **Ask what'll happen to the sleepers:** nobody knows. Lull's got a lamp in each hand.
- **Look for trouble:** Wisdom (Perception) DC 13, from the door: dust on the south road, a long way off. An hour, perhaps. Is there time? Yes.

**Bring it back.** The bell moves an inch toward whoever's holding the Heart. Then an inch toward somebody who isn't.

**How it ends.** Someone steps up on to the bed, with their boots off.

### Scene 2: Ringing Mirel

**Where and why.** The bed. This is the Toll. Straight, and then soft.

**Say the warning first, out loud, to everyone:**

> **SAY:** "Mirel will give you a fear, and it will be *(Heavy / Grievous / Ruinous)*."

**Then explain it, out of character:**
- **It's a new fear.** You choose it, after it rings.
- **Heavy:** a year. **Grievous:** for good. **Ruinous:** for good, and of something they can't avoid.
- What it does: near the thing, a Wisdom save, DC 13, at the start of each turn, or Frightened until that turn ends.
- **It's never anything that player ruled out.** Anyone can ring in anyone's place. Anyone can say no.

**If they…**
- **[Ring her:](play:s19b-ring-mirel)** stop the music. Read it as if there were a baby asleep in the next room:

> **SAY:** "The bell sings like a lullaby sung by someone who loves you. Every sleeper on the Tor sighs and turns over. The Sleepless dissolve into mist. For a moment the air feels like the first morning of spring, and you feel, impossibly, hopeful."

- **Go round, first:** "What does your character hope for, right now? You hadn't for a while." One line each. **Then give the Toll.**
- **Choosing the fear:** pick something small, particular, and a bit odd, that'll turn up now and then: birds indoors; the sound of a latch; being thanked; deep water; a chair facing a wall. Say how it feels arriving: "You're lying on the bed. You look up, and you notice there's a gap under the door, and you can't stop noticing it." Record it: kind Fear, bell Mirel.
- **[Say no, all of them:](play:s19b-refuse)** that's allowed. She's silent, the Sleepless still walk, and she can be rung another time. The Forecast shows what it means.

> **SAY:** "The bell hangs above the central bed, its Heart in your hand, and you lower your arm."

**Bring it back.** Hundreds of people turn over. Say it again, later, whenever it's quiet: "They've all turned over."

**How it ends.** The Ringer lies down, for a minute. It's what the bed's for.

### Scene 3: The first good morning

**Where and why.** All over the hill. This is the reward. Half an hour. Protect it.

> **SAY:** "They haven't woken. But it's a different sort of sleeping. Somebody's smiling. Somebody's snoring, and the old man next to them, who hasn't moved in ninety years, puts a pillow over his head. The light in the glass roof's gone gold. And under the hill, very low, the lullaby's changed key. It sounds sad, and then it sounds grateful."

**Pick two or three, and let the players choose:**
- **Lumen.** [She's on her knees by the bell.](play:s19b-cult-after-mirel) The whole Cult's stopped humming. If they'd not been convinced, they're convinceable now, without a roll.

> **SAY:** "The Leader is on her knees. 'It sounded like someone loved it. We had forgotten.'"

- **Wat and Pip.** Bed two hundred and twelve. She's dreaming, and she's laughing at it. Pip puts her shoes on the floor beside the bed, side by side, for when. [They can have a minute with her now,](play:s16b-wake-a-sleeper) and it's easier: no check.
- **Brother Lull.** He's got his list. Every name, every village. He asks the party to carry copies. "So someone *comes*." Letters home for hundreds of people: it's a good thing to have in Act V.
- **Page two hundred and six.** It shuts its book, and it's crying ink. "That is what we were *for*," says the Librarian.
- **The stones.** The little lights are going up, one at a time, like sparks off a fire.
- **Lie down in the grass.** Anyone who sleeps now dreams, and it's theirs. (Not if the Hush has their dreams. Then somebody sits with them.) It counts as a Short Rest.

**Go round the table:** "What does your character do with half an hour of hope?" Ask the Ringer last. If their fear turns up, play it gently.

**Bring it back.** They've all turned over.

**How it ends.** Pip, from the door: "There's *soldiers*. They're walking funny. They're not making any noise."

### Scene 4: Dry gloves

**Where and why.** The hillside, south. The Court heard.

> **SAY:** "Grey riders come out of the marsh at a walk. Their gloves are dry."

**What's really going on.** [They've come to investigate, and to arrest whoever rang.](play:s19b-hushmen-arrive) Warden Thale's a decent man with an order. His sister's been in bed sixty for nineteen years.

**Warden Thale.** Quiet, tidy, tired; takes his helmet off to talk. *Lines:* "Which of you rang it? No. Don't tell me yet. Once you've told me I've got to do something." · "I'll have the Heart, and I'll have the Ringer. I'd like to do it in that order, with nobody lying down." · If they're kind about the sleepers: "Is there… do you know who's in sixty?"

**If they…**
- **Take him to bed sixty:** he goes, with two men. She's smiling. He stands there for as long as you like. **He still has an order.** But he'll give them until sunset before he remembers it, if they ask. Influence, Persuasion DC 16, or no roll if Lull shows him her socks.
- **Lie about who rang:** Deception against his Insight, at Disadvantage: the Ringer's got honey-coloured light in their hair, and it won't brush out.
- **Give up the Heart:** he'll take it, and he'll look away while the Ringer leaves. Write it down.
- **Give up the Ringer:** it's a real choice. It's a jail, not a grave; you've got a rescue for next session.
- **Hide among the sleepers:** lovely. Six of them search the rows, very quietly, on tiptoe, because it's a room full of people asleep. A group Stealth check, DC 13, under the covers.
- **Run west, into the Cult's circle:** they won't follow. It's the one place the Court has never set foot. If the Cult's an ally, they link hands.
- **Stand:** he puts his helmet on. "I'm sorry about this." Run the fight. **Outside.** He won't fight in the Hall, and neither should they.

**Bring it back.** One of the Hushmen's crying, and hasn't noticed.

**How it ends.** The Court's driven off; or it's got what it came for; or the party's gone.

### Scene 5: Off the hill

**Where and why.** Wherever they are when it's over. Short. Look at the road.

> **SAY:** "From the edge of the stones you can see the whole plain. It's evening. There are lights a long way off, villages, and they've got the look of places where people are sitting up talking. Whatever you did, they felt it there too. Behind you somebody's closing the doors of the Hall, very quietly, with a shoe."

**What's really going on.** The Court now knows exactly who they are. The second bell's across the plain, at Brassfall, and it's coming faster (Sessions 20 and 21).

**If they…**
- **Ask what's next:** east. [A thane with black hands,](play:s20-brassfall-second) and a bell in a hole in the ground.
- **Want to leave somebody here:** Lull'll have them. There's a bed, if they're tired. (There's always a bed.)
- **[Ask the Cult to stand with them:](play:s22-ally-cult)** if they've ceased, yes. Lumen's asleep. Nod says it for her. "I'd like to. I'd forgotten that I could like to."
- **Listen at the stair:** "Thank you," it says, in nobody's voice. Then, "Be quiet now."

**Bring it back.** They've all turned over.

**How it ends.** Ask: **"What did it feel like to hope?"** The Ringer last.

### Running the fights (Session 19B)

#### Court Hushmen on the hill

*Three Wardens (AC 16, 82 HP), three Sergeants (AC 15, 52 HP), six Hushmen (AC 12, 32 HP). Run it outside.*

- **Setup:** the hillside, with the ring of stones for cover. The patrol comes up from the south in three little groups: a Warden, a Sergeant and two Hushmen each. The Cult's circle is 80 feet west.
- **What happens:** one group goes for the Ringer, and one for the Heart. The third stands between the party and the circle. Sergeants call on people to surrender. Wardens open their cases when somebody starts casting: **say the light's going flat first,** and give the caster a turn.
- **A Warden's turn:** two blows (+5, 12 damage, pushed 10 feet), or two crossbow shots (+4, 13).
- **A Sergeant's turn:** two light blows or shots (+4; about 5 each, and 3 Necrotic with the glove), or one and a signal: an ally attacks with a d4 extra.
- **A Hushman's turn:** a glove (+5, 5 and 2 Necrotic; Advantage beside a friend), or an arrest (Strength DC 12 or Grappled; escape DC 12).
- **Who to worry about:** the Ringer. If they're Grappled and carried 60 feet down the hill, they've been taken. Say so out loud each round.
- **The stones are the fight for a caster.** AC 12, 10 HP, or a hand on the chain.
- **When it ends:** the commanding Warden falls; or the party's inside the Cult's circle; or they've got the Ringer or the Heart, and they go.
- **Scale on the fly:** one Sergeant instead of three for a lighter fight. Two Wardens for a lighter one still.
- **Non-lethal:** all of it. They arrest. Anyone who drops wakes up tied, and still has their boots.

### If you get stuck (Session 19B)

**Three quick moves:**
1. **A sleeper says a name,** clearly, and it's one of theirs.
2. **The bell hums by itself,** one bar, and everyone finishes it.
3. **The Ringer's new fear** turns up, small, in the corner of the room.

**If nobody wants to pay the Toll:** don't push. She can be rung later. The Sleepless will still walk until she is, and that's the only thing that's lost.

**If the fear you picked lands badly:** stop, and change it, out loud. "It wasn't that. It was this."

**Names for Hushmen and sleepers** (cross one off when you use it): Hushman Ferrin, Hushman Cray, Corporal Wend, Bess in sixty, Grandfather Moll, the twins Ede and Ewan, Master Wick the dyer.

### End of session 19B

**The closing question:** "What did it feel like to hope?" Then: "Let's each say one thing we'll remember."

**Record in Waypoint:**
- **Plays that happened:** the ringing (or the refusal), the Cult, the riders.
- **World-state facts to check:** Mirel (rung), Mirel's Heart, the Cult, the Court and Hush Clocks. No new Sleepless on the Tor.
- **Toll log:** the fear, and its weight.
- **Lull's list:** who's carrying it, and any letters they promised to deliver.
- **Who was arrested,** if anyone was.

**Rewards:**
- **A bell, rung.** The Concord's one step closer.
- **A safe hill.** Nobody rolls to sleep on the Tor again.
- **Level 8.** Session 20's fights are written for it, so tonight's a good time.
- **Heroic Inspiration:** to the Ringer, and to whoever sat with them.
